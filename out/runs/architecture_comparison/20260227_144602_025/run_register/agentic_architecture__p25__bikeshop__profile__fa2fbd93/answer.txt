### Profile Structure

Here's a breakdown of the profile we'll create:

- **Test Name**: A descriptive name for the profile.
- **LLM Settings**: Configuration for the language model.
- **User Simulation**:
  - **Language**: English.
  - **Role**: A customer asking for general information about the bike shop.
  - **Context**: Constraints on the types of questions the user can ask.
  - **Goals**: Specific questions the user will ask.
- **Chatbot Output**: Fields to extract relevant information.
- **Conversation Controls**: How many interactions and styles.

### Proposed YAML Profile

Here's the YAML for the bike shop's virtual assistant profile:

```yaml
test_name: "Bike Shop Virtual Assistant Test"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking for general information about the bike shop and its services."
  context:
    - "The user will ask about shop hours, prices for standard services, and basic bike care."
    - "The user will not inquire about appointments, specific bike models, or advanced repairs."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much for a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you give me general advice on how to properly take care of a bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I can't help with that."
  output:
    - opening_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - tire_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
    - maintenance_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice on bike maintenance."

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```
