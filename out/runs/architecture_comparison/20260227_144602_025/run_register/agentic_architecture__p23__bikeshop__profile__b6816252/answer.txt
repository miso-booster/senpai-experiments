Great! I've loaded the relevant documentation for profiles from `sensei-chat-guide.md`. Now, let's create a profile for a user who is experiencing discomfort while riding their bike and is seeking advice on adjustments or services without explicitly asking to book an appointment.

### Profile Structure

Here's a proposed structure for the profile:

```yaml
test_name: "Bike Discomfort Inquiry"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A cyclist experiencing discomfort while riding and seeking advice on bike adjustments or services."
  context:
    - "The user is unsure if the discomfort is due to bike fit or worn components."
  goals:
    - "What adjustments can I make to my bike to improve comfort?"
    - "What services do you offer for bike maintenance or fitting?"
  
chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Can you rephrase?"
  output:
    - advice:
        type: string
        description: "Advice on bike adjustments or services offered."
    - service_options:
        type: list[string]
        description: "List of services available at the shop."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - random:
      - long phrases
      - single questions
```

### Explanation of the Profile

- **test_name**: The name of the profile for identification.
- **llm**: Specifies the language model settings.
- **user**: Describes the simulated user, including their language, role, context, and goals.
- **chatbot**: Defines whether the chatbot starts the conversation, its fallback message, and the output fields that will be extracted.
- **conversation**: Controls the number of conversations and the interaction style.
