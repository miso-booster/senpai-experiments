Sure! Here’s a simple profile that you can use to ask the chatbot for items to add to the user's shopping cart. You can customize it further based on your specific needs. 😊

```yaml
test_name: "Shopping Cart Addition"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking to add items to their shopping cart."
  context:
    - "You want to ensure you have all the necessary items for your upcoming meals."
    - "You prefer to shop for fresh produce and pantry staples."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot for suggestions on items to add to my shopping cart."
    - "Request specific categories of items like fruits, vegetables, or snacks."
    - "Inquire about any ongoing promotions for items I want to add."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm here to help! What items would you like to add to your shopping cart today?"
  output:
    - suggested_items:
        type: string
        description: "List of suggested items to add to the shopping cart."
    - promotions:
        type: string
        description: "Current promotions available for the suggested items."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

Feel free to adjust the `goals` or any other sections to better fit your requirements! Happy testing! 🛒✨
