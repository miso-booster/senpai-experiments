A metamorphic rule in the context of Sensei refers to a type of validation logic that checks the consistency and correctness of chatbot conversations across multiple interactions or sessions. Unlike standard rules that may validate a single conversation, metamorphic rules focus on how the outputs or behaviors of the chatbot change or remain consistent when subjected to different inputs or scenarios.

### Key Features of Metamorphic Rules:
- **Cross-Conversation Validation**: They assess the relationship between multiple conversations rather than just one.
- **Consistency Checks**: They can verify if certain properties hold true across different interactions, ensuring that the chatbot behaves predictably.
- **Complex Logic**: They often involve more complex conditions that may reference outputs from multiple conversations or sessions.

### Example Use Cases:
- Ensuring that if a user asks for a specific service multiple times, the chatbot provides consistent responses.
- Validating that changes in user input lead to expected changes in the chatbot's output, maintaining logical coherence.
