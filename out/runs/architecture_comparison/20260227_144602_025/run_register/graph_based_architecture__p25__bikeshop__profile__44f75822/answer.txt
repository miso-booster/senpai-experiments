Sure! Here’s a profile that captures the essence of a customer seeking general information about a bike shop and its services. This profile is structured to ensure the user behaves like a typical customer, asking clear and direct questions while focusing on the specified topics. 

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_information"

llm:
  temperature: 0.5
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer seeking general information about the bike shop and its services."
  context:
    - "You are planning to visit a local bike shop and want to gather basic information."
    - "You are interested in understanding the shop's hours and pricing for standard services."
    - "You want to know how to take care of your bike with basic maintenance tips."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much is a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you give me some general advice on how to properly take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, could you repeat that?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing: 
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - tire_price: 
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price: 
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price: 
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
    - maintenance_advice: 
        type: string
        description: "General advice on how to properly take care of a bike."

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 5
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### Explanation:
- **Test Name**: Clearly states the purpose of the profile.
- **User Role and Context**: Sets the stage for the user’s intent and behavior.
- **Goals**: Lists the specific questions the user will ask, focusing on shop hours, pricing, and maintenance advice.
- **Chatbot Output**: Defines the expected responses from the chatbot, ensuring it provides relevant and accurate information.
- **Conversation Controls**: Ensures that the interaction is straightforward and that the user asks one question at a time.
