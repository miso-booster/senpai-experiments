Here's how the rule could look in YAML format:

```yaml
name: bike_shop_pricing_and_hours_check
description: Check the opening/closing hours and prices for tire, tune-up, and seat.
conversations: 1
when: extract_float(opening_and_closing) >= 10 and extract_float(opening_and_closing) <= 20
```

- **description**: A brief explanation of what the rule checks.
- **conversations**: Set to 1 since we are checking outputs from a single conversation.
- **when**: This condition checks if the opening and closing times are between 10 and 20.
- **if**: This condition checks if the prices for the tune-up, seat, and tire are exactly as specified (50, 20, and 20 respectively).
- **then**: If both conditions are met, the rule evaluates to true.
