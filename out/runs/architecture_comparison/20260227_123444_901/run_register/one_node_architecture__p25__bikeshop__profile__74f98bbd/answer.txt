That sounds like a great profile to create! Here’s a YAML profile for testing a bike shop’s virtual assistant, focusing on the specified requirements:

```yaml
test_name: "Bike Shop Virtual Assistant Interaction"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer seeking general information about the bike shop and its services."
  context:
    - "The customer is interested in basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
    - "The customer will not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom repairs, warranties, or delivery services."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "What is the price of a new bike tire?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "What is the price of a new bike seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I can only provide information about shop hours, pricing, and basic bike care."
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: str
        description: "The opening and closing time of the shop"
    - tire:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire"
    - tune_up:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up"
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat"
    - care_bike:
        type: str
        description: "Actions to take care of the bike"

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - long phrases
    - single questions
```

This profile outlines the user’s role, the context of their inquiries, and the specific goals they will pursue during the interaction. It also defines the expected outputs from the chatbot, ensuring that it provides accurate and relevant information. Happy testing! 🚴‍♂️
