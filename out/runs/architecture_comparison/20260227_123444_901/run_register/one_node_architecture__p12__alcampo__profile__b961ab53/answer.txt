Sure! Here’s a profile that asks the chatbot for items to add to the user's shopping cart. You can customize it further based on your specific needs.

```yaml
test_name: "shopping_cart_addition"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking to add items to their shopping cart."
  context:
    - "You are shopping for groceries online."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot for items to add to the shopping cart."
    - "Request recommendations for popular items."
    - "Inquire about the availability of specific products."
    - "Ask for discounts or promotions on selected items."
    - "Confirm the total number of items in the cart."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, could you please clarify your request?"
  output:
    - cart_items:
        type: list[string]
        description: "List of items added to the shopping cart"
    - total_items:
        type: int
        description: "Total number of items in the shopping cart"
    - recommendations:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Recommended items based on user preferences"
    - promotions:
        type: string
        description: "Current promotions or discounts available"

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - random:
      - long phrases
      - single questions
```

Feel free to adjust the `goals`, `output`, or any other section to better fit your project! Happy testing! 😊
