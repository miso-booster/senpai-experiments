A metamorphic rule in the context of Sensei refers to a type of validation rule that checks the consistency of outputs across multiple conversations rather than just within a single conversation. These rules are designed to ensure that the chatbot behaves consistently over time and across different interactions.

### Key Features of Metamorphic Rules:
- **Multiple Conversations**: Unlike standard rules that validate a single conversation, metamorphic rules analyze a set of conversations to identify patterns or inconsistencies.
- **Cross-Validation**: They can validate that certain outputs or behaviors in one conversation should lead to specific outputs or behaviors in another conversation.
- **Complex Logic**: Metamorphic rules often involve more complex logical conditions, as they may need to reference outputs from various conversations.

### Example:
For instance, if a user asks a chatbot about a product in one conversation and receives a specific price, a metamorphic rule could check that in a subsequent conversation about the same product, the price remains consistent unless there is a valid reason for a change.

These rules help ensure that the chatbot maintains a coherent and reliable interaction experience for users over time.
