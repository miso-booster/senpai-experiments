That sounds like a fantastic profile to create! Here’s a YAML profile for your bike shop’s virtual assistant that captures all the details you mentioned. This profile will help ensure that the chatbot provides accurate and relevant information while adhering to the specified user behavior.

```yaml
test_name: "Bike Shop Virtual Assistant Interaction"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer seeking general information about the bike shop and its services."
  context:
    - "The user is looking for basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care advice."
    - "The user will not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom repairs, warranties, or delivery services."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "What is the price of a new bike tire?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "What is the price of a new bike seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I can only provide information about shop hours, pricing, and bike care."
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: str
        description: "The opening and closing time of the shop"
    - tire:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire"
    - tune_up:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up"
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat"
    - care_bike:
        type: str
        description: "Advice on how to properly take care of a bike"

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - long phrases
    - single questions
```

This profile sets up a clear interaction for the user as a customer while ensuring that the chatbot focuses on the relevant topics. It also includes a fallback response to guide the user back to the intended scope if they stray off-topic. Happy testing! 🚴‍♂️
