In a SENSEI profile, certain fields are optional, meaning they can be included based on the specific needs of your project but are not required for the profile to function. Here are some commonly optional fields:

1. **LLM Settings**: These settings can be included to customize the language model's behavior but are not mandatory.
   
2. **Conversation Controls**: Fields that manage the flow of conversations, such as stopping logic or cost limits, can be optional depending on how you want to structure the interactions.

3. **Goals**: While defining goals can enhance the profile's functionality, they are not strictly required.

4. **Fallback Message**: This is used to provide a default response if the chatbot cannot understand the user's input, but it can be omitted.

5. **Structured Output Fields**: While defining output fields is essential for extracting information, you may choose to have fewer or none, depending on your validation needs.

If you'd like to know more about specific fields or how to structure your profile, feel free to ask! 😊
