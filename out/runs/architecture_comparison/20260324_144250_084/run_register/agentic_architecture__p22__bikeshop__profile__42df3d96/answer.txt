Great! I've loaded the relevant documentation for profiles from `sensei-chat-guide.md`. Now, let's create a profile for the user who is experiencing discomfort while riding their bike and is seeking advice on adjustments or services.

### Profile Structure
Here's a breakdown of the profile we'll create:

- **Test Name**: A descriptive name for the profile.
- **User Role**: Describes the user's situation and concerns.
- **Goals**: What the user aims to achieve in the conversation.
- **Chatbot Output**: Fields that we want to extract after the conversation.
- **Conversation Controls**: How many interactions and the style of interaction.

### Proposed Profile YAML
Here's a draft of the YAML profile:

```yaml
test_name: "Bike Discomfort Inquiry"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a cyclist experiencing discomfort while riding your bike. 
    You are unsure if the discomfort is due to improper bike fit or worn components.
  context:
    - "You are looking for advice on possible adjustments or services offered by the shop."
  goals:
    - "Ask about bike fit adjustments and component checks."
    - "Inquire about services available at the shop."
    
chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, could you clarify that?"
  output:
    - advice:
        type: string
        description: "Advice on adjustments or services for the bike."
    - service_options:
        type: list[string]
        description: "List of services offered by the shop."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - random:
      - long phrases
      - single questions
```

### Summary of the Profile
- **Test Name**: "Bike Discomfort Inquiry" — clearly indicates the focus of the profile.
- **User Role**: Describes the user's discomfort and uncertainty.
- **Goals**: Focused on obtaining advice and service options without explicitly asking for an appointment.
- **Chatbot Output**: Includes fields for advice and service options.
- **Conversation Controls**: Allows for three interactions with a mix of long phrases and single questions.
