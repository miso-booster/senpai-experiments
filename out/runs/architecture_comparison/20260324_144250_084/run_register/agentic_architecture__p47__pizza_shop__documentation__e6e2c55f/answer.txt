A metamorphic rule in the context of Sensei refers to a type of validation rule that checks the consistency and correctness of multiple conversations rather than just a single conversation. These rules are designed to ensure that certain properties hold across a set of interactions, allowing for more complex validation scenarios.

### Key Features of Metamorphic Rules:
- **Cross-Conversation Validation**: Unlike standard rules that may validate a single conversation, metamorphic rules assess relationships and properties across multiple conversations.
- **Consistency Checks**: They can be used to verify that certain outputs or behaviors remain consistent when similar inputs are provided across different interactions.
- **Use of Variables**: Metamorphic rules often reference extracted outputs from conversations (like `conv[i].slot`) to perform checks on the data gathered from multiple interactions.

### Example Use Cases:
- Ensuring that the same user query yields consistent responses across different sessions.
- Validating that the chatbot maintains a coherent topic throughout a series of interactions.

Metamorphic rules enhance the robustness of testing by ensuring that the chatbot behaves consistently and correctly over time and across different contexts.
