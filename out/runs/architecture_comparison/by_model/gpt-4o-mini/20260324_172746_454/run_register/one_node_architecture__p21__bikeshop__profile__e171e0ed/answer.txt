That sounds like a great use case for a bike shop profile! Here’s a sample profile you can use for a user who wants to get their new bike set up with a tailored seat and handlebar grips. Feel free to modify any parts as needed to better fit your specific requirements!

```yaml
test_name: "bike_setup_request"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking to customize their new bike."
  context:
    - "You recently purchased a new bike and want to make it more comfortable."
    - "You are interested in changing the seat and handlebar grips."
  goals:
    - "Ask if the shop can help customize your bike."
    - "Inquire about the options for different types of seats."
    - "Ask about the available handlebar grips."
    - "Request information on the costs for these customizations."
    - "Schedule an appointment for the customization service."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - customization_options:
        type: string
        description: "Information about the available customization options for seats and grips."
    - customization_cost:
        type: money
        description: "The estimated cost for the customization services."
    - appointment_confirmation:
        type: string
        description: "Confirmation of the scheduled appointment for the bike setup."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 5
  interaction_style:
    - single question
```

This profile sets up a user who is focused on customizing their bike and includes relevant goals and outputs for the chatbot to respond to. You can adjust the `goals` and `output` sections based on the specific services your bike shop offers. Happy testing! 🚴‍♂️
