### Profile Structure

Based on the user's context and goals, here's a proposed structure for the profile:

- **Test Name**: A descriptive name for the profile.
- **User Role**: Describes the user's situation and feelings.
- **User Goals**: Specifies what the user wants to achieve (e.g., advice on bike adjustments or services).
- **Chatbot Output**: Defines what information should be extracted after the conversation.
- **Conversation Controls**: Specifies how many conversations to run and the interaction style.

### Proposed YAML Profile

Here's the YAML for the profile:

```yaml
test_name: "Bike Discomfort Consultation"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A cyclist experiencing discomfort while riding, unsure if it's due to bike fit or worn components."
  context:
    - "The user is looking for advice on possible adjustments or services."
  goals:
    - "What adjustments can I make to my bike to improve comfort?"
    - "What services do you offer that could help with bike fit or component wear?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, could you clarify that?"
  output:
    - advice:
        type: string
        description: "Advice on bike adjustments or services offered"
    - service_options:
        type: list[string]
        description: "List of services available at the shop"

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - random:
      - long phrases
      - single questions
```

### Summary of the Profile

- **Test Name**: "Bike Discomfort Consultation" – A clear identifier for the profile.
- **User Role**: Captures the user's discomfort and uncertainty.
- **Goals**: Focuses on seeking advice and service options without explicitly asking for an appointment.
- **Chatbot Output**: Includes fields for advice and service options to be extracted after the conversation.
- **Conversation Controls**: Runs three conversations with a mix of interaction styles.
