### Profile Structure

Here's how the profile will be structured based on your requirements:

- **Test Name**: A descriptive name for the profile.
- **User**: Defines the language, role, context, and goals of the simulated user.
- **Chatbot**: Specifies the chatbot's behavior, fallback message, and output fields.
- **Conversation**: Controls how many conversations to run and the interaction style.

### YAML Profile

Here's the YAML for the bike shop's virtual assistant profile:

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_virtual_assistant"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: "gpt-4"
  model_prov: "openai"
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer seeking general information about the bike shop and its services."
  context:
    - "The user is interested in shop hours, pricing for standard services, and basic bike maintenance."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much for a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you give me general advice on how to properly take care of a bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I can't help with that."
  output:
    - opening_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - tire_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
    - maintenance_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice on bike maintenance."

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### Summary

- **Profile Name**: `bike_shop_virtual_assistant`
- **User Goals**: The user will ask about shop hours, prices for services, and general bike maintenance.
- **Chatbot Output**: The profile defines specific outputs that the chatbot should provide, ensuring it delivers accurate and relevant information.
