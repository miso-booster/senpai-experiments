Sure! Here’s a simple profile that asks the chatbot for items to add to the user's shopping cart. You can customize it further based on your specific needs. Just copy and paste this into a new YAML file under your `profiles/` directory:

```yaml
test_name: "Shopping Cart Item Addition"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A shopper looking to add items to their shopping cart."
  context:
    - "You want to ensure you have all the necessary items for your shopping list."
    - "You're interested in both fresh produce and household supplies."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot for suggestions on items to add to my shopping cart."
    - "Request specific categories of items like fruits, vegetables, and cleaning supplies."
    - "Inquire about any current promotions on the items suggested."

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm here to help you with your shopping! What items would you like to add to your cart today?"
  output:
    - suggested_items:
        type: string
        description: "List of items suggested for the shopping cart."
    - promotions:
        type: string
        description: "Current promotions available for suggested items."

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    steps: 10
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

This profile sets up a user who is looking to add items to their shopping cart and includes goals for asking for suggestions and promotions. Feel free to modify the `goals` or add more details to the `context` as needed! Happy testing! 😊
