That sounds like a great profile to create! Here’s a YAML profile that captures the requirements for testing a bike shop’s virtual assistant. It defines the user’s goals, context, and the expected outputs. Let’s dive right in! 🚴‍♂️

```yaml
test_name: "Bike Shop Virtual Assistant Interaction"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer inquiring about the bike shop's services and pricing."
  context:
    - "The user is looking for general information about the shop and its standard services."
    - "The user will ask about opening and closing times, prices for bike tires, tune-ups, and bike seats."
    - "The user seeks advice on basic bike maintenance and care."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "What is the price of a new bike tire?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "What is the price of a new bike seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, but I can only provide information about shop hours, pricing, and bike care."
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: str
        description: "the opening and closing times of the shop"
    - tire:
        type: money
        description: "the price of a new bike tire"
    - tune_up:
        type: money
        description: "the price of a standard bike tune-up"
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "the price of a new bike seat"
    - care_bike:
        type: str
        description: "actions to take care of the bike"

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - long phrases
    - single questions
```

This profile sets up the user to ask clear and direct questions while ensuring that the chatbot responds with relevant and accurate information. It also includes a fallback message to keep the conversation focused on the specified topics. Happy testing! If you need any adjustments or additional features, just let me know! 😊
