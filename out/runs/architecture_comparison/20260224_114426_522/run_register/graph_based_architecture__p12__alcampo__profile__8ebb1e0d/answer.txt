Sure! Here’s a simple profile that you can use to ask the chatbot for items to add to the user's shopping cart. You can customize it further based on your specific needs. Just copy and paste this into a new YAML file under your `profiles/` directory!

```yaml
test_name: "Shopping Cart Item Inquiry"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking to add items to their shopping cart."
  context:
    - "You are preparing for your weekly grocery shopping and want to ensure you have everything you need."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot for suggestions on items to add to my shopping cart."
    - "Request specific categories of items, such as fruits, vegetables, or snacks."
    - "Inquire about any current promotions or discounts on items I can add."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm here to help! What items are you looking to add to your shopping cart? 😊"
  output:
    - suggested_items:
        type: string
        description: "List of suggested items to add to the shopping cart."
    - promotions:
        type: string
        description: "Current promotions or discounts available for items."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

Feel free to adjust the `goals`, `context`, and `output` sections to better fit your shopping scenario! If you need any more help or modifications, just let me know! Happy testing! 🛒😊
