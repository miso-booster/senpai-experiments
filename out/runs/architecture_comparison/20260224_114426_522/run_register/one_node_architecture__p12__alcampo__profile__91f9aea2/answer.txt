Sure! Here's a simple profile that asks the chatbot for items to add to the user's shopping cart. You can customize it further based on your specific needs.

```yaml
test_name: "shopping_cart_addition"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "You are a customer looking to add items to your shopping cart."
  context:
    - "You want to inquire about available products and add them to your cart."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot for a list of available items."
    - "Request to add {{item}} to the shopping cart."
    - "Inquire about the quantity of {{item}} you can add."
    - "Ask for confirmation that {{item}} has been added to your cart."
    - item:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [milk, bread, eggs, cheese, fruits, vegetables]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, could you please specify the item you want to add?"

output:
  - item_added:
      type: string
      description: "Confirmation of the item added to the cart"
  - cart_total:
      type: currency
      description: "Total cost of items in the shopping cart"
  - available_items:
      type: list[string]
      description: "List of items available for addition to the cart"

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - random:
      - long phrases
      - single questions
```

Feel free to adjust the items in the `data` section or any other part to better fit your shopping cart scenario! Happy testing! 😊
