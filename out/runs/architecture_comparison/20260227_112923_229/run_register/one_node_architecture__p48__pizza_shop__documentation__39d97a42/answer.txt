Great question! 😊 In the context of Sensei, a **metamorphic rule** is a condition that you define to check the correctness of conversations generated by Sensei-chat. These rules help ensure that the chatbot behaves as expected under various scenarios.

Here's a simple breakdown:

1. **Purpose**: Metamorphic rules compare pairs or tuples of conversations to assert how outputs should change (or remain the same) when inputs change. This approach helps address the oracle problem by focusing on relationships between outputs rather than just absolute correctness.

2. **Types of Checks**:
   - They can target a **single conversation** (traditional checks).
   - They can check **several conversations** at once (this is where metamorphic testing shines).
   - They can apply to **all conversations** in a batch (global checks).

3. **Structure**: When writing a metamorphic rule, you typically specify:
   - How many conversations are needed.
   - Any filters to select eligible conversations.
   - The relationship or condition to check between the conversations.

For example, if you have two pizza orders with the same size and drink, a metamorphic rule could check that the order with more toppings has a higher price. 

This way, you can ensure that your chatbot maintains consistent and expected behavior across different interactions! 🌟 If you have more questions about writing or applying these rules, feel free to ask!
