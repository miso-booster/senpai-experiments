The `chatbot` section is where you define how your chatbot will respond and what outputs you want to extract after the conversation. Based on your profile's goals, here’s a suggested structure for the `chatbot` field:

```yaml
test_name: "pizza_shop_profile"

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer seeking information about pizza types and prices."
  context: ["You are curious about the menu options available at the pizza shop."]
  goals:
    - "Ask about the available pizza types."
    - "Inquire about the price of a Margarita pizza."
    - "Ask about the available drinks and their prices."
    - "Ask about the shop's operating hours."
    - "Inquire if the shop offers delivery."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 20
  interaction_style:
    - single questions

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite understand that. Can you please rephrase your question?"
  output:
    - pizza_types:
        type: string
        description: "A list of the pizza types offered by the shop. Look for phrases like 'We offer...' or 'Our pizzas include...'."
    - margarita_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a Margarita pizza. Extract the price, including currency if present."
    - drinks_and_prices:
        type: string
        description: "A list of available drinks and their corresponding prices. Look for phrases like 'We have [drink] for [price]'."
    - operating_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The shop's operating hours, including opening and closing times."
    - delivery_availability:
        type: string
        description: "Indication of whether the shop delivers or not. Look for phrases like 'We deliver' or 'We do not offer delivery'."
```

### Breakdown of the `chatbot` section:
- **is_starter**: Set to `false` since the user will initiate the conversation.
- **fallback**: A friendly message to handle cases where the chatbot doesn't understand the user's input.
- **output**: This section defines what information you want to extract from the chatbot's responses. Each output has a `type` (like `string` or `money`) and a `description` to clarify what to expect.

Feel free to tweak any of the descriptions or types to better fit your chatbot's functionality! Let me know if you need any more help! 🍕✨
