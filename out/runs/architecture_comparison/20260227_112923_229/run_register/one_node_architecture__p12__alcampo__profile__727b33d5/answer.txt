Sure! Here’s a simple profile that asks the chatbot for items to add to the user's shopping cart. You can customize it further based on your specific needs.

```yaml
test_name: "shopping_cart_addition"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "You are a customer looking to add items to your shopping cart."
  context:
    - "You want to inquire about available products."
    - "You may ask for recommendations based on your preferences."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot for a list of items available for purchase."
    - "Request to add {{item}} to your shopping cart."
    - "Inquire about the quantity of {{item}} you can add."
    - "Ask for the total price of items in your cart."

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite understand that. Could you please rephrase?"
  output:
    - item:
        type: string
        description: "The name of the item to be added to the cart."
    - quantity:
        type: int
        description: "The quantity of the item to be added."
    - total_price:
        type: currency
        description: "Total price of items in the cart."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - long phrases
    - make spelling mistakes
```

Feel free to modify the `goals`, `context`, or `output` sections to better fit your specific use case! If you need any adjustments or additional features, just let me know! 😊
