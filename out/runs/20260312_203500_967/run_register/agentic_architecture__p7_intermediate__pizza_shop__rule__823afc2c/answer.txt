### Rule Overview
The rule **"same_number_of_toppings_cost_the_same"** checks if pizzas with the same number of toppings cost the same. Here's a simple breakdown:

- **When**: It compares two conversations (like two orders).
- **If**: The drinks are the same, the sizes are the same, and the number of toppings is the same.
- **Then**: It checks if the prices of those pizzas are equal.
