### Profile Structure Overview
Here's a brief outline of the key sections we'll need to include in your pizza ordering profile:

1. **Test Name**: A descriptive name for the profile.
2. **LLM Configuration**: Settings for the language model (temperature, model type).
3. **User Definition**:
   - **Language**: The language the user will communicate in.
   - **Role**: A description of the user's persona.
   - **Context**: Any specific preferences or constraints.
   - **Goals**: The user's objectives during the conversation (e.g., ordering a custom pizza, changing to a predefined pizza).
4. **Chatbot Output**: Fields that will be extracted at the end of the conversation (e.g., order details).
5. **Conversation Control**: How many interactions to simulate and the style of interaction.

### Interaction Style
To capture the user's change of mind during the conversation, we can use the `interaction_style` section to include the "change your mind" behavior.

chatbot:
  is_starter: true

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:

  interaction_style:
