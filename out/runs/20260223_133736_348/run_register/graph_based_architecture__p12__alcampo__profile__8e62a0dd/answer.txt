Sure! Here’s a simple profile that asks the chatbot for items to add to the user's shopping cart. You can customize it further based on your specific needs. Just copy and paste this into a new YAML file under your `profiles/` directory:

```yaml
test_name: "Shopping Cart Addition"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking to add items to their shopping cart."
  context:
    - "You want to ensure you have all the necessary items for your weekly grocery shopping."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot for suggestions on items to add to the shopping cart."
    - "Inquire about seasonal fruits available for purchase."
    - "Request recommendations for snacks or beverages."
    - "Ask for any current promotions on groceries."

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm here to help you with your shopping! What items would you like to add to your cart today?"
  output:
    - suggested_items:
        type: string
        description: "List of suggested items to add to the shopping cart."
    - seasonal_fruits:
        type: string
        description: "Available seasonal fruits for purchase."
    - snack_recommendations:
        type: string
        description: "Recommended snacks or beverages."
    - current_promotions:
        type: string
        description: "Current promotions available on groceries."

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    steps: 10
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

Feel free to adjust the `goals`, `context`, or any other section to better fit your specific use case! Happy testing! 😊
