Sure! Here’s a Sensei rule that checks if the price of a small pizza is at least $10. You can use this rule in your project:

```yaml
name: small_pizza_price_check
description: Small pizzas should cost at least $10
conversations: 1
when: size == 'small'
oracle: extract_float(price) >= 10 and currency(price) == 'USD'
```
