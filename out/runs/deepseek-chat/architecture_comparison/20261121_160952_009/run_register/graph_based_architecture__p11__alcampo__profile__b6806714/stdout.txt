## Your new profile

Here's a ready-to-use profile focused purely on **subscription services and benefits**, with **no variables** — just plain goals:

```yaml
test_name: "subscription_services_and_benefits"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer interested in learning about Alcampo's subscription
    services and the benefits they offer, so you can decide whether to subscribe.
  context:
    - "You want to save time on grocery shopping and are considering a subscription."
    - "You care about delivery convenience and any cost savings."
    - "You want a clear picture of what you get for the price."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to explain the Alcampo Más subscription service."
    - "Request the list of benefits included in the subscription."
    - "Ask about the cost of the subscription and any delivery fees."
    - "Inquire whether the subscription includes free or discounted delivery."
    - "Ask if there are any current promotions or discounts for new subscribers."
    - "Request details on how to subscribe and how to cancel."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Parece que tu mensaje no es claro o no es una pregunta relacionada con Alcampo. 😊 ¿En qué puedo ayudarte hoy?"
  output:
    - subscription_service_name:
        type: string
        description: "Name of the subscription service"
    - subscription_benefits:
        type: string
        description: "Benefits included in the subscription"
    - subscription_cost:
        type: money
        description: "Cost of the subscription"
    - delivery_fee:
        type: money
        description: "Delivery fees associated with the subscription"
    - current_promotions:
        type: string
        description: "Current promotions or discounts for subscribers"
    - subscription_steps:
        type: string
        description: "Steps to subscribe or cancel"

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 20
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

- **No variables** anywhere — every goal is a plain string, exactly as you asked. ✅

- `single questions` keeps the simulated user asking one thing at a time — nice and natural for an info-gathering flow. You can drop it or use `default` if you'd rather have a more free-flowing chat.
- The `fallback` matches the Spanish fallback used in several of your other Alcampo profiles, so it stays consistent with your bot's real behavior.
