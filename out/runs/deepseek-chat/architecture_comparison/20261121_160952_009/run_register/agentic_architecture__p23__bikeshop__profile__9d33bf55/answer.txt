I found several issues in your profile. Here's what's wrong and how I fixed it 👇

## 🐛 Issues found

1. **`llm.temperature: low`** — The docs say `temperature` must be a **float** (e.g. `0.7`). `low` is not a valid value.
2. **`chatbot.output` uses `type: money`** — `money` is **not** a documented type. Valid types are primitives (`int`, `float`, `string`), `time(...)`, `date(...)`, `list[...]`, or a **custom type** defined in `types/`. For prices you should use a custom `currency` type (like the doc's example) or `float`.
3. **`seat_price` and `care_bike` have no `type`** — Every output field **must** declare a `type`. Missing types will break extraction.
4. **`conversation.interaction_style: - default`** — `default` is a valid keyword, so this is fine ✅ (just noting it's OK).
5. **No `goal_style`** — Not strictly required, but without it the conversation has no defined stopping logic. I'll add a sensible `goal_style` so the run terminates cleanly (recommended in the docs).

## ✅ Fixed profile

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: currency
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: currency
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - default
```
