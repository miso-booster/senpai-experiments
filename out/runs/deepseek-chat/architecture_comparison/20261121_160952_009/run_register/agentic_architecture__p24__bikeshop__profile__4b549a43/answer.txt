### What this does and why 🚲

- **`user.role` + `context`** encode the customer persona and the hard boundaries you described (no appointments, models, advanced repairs, warranties, delivery, or off-topic questions). The context lines explicitly tell the simulator to stay within basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care.
- **`user.goals`** are the five requests you listed: opening/closing times, tire price, tune-up price, seat price, and general bike-care advice. They're plain strings (no variables needed here), so the simulator asks them naturally.
- **`chatbot.output`** defines the extraction schema — this is the contract that future SENSEI-check rules will read as `conv[i].<field>`. I used `currency` for the three prices (so numeric/price rules work cleanly) and `string` for hours and advice. Field names are stable and descriptive, per the best-practices section of `sensei-chat-guide.md`.
- **`conversation`** mirrors the existing bike-shop profiles: `all_answered` goal style (so the run continues until every goal is covered, with a safety `limit`), `single questions` interaction style (one clear, direct question per turn — matching your "phrase questions clearly and directly" requirement), and a `max_cost` cap for safety.
