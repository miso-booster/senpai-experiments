Hey there! 😊 Great question — the `chatbot` field is basically where you tell Sensei *what to extract* from the conversation at the end, and it's also the contract your rules will read later (as `conv[i].<field_name>`). Let me help you fill it in! 🙌

Looking at your goals, you're asking about **pizza types, Margarita price, drinks & prices, operating hours, and delivery** — so your `chatbot.output` should mirror exactly those things. Here's a completed version:

```yaml
test_name: pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker

llm:
  temperature: 0.3
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer seeking information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours before potentially placing an order."
  context:
    - "personality: personalities/skeptical-user"
    - "You're planning a casual pizza night at home with friends this weekend and are considering ordering from a local pizzeria."
    - "You want to gather all the necessary information before deciding what to order and whether to pick it up or have it delivered."
    - "You have a variety of preferences among your friends, so you need to find options that cater to different tastes."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the available pizza types."
    - "Ask about the price of a Margarita pizza."
    - "Ask about the available drinks and their prices."
    - "Ask about the shop's operating hours."
    - "Ask if they deliver."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I did not get what you said. I can help you ordering predefined or custom pizzas, and then drinks."
  output:
    - pizza_types:
        type: string
        description: "A list of the pizza types offered by the shop. Look for phrases like 'We offer [pizza types]' or a list of available pizzas."
    - margarita_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a Margarita pizza. Extract the price, including currency if present. If multiple sizes are mentioned, extract the price for all sizes mentioned."
    - drinks_and_prices:
        type: string
        description: "A list of available drinks and their corresponding prices. Extract the name and price of each drink."
    - operating_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The shop's operating hours, including opening and closing times. Look for phrases like 'Our hours are [hours]' or 'We are open from [opening time] to [closing time]'."
    - delivery_availability:
        type: string
        description: "Indication of whether the shop delivers or not. Look for phrases like 'We deliver' or 'We do not offer delivery'."

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 20
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### A few things worth knowing 👀

- **`is_starter: false`** → means the *user* speaks first (your simulated customer kicks things off). Set it to `true` only if the bot should greet first.
- **`fallback`** → paste your bot's *real* fallback message here. This helps Sensei avoid endless loops when the bot doesn't understand. I used the one from your other profiles, but double-check it matches your actual bot!
- **`output` fields** → each one maps 1:1 to a goal you're testing. That's the golden rule: *if you ask about it, extract it.* This way your rules can later check things like `conv[0].margarita_price` or `conv[0].delivery_availability`. 🎯

### Quick tip on types 💡
- `money` and `currency` are custom types — make sure you have a matching YAML in your `types/` folder (e.g. `types/money.yml`). If you don't, swap it to `string` or `float` to be safe.
- For `operating_hours`, `string` is fine since hours can be messy ("Mon–Fri 11:00–23:00"). If you want stricter parsing later, you could use `time("hh:mm")` — but only if the bot always returns a clean time.
