## 🧠 Quick design thinking

Before writing, here's what I kept in mind (based on the Sensei Profiles guide):

- **`user.goals`** → the actual requests ("add X to my cart", "add Y", etc.)
- **Variables** → I used `forward()` on a couple of them so you get a nice combination matrix, and `random()` for simpler picks.
- **`chatbot.output`** → fields the extractor will pull at the end (cart contents, quantities, totals…). These are also what your **Sensei-check rules** can later read as `conv[i].<field>`.
- **`conversation.number`** → since I used **two `forward()` variables**, I can safely use `combinations(...)`. 🎯
- **`interaction_style`** → kept it natural with a touch of `change your mind` (super realistic for cart scenarios!).

## 📄 Ready-to-use profile

Save this as `profiles/shopping_cart_management.yaml`:

```yaml
test_name: "shopping_cart_management"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer shopping online at Alcampo. You want to build up your
    shopping cart by asking the chatbot to add specific products, adjust
    quantities, and confirm what is currently in your cart.
  context:
    - "You are doing your weekly grocery shopping and prefer to add items one by one."
    - "You care about getting the right quantity and the correct product variant."
    - "You like to double-check your cart total before finishing."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to add {{quantity}} of {{product}} to your shopping cart."
    - "Ask the chatbot to add {{second_product}} to your cart as well."
    - "Request a summary of everything currently in your cart."
    - "Ask for the current total price of your cart in {{currency}}."
    - product:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [milk, eggs, bread, olive oil, rice]
    - quantity:
        function: forward()
        type: int
        data:
          min: 1
          max: 3
          step: 1
    - second_product:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [apples, cheese, coffee, pasta, yogurt, tomatoes]
    - currency:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [EUR, USD]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Parece que tu mensaje no es claro o no es una pregunta relacionada con Alcampo. 😊 ¿En qué puedo ayudarte hoy?"
  output:
    - added_items:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Products the user asked to add to the cart"
    - cart_summary:
        type: string
        description: "Summary of all items currently in the cart"
    - cart_total:
        type: currency
        description: "Total price of the cart"
    - item_quantities:
        type: string
        description: "Quantities associated with each cart item"

conversation:
  number: combinations(0.6, forward)
  goal_style:
    steps: 8
    max_cost: 0.1
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
    - random:
        - make spelling mistakes
        - long phrases
```

---

## 🔍 Why this works nicely

| Piece | Why it's there |
|---|---|
| **Two `forward()` variables** (`product`, `quantity`) | Enables `combinations(0.6, forward)` → you get a solid grid of product × quantity combos without exploding the run. ✅ |
| **`second_product` with `random()`** | Adds variety without bloating the matrix. |
| **`change your mind`** | Very realistic — users often tweak quantities or swap items mid-cart. 😄 |
| **`chatbot.output` fields** | Ready to be referenced in rules like `conv[0].cart_total` or `conv[0].added_items`. |

---

## 💡 A couple of tips

- If you want **full coverage** of every product × quantity combo, swap `combinations(0.6, forward)` for `combinations` (no fraction).
- If you later write **Sensei-check rules** about the cart (e.g., "total must be positive", "added items must appear in summary"), the `cart_total` and `added_items` fields are already there for you. 🎯
- Want a **custom type** for currency? You likely already have `types/currency.yml` in your project — just reference `currency` as I did. If not, let me know and I'll show you the file. 😊
