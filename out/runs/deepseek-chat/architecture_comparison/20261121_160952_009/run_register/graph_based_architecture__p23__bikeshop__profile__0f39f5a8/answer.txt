Hey there! 😊 Happy to help you get this profile working — I can see a few things that would trip Sensei up. Let me walk you through them one by one, then give you the fixed version. 🚴

## What's broken 🔍

**1. `llm.temperature: low`** — Temperature must be a **float** (e.g. `0.0`, `0.5`), not a word. `low` isn't a valid value.

**2. `chatbot.output` types are wrong** ⚠️
- `type: str` is **not** a valid type. The valid primitives are `int`, `float`, `string` (plus `time(...)`, `date(...)`, `list[...]`, and custom types).
- `type: money` — unless you have a custom type called `money` defined in your `types/` folder, this will fail. The default types don't include `money`. If you don't have that custom type, use `float` (or `string` if you just want the raw text).
- `seat_price` and `care_bike` are **missing the `type` field entirely** — every output field needs one.

**3. `conversation` is missing `goal_style`** — Without it, Sensei doesn't know how/when to end the conversation. Since you have 5 goals and want them all covered, `all_answered` is the natural fit.

**4. `conversation.number: 5`** — That's fine as a fixed integer, but note your goals don't use any variables, so every conversation will be identical. That's OK for a smoke test, just something to be aware of. 👍

## The fixed profile ✅

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.0
  model: gpt-4o-mini

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: float
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: float
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: float
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 9
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

## A couple of notes 💡

- **If you *do* have a custom `money` type** in your `types/` folder, you can keep `type: money` for the price fields — just make sure the file exists and the `name` matches. Otherwise, `float` is the safe choice.
- I set `goal_style.all_answered` with `export: true` so you get a CSV showing which outputs were satisfied — handy for a QA profile like this one. The `limit: 9` is a safety cap (default is 30 if omitted).
- I kept `is_starter: false` — but your original had `True`. If your bot *doesn't* greet first, flip it back to `true` so the simulated user opens the conversation. 🙂
