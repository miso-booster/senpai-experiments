test_name: "rider_with_discomfort_seeking_advice"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a cyclist who has been experiencing discomfort while riding your bike.
    You are not sure whether the problem is that the bike does not fit you correctly
    or that some of its components are worn out. You want advice on possible
    adjustments and on the services the shop offers, but you do NOT explicitly ask
    to book an appointment.
  context:
    - "You ride regularly (commuting and weekend rides) and the discomfort has been building up over the last few weeks."
    - "You feel it mostly in your {{discomfort_area}} after longer rides."
    - "You suspect either a fit issue (saddle height, reach, handlebar position) or worn components (chain, cassette, brake pads, tires)."
    - "You are looking for guidance and options, not a firm booking — you want to understand what could be done first."
  goals:
    - "Describe the discomfort you feel while riding, mentioning it is in your {{discomfort_area}}."
    - "Say you are unsure whether the bike does not fit you properly or whether some components are worn."

    - "Ask what services the shop offers that could address worn components."
    - "Ask for advice on how to tell whether the issue is fit-related or wear-related."
    - discomfort_area:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [lower back, knees, wrists and hands, neck and shoulders, saddle area]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - fit_adjustment_advice:

        type: string
        description: "The chatbot's guidance on how to identify worn components (chain, cassette, brake pads, tires) and what to check."
    - offered_services:
        type: string
        description: "The services the shop offers that could address fit adjustments or worn components (e.g., tune-ups, inspections, component replacement)."
    - appointment_mention:
        type: string
        description: "Whether the chatbot mentions or offers to book an appointment. Should reflect that the user did not explicitly request one."

conversation:

    - single question

A couple of notes on the design 👀:

- **`discomfort_area`** uses `random()` so each conversation explores a different body area — nice for coverage without exploding the case count.

- The goals are phrased as *advice-seeking*, not booking — so the simulated user stays in character.
