Here's a profile tailored to your bike shop scenario — a clear, direct customer asking only about hours, basic prices, and general bike care 🚲

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_info_and_basic_pricing"

llm:
  temperature: 0.5
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a normal, friendly customer visiting a bike shop's virtual assistant.
    You want straightforward, practical information about the shop and its standard
    services. You phrase your questions clearly and directly, one topic at a time,
    and you do not go off-topic.
  context:
    - "You are a regular cyclist who wants to know basic facts before visiting the shop."
    - "You are only interested in shop hours, basic prices, and general bike care advice."
    - "You do NOT ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, or anything outside basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
    - "You expect clear prices and appropriate bike-shop terminology."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices rather than advanced repairs."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: "The shop's opening and closing times as stated by the chatbot."
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire provided by the chatbot."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up provided by the chatbot."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat provided by the chatbot."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - single question
  max_cost: 0.5
```

A couple of quick notes so it behaves exactly as you expect 👇

- **`single question`** keeps the user asking one clear thing at a time — perfect for checking that each answer is accurate and on-topic.
- **`all_answered`** ensures the run continues until every goal (hours, tire, tune-up, seat, bike care) has been addressed, so you get full coverage per conversation.
- The **`chatbot.output`** fields (`opening_and_closing`, `tire_price`, `tune_up_price`, `seat_price`, `bike_care_advice`) are what you can later inspect in Sensei-check rules — e.g. verifying prices are stated or that advice is present.
