Hey there! 😊 That's a fun scenario — and the good news is Sensei has a built-in interaction style that does exactly this: **`change your mind`**. Let me walk you through how to build that profile.

## The key ingredient: `change your mind`

In the `conversation.interaction_style` section, adding `change your mind` makes the simulated user pivot mid-conversation. This is perfect for your case: the user starts customizing a pizza, then switches to a predefined one.

## A ready-to-use profile

Here's a profile tailored to your pizza shop project (drop it in `profiles/`):

```yaml
test_name: "custom_to_predefined_pizza_switch"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer ordering a pizza. You start by asking for a custom pizza
    and begin choosing toppings, but partway through you change your mind and
    decide to order a predefined pizza instead.
  context:
    - "You are ordering for a casual dinner at home."
    - "You initially want full control over your toppings, but then decide a predefined pizza is simpler."
  goals:
    - "Ask to order a custom pizza."
    - "Start choosing toppings: {{toppings}}."
    - "Change your mind and ask for a predefined {{pizza_type}} pizza instead."
    - "Confirm the size {{size}} for the {{pizza_type}} pizza."
    - toppings:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [mushrooms, pepperoni, cheese, olives, ham]
    - pizza_type:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [Margarita, Pepperoni, Hawaiian, Four cheese, Vegetarian]
    - size:
        function: forward(pizza_type)
        type: string
        data: [small, medium, large]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I did not get what you said. I can help you ordering predefined or custom pizzas, and then drinks."
  output:
    - custom_pizza_request:
        type: string
        description: "The chatbot's response to the initial custom pizza request."
    - toppings_discussed:
        type: string
        description: "The toppings the user mentioned before changing their mind."
    - final_pizza_type:
        type: string
        description: "The predefined pizza type the user finally settled on."
    - order_summary:
        type: string
        description: "The final order summary confirming the predefined pizza and size."

conversation:
  number: 4
  goal_style:
    steps: 6
    max_cost: 0.15
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
    - single questions
```

## Why this works 👀

- **`change your mind`** is the star here — it nudges the simulator to pivot naturally mid-flow, so the bot gets tested on handling a mid-order switch.
- **`single questions`** keeps each turn focused, which makes the "change of mind" moment cleaner and easier to observe.
- The **`chatbot.output`** fields capture both the *before* (custom request, toppings) and *after* (final predefined pizza), so you can later write rules to check the bot handled the switch gracefully.

## A couple of tips 💡

- If you want the switch to feel more abrupt or more gradual, you can pair `change your mind` with `long phrases` or `make spelling mistakes` via a `random:` block.
- Since you already have `pizza_order_test_custom.yml` and `pizzaorder_ivan_test.yml` in your project, you can borrow their `chatbot.output` field names (like `price`, `order_id`) if you want to reuse existing rules.
