Hey there! 👋 Happy to help you get this profile working. I spotted a few things that would trip up Sensei — let me walk you through them and then give you a clean version 😊

**What's off:**

1. **`llm.temperature: low`** → must be a **float** (e.g., `0.0`), not a word.
2. **`chatbot.output` types** → `str` isn't a valid type; use `string`. Also `money` isn't a default type — use `currency` (or a custom type). And `seat_price` / `care_bike` are missing a `type` entirely.
3. **`conversation.goal_style`** → you have `interaction_style` but no `goal_style`. Since your goals are a fixed list, `all_answered` (or `steps`) is what you want.
4. **`conversation.number: 5`** → fine as a fixed int, but note your goals have no variables, so all 5 runs will be identical. That's okay for a smoke test, but worth knowing.

Here's the fixed profile 👇

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.0
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: currency
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: currency
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 9
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

**Quick notes on the choices I made:**
- `all_answered` with `limit: 9` gives the simulator room to hit all 5 goals (your original project file used `limit: 9` too 👍).
- I switched `money` → `currency` since that's a built-in type. If you have a custom `money` type in `types/`, feel free to keep it — just make sure the file exists!
- `care_bike` and `seat_price` now have explicit `string` / `currency` types so extraction doesn't fail.
