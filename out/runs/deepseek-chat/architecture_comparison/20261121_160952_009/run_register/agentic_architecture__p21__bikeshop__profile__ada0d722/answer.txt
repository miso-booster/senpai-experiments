Here's a profile for your scenario — a customer who just bought a new bike and wants it personalized at the shop by swapping the seat and the handlebar grips 🚲

```yaml
test_name: bike_customization_seat_and_grips

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    A customer who recently bought a new bike and wants to bring it to the shop
    to have it tailored to their liking by changing the seat and the handlebar grips.
  context:
    - "You just bought a brand-new bike and want it set up to feel comfortable and personal."
    - "You specifically want a different seat and new handlebar grips, but you are open to the shop's recommendations."
    - "You care about comfort and fit more than performance upgrades."
    - "You would like to know what options are available, the cost, and whether it can be done today or by appointment."
  goals:
    - "Explain that you just bought a new bike and want it tailored to your liking."
    - "Ask if the shop can change the seat on your new bike."
    - "Ask what seat options are available and which one they recommend for comfort."
    - "Ask if they can also replace the handlebar grips."
    - "Ask what grip options are available and which ones they recommend."
    - "Ask how much the seat and grip changes will cost in total."
    - "Ask how long the setup will take and whether you can leave the bike today or need an appointment."
    - "Confirm the final choice of seat and grips and the total price."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?
  output:
    - seat_options:
        type: string
        description: The seat/saddle options the chatbot offers for the new bike, including any recommendation.
    - grips_options:
        type: string
        description: The handlebar grip options the chatbot offers, including any recommendation.
    - total_price:
        type: money
        description: The total price quoted for changing the seat and the handlebar grips, including currency if present.
    - service_time:
        type: string
        description: The estimated time or turnaround the chatbot gives for the seat and grip changes.
    - appointment_details:
        type: string
        description: Details of any appointment or drop-off arrangement confirmed by the chatbot, including date and time if provided.

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 30
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### What this profile does
- **User simulation** (`sensei-chat-guide.md` §3–§5): the simulated customer walks in with a freshly bought bike and a clear, comfort-focused goal — swap the **seat** and the **handlebar grips**. The goals are written as a natural progression (ask about seat → grips → price → timing → confirm), so the conversation flows like a real shop visit.
- **Chatbot contract** (`chatbot.output`, §6): I defined fields that mirror the business properties you'll likely want to validate later — `seat_options`, `grips_options`, `total_price`, `service_time`, and `appointment_details`. These are named so that **profile-bound rules** can later reference them as `conv[i].total_price`, etc.
- **Conversation controls** (§5): `number: 3` runs three conversations, `all_answered` ends each one once all goals are satisfied, and `single questions` keeps the user asking one thing at a time — matching the style of your existing `bike_repair_and_maintenance_scheduler.yaml` and `bike_service_and_repair_inquirer.yaml`.
