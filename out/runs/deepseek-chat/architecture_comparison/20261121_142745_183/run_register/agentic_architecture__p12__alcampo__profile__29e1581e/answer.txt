Here's a profile that simulates a user asking the chatbot to add items to their shopping cart. I based it on `sensei-chat-guide.md` and matched the conventions of your existing Alcampo profiles (Spanish fallback, `money` type, `single questions` style).

```yaml
test_name: "Shopping Cart Item Addition"

llm:
  temperature: 0.9
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer shopping online who wants to add several products to your
    shopping cart. You ask the chatbot to add specific items, specify quantities,
    and confirm what is currently in your cart.
  context:
    - "You are doing your weekly grocery shopping and want to fill your cart before checkout."
    - "You care about getting the right quantity of each product."
    - "You like to confirm the cart contents and total before finishing."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to add {{quantity}} {{unit}} of {{product}} to your shopping cart."
    - "Ask the chatbot to add another item: {{second_product}}."
    - "Ask the chatbot to show you what is currently in your cart."
    - "Ask for the current cart total."
    - "Ask the chatbot to remove one item from the cart if you change your mind."
    - product:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - milk
          - eggs
          - bread
          - apples
          - rice
          - olive oil
    - quantity:
        function: random()
        type: int
        data:
          min: 1
          max: 6
          step: 1
    - unit:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [units, packs, bottles, kilograms]
    - second_product:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - cheese
          - tomatoes
          - pasta
          - coffee
          - yogurt

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Parece que tu mensaje no es claro o no es una pregunta relacionada con Alcampo. 😊 ¿En qué puedo ayudarte hoy? Estoy aquí para resolver tus dudas sobre nuestros productos y servicios."
  output:
    - added_items:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Items the chatbot confirmed were added to the cart"
    - item_quantities:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Quantities associated with each added item"
    - cart_contents:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Full list of items currently in the shopping cart"
    - cart_total:
        type: money
        description: "Current total price of the shopping cart"
    - removed_item:
        type: string
        description: "Item the chatbot removed from the cart, if any"
    - confirmation_message:
        type: string
        description: "Chatbot's confirmation that the item was added"

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 20
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### What this profile does & how it maps to the docs

- **`user.goals` + variables** (`sensei-chat-guide.md` §4): The goals ask the bot to add items, show the cart, give a total, and remove an item. Variables like `{{product}}`, `{{quantity}}`, `{{unit}}`, and `{{second_product}}` are declared with matching names and filled via `random()` (and a `min/max/step` range for `quantity`).
- **`chatbot.output`** (§6): This is the contract for future rules. Fields like `added_items`, `cart_contents`, `cart_total`, and `removed_item` can later be referenced in rules as `conv[i].cart_total`, etc. I used `list[string]` and `money` (matching your existing profiles' `money` type) so rules can check cart contents and totals.
- **`conversation`** (§5): `single questions` interaction style fits a step-by-step cart-building flow, with a `max_cost` cap and `steps: 20` to keep runs bounded.
- **Conventions matched**: Spanish fallback message and `gpt-4o-mini` model mirror your existing Alcampo profiles.
