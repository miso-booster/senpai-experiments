Now, here's a fresh profile focused on **asking the chatbot for items to add to the shopping cart**. It follows the same style as your other Alcampo profiles (English, `single questions`, `gpt-4o-mini`, sensible cost caps) and exposes `chatbot.output` fields you can later reference in Sensei-check rules as `conv[i].<field>`.

```yaml
test_name: Shopping Cart Item Addition

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    A shopper building an online grocery cart at Alcampo who wants the chatbot
    to help find and add specific items to the cart.
  context:
    - You are preparing a weekly grocery order and want to fill your cart efficiently.
    - You have a mix of everyday staples and a few special items in mind.
    - You prefer clear guidance on how to add products to your cart and confirm what is already in it.
  goals:
    - Ask the chatbot how to add a product to your shopping cart on the Alcampo website.
    - Request help finding a specific item to add to your cart, such as {{product_name}}.
    - Ask the chatbot to confirm which items are currently in your cart.
    - Inquire about the price of the {{product_name}} before adding it to the cart.
    - Ask how to change the quantity of an item already in your cart.
    - product_name:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [whole milk, free-range eggs, whole wheat bread, olive oil, organic bananas]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Parece que tu mensaje no es claro o no es una pregunta relacionada con Alcampo. 😊 ¿En qué puedo ayudarte hoy? Estoy aquí para resolver tus dudas sobre nuestros productos y servicios."
  output:
    - add_to_cart_instructions:
        type: string
        description: Steps or guidance on how to add a product to the shopping cart
    - product_name:
        type: string
        description: Name of the product the user wants to add to the cart
    - product_price:
        type: money
        description: Price of the product being added to the cart
    - cart_items:
        type: list[string]
        description: Items currently present in the shopping cart
    - cart_item_count:
        type: int
        description: Total number of items in the shopping cart
    - quantity_update_instructions:
        type: string
        description: Guidance on how to change the quantity of an item in the cart

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 24
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

A couple of notes so it plays nicely with your project 👀:

- **`product_name`** is declared as a variable and reused in both the goal text and `chatbot.output` — that keeps the contract clean for any future rules (e.g., checking that the confirmed `cart_items` actually contains the requested `product_name`).
- I used `money` for prices to match the convention in your other profiles (like `delivery_fee`, `order_amount`).
