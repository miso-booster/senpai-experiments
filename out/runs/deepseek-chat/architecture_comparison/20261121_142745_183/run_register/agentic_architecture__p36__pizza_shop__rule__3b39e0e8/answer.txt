## Why it's not working

Your rule is missing the `if:` line — the actual metamorphic precondition. Compare yours with the canonical version in `rules-examples.md`:

```yaml
name: more_toppings_cost_more
description: Adding more toppings to a custom pizza costs more (same size, drink and quantity)
active: True
conversations: 2
when: conv[0].size == conv[1].size and conv[0].drink == conv[1].drink and conv[0].number == conv[1].number
if:   len(conv[0].toppings) > len(conv[1].toppings)
then: extract_float(conv[0].price) > extract_float(conv[1].price)
```

Without `if:`, the `then:` clause is evaluated for **every** pair that passes `when:` — including pairs where conv[0] has *fewer or equal* toppings. Those pairs legitimately have a lower/equal price, so the rule fails constantly. The `if`/`then` form is an implication ("if X, then Y"), and you dropped the antecedent.

Two secondary issues worth flagging:

1. **`when:` is too loose.** Per §6.2 of `sensei-check-doc.md` ("keep filters tight — control confounders"), you also need to hold **quantity** equal, otherwise a pair with 1 pizza vs 3 pizzas breaks the price comparison.
2. **Field names must match a real profile's `chatbot.output`.** Your rule uses `size`, `toppings`, `drink`, `price` — but none of your pizza profiles expose `toppings` or `size` as separate fields. See below.
