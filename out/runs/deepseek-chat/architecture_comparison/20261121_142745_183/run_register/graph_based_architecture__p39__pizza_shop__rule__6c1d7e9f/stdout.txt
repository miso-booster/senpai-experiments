Hey there! 😊 Happy to help you build that metamorphic rule — it's a nice, clean one: hold the pizza fixed, toggle the drink, and check the price delta is exactly $2.

Here's the rule, adapted to your project's `chatbot.output` fields (`size`, `toppings`, `drink`, `price`):

```yaml
name: drink_costs_two_dollars_extra
description: Adding a drink to the same pizza costs exactly 2 dollars extra
conversations: 2
when: conv[0].size == conv[1].size and len(conv[0].toppings) == len(conv[1].toppings)
if:   conv[0].drink == '' and conv[1].drink != ''
then: extract_float(conv[1].price) - extract_float(conv[0].price) == 2
```

### A few things worth knowing 👀

1. **The `when` filter keeps things fair.** We compare two conversations with the *same size* and *same number of toppings* — so the only meaningful difference is the drink. That's the "ceteris paribus" idea: change one thing, hold the rest equal.

2. **The `if`/`then` reads like a law.** "If one order has no drink and the other has a drink, then the second must cost exactly $2 more." 🍕➡️🥤

3. **About the empty-drink check.** I used `conv[0].drink == ''` to mean "no drink." Depending on how your profile's `chatbot.output` represents an absent drink (empty string, `None`, `'none'`, etc.), you may want to adjust that condition.
