## 🛒 New profile: `shopping_cart_items.yaml`

Here's a complete, runnable profile that asks the chatbot for items to add to the shopping cart. It follows the same structure as your other Alcampo profiles (English, `single questions`, `gpt-4o-mini`, etc.), so it'll fit right in:

```yaml
test_name: Shopping Cart Items
llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text
user:
  language: English
  role: >
    A customer doing their weekly grocery shopping online who wants to build a
    shopping cart by asking the chatbot for specific items to add.
  context:
    - "You are planning meals for the week and want to fill your online cart efficiently."
    - "You prefer asking the chatbot to add items for you rather than searching manually."
    - "You care about getting the right quantities and confirming items are in your cart."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to add {{product}} to your shopping cart."
    - "Request {{quantity}} units of {{product}} be added to the cart."
    - "Ask the chatbot to confirm which items are currently in your shopping cart."
    - "Inquire about the total price of the items in your cart."
    - "Ask how to remove an item from the shopping cart if you change your mind."
    - product:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [milk, eggs, whole wheat bread, olive oil, chicken breast, apples]
    - quantity:
        function: random()
        type: int
        data:
          min: 1
          max: 6
          step: 1
chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Parece que tu mensaje no es claro o no es una pregunta relacionada con Alcampo. 😊 ¿En qué puedo ayudarte hoy? Estoy aquí para resolver tus dudas sobre nuestros productos y servicios."
  output:
    - added_item:
        type: string
        description: Name of the product added to the shopping cart
    - added_quantity:
        type: int
        description: Quantity of the product added to the cart
    - cart_contents:
        type: list[string]
        description: List of items currently in the shopping cart
    - cart_total:
        type: money
        description: Total price of the items in the shopping cart
    - removal_instructions:
        type: string
        description: Steps to remove an item from the shopping cart
conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 24
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

---

## 🔍 A few notes on the choices I made

- **`product` uses `forward()`** → it cycles deterministically through the list, so each conversation targets a different item. 🎯
- **`quantity` uses `random()`** with a `min`/`max`/`step` range → picks one integer between 1 and 6 each run. (No `list` type in `user.goals`, so a range is the clean way to do this.)
- **`chatbot.output`** captures what a cart flow would realistically return: the added item, its quantity, the full cart contents, the total, and removal instructions. These field names are stable and descriptive, so you can later write **profile-bound rules** against them (e.g. `conv[0].cart_total > 0`, or checking that `added_item` appears in `cart_contents`). 🧠
- **`money`** — I used it for `cart_total` since your other Alcampo profiles use `money` for prices. Just make sure a `money` custom type exists in your `types/` folder (I saw it referenced in several of your profiles, so it likely does 👍).
- **`single questions`** keeps it consistent with your other profiles — one query per turn, which suits a step-by-step cart-building flow.
