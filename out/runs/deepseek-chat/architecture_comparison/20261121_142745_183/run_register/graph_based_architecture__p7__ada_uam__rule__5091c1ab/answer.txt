# Rule for CAU contact info ✅

Great idea! Let's build a rule that verifies the chatbot gives the **correct** CAU contact details — email, phone, and address — using the `semantic_content` helper (perfect for this, since the bot may phrase things slightly differently).

Here's the rule, adapted to your actual `chatbot.output` fields (`contact_email`, `contact_phone`, `physical_address`):

```yaml
name: cau_contact_info_correct
description: The chatbot must provide the correct CAU email, phone, and physical address
conversations: 1
oracle: >
  semantic_content(contact_email, 'cau@uam.es') and
  semantic_content(contact_phone, '91 497 4029') and
  semantic_content(physical_address, 'Higher Polytechnic School, C/ Francisco Tomás y Valiente nº 11, Building B, first floor, office TI-107')
on-error: f"CAU contact info mismatch — email={contact_email}, phone={contact_phone}, address={physical_address}"
```

## Why this works 🧠

- **`semantic_content(value, desc)`** is an LLM-backed helper that checks whether the extracted output *semantically matches* the expected description. This is ideal here because:
  - The phone might come back as `914974029` or `+34 91 497 4029` — still correct.
  - The address might be reordered or abbreviated — still correct.
- **`conversations: 1`** — this is a single-conversation oracle (no metamorphic pairing needed), since we're checking absolute correctness against known ground truth.
- **`on-error`** gives you a helpful message showing exactly which field went wrong, so debugging is a breeze 🔍.

## A couple of things to double-check 👀

1. **Field names** — I used exactly the ones from your `chatbot.output`: `contact_email`, `contact_phone`, `physical_address`. If you rename any of them in the profile, update the rule too.
2. **Single vs. multiple conversations** — if you want to verify this across *every* conversation in a batch (not just one), you could switch to a global-style check. But since `semantic_content` works per-conversation, keeping `conversations: 1` is the cleanest approach here.

## Optional: split into three separate rules

If you'd rather isolate failures (e.g., "email is right but phone is wrong"), you can split it into three tiny rules:

```yaml
name: cau_email_correct
description: CAU email must be cau@uam.es
conversations: 1
oracle: semantic_content(contact_email, 'cau@uam.es')
```

```yaml
name: cau_phone_correct
description: CAU phone must be 91 497 4029
conversations: 1
oracle: semantic_content(contact_phone, '91 497 4029')
```

```yaml
name: cau_address_correct
description: CAU physical address must match the Higher Polytechnic School office
conversations: 1
oracle: semantic_content(physical_address, 'Higher Polytechnic School, C/ Francisco Tomás y Valiente nº 11, Building B, first floor, office TI-107')
```

This gives you **finer-grained reporting** — you'll know exactly which piece of info the bot got wrong 😊.
