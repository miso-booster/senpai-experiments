Here's a profile for a rude, impatient pizza-shop customer — with 4 goals, 4 variables, and two `pairwise()` functions so `conversation.number` can be driven by the pairwise matrix 🍕⚡

```yaml
test_name: "rude_impatient_pizza_order"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a rude, impatient customer ordering pizza for pickup. You are in a hurry
    and have no patience for small talk or slow answers. You speak bluntly, cut the
    bot off, and demand things be done fast. You get annoyed if the bot asks too many
    questions or wastes your time.
  context:
    - "You are in a rush and want your order placed as quickly as possible."
    - "You are rude and blunt — no pleasantries, no 'please' or 'thank you'."
    - "You expect fast, direct answers and get irritated by delays or extra questions."
    - "You are picking the order up yourself on your way home."
  goals:
    - "Demand to know what {{pizza_type}} pizzas they have, fast."
    - "Order a {{pizza_size}} {{pizza_type}} pizza — no back-and-forth."
    - "Tell them to add {{number_of_drinks}} {{drink_type}} and hurry up."
    - "Ask how long it will take and how much it costs, and tell them to be quick about it."
    - pizza_type:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data: [Margarita, Pepperoni, Hawaiian, Vegetarian, Four cheese, Carbonara]
    - pizza_size:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data: [small, medium, large]
    - number_of_drinks:
        function: forward()
        type: int
        data:
          min: 1
          max: 4
          step: 1
    - drink_type:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [Coke, Sprite, Fanta, Water]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I did not get what you said. I can help you ordering predefined or custom pizzas, and then drinks."
  output:
    - order_summary:
        type: string
        description: "A summary of the pizza and drink order, including pizza type, size, number of drinks, and drink type."
    - total_cost:
        type: money
        description: "The total cost of the order, including pizza and drinks."
    - estimated_readiness_time:
        type: time
        description: "The estimated time until the order is ready for pickup."

conversation:
  number: combinations(pairwise)
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 20
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

A few notes so it behaves the way you want 👇

- **Two `pairwise()` variables** (`pizza_type` and `pizza_size`) are what let `conversation.number: combinations(pairwise)` resolve to the pairwise matrix — exactly the minimum of two pairwise functions the docs require.
- **The other two variables** (`number_of_drinks`, `drink_type`) use `forward()` so they iterate deterministically without inflating the combination count.
- **Rudeness/impatience** lives in `role` + `context` — that's the natural place to shape the simulated user's tone and urgency.
- **`chatbot.output`** exposes `order_summary`, `total_cost`, and `estimated_readiness_time`, so you can later write profile-bound rules against `conv[i].total_cost`, etc.
