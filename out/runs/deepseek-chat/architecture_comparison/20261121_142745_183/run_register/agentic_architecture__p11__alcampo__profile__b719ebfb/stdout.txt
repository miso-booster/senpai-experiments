### What I did and why
- **Goals only, no variables** ✅ — every entry under `user.goals` is a plain string, so there are no `{{variables}}` and no variable declarations (per §4 of `sensei-chat-guide.md`).
- **`chatbot.output`** — I added a small extraction schema so you can later write Sensei-check rules against these fields (e.g., `conv[i].subscription_benefits`). This follows the "contract for rules" guidance in §2.1.1. If you'd rather have **no outputs at all**, just remove the `output` block — but note that rules won't be able to read anything from these conversations then.
- **Conversation controls** — `single questions` interaction style fits the "ask one thing at a time" behaviour, and `steps: 8` gives room for the six goals plus follow-ups.
