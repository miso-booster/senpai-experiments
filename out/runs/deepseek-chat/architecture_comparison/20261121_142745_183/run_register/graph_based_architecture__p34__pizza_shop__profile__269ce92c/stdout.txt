Hey there! 😊 Great idea — that's a classic "change of heart" scenario, and Sensei has a built-in interaction style for exactly this: **`change your mind`**. Let me walk you through how to build it.

## The key ingredient: `change your mind`

This interaction style makes the simulated user eventually pivot mid-conversation — perfect for your custom → predefined pizza switch. You just add it under `conversation.interaction_style`.

## A ready-to-use profile

Here's a complete profile you can drop into `profiles/`:

```yaml
test_name: "custom_to_predefined_pizza_switch"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer ordering a pizza. You start by asking for a custom pizza
    with specific toppings, but partway through the conversation you change your
    mind and decide to order a predefined pizza instead.
  context:
    - "You are ordering for a casual dinner at home."
    - "You initially want full control over your toppings, but then realize a predefined pizza is simpler."
  goals:
    - "Ask to build a custom {{custom_size}} pizza with {{toppings}}."
    - "Ask what predefined pizzas are available."
    - "Switch your order to a {{predefined_pizza}} pizza instead."
    - "Confirm the final order and ask for the total price."
    - custom_size:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [small, medium, large]
    - toppings:
        function: random(2)
        type: string
        data: [cheese, mushrooms, pepperoni, olives, ham, pineapple]
    - predefined_pizza:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [Margarita, Pepperoni, Hawaiian, Four cheese, Vegetarian]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I did not get what you said. Can you rephrase?"
  output:
    - initial_custom_request:
        type: string
        description: "The chatbot's response to the initial custom pizza request, including toppings and size."
    - predefined_options:
        type: string
        description: "The list of predefined pizzas the chatbot offers."
    - final_order:
        type: string
        description: "The final confirmed order, which should reflect the predefined pizza, not the custom one."
    - total_price:
        type: money
        description: "The final price of the order."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 20
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
    - single questions
```

## Why this works 👇

- **`change your mind`** nudges the simulator to pivot during the conversation — so the user naturally abandons the custom pizza and lands on a predefined one.
- **`single questions`** keeps each turn focused, which makes the switch easier to observe turn-by-turn.
- The **`final_order`** output field is your contract for later rules — you can write a Sensei-check rule asserting the final order reflects the predefined pizza (not the custom toppings). 🎯
- **`all_answered` with `export: true`** gives you a CSV showing which outputs got satisfied — handy for debugging the switch.

## A couple of tips 💡

- If you want the switch to be *guaranteed* rather than probabilistic, you can also make the goal text explicit (as I did: "Switch your order to a predefined pizza instead") — combining an explicit goal with `change your mind` makes the pivot very reliable.
- Keep `chatbot.fallback` set to your bot's **real** fallback text to avoid endless loops.
