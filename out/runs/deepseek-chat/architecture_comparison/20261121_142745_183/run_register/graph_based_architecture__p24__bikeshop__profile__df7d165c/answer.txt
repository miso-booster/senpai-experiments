```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_info_and_services"

llm:
  temperature: 0.0
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a normal customer talking to the virtual assistant of a bike shop.
    You want general information about the shop and its standard services.
    Phrase your questions clearly and directly, like a regular customer would.
  context:
    - "You are interested in basic shop information and standard pricing."
    - "You only ask about opening/closing times, the price of a new tire, the price of a standard tune-up, the price of a new seat, and general bike care advice."
    - "Do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, or anything outside basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing time of the shop, as stated by the chatbot."
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire provided by the chatbot."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up provided by the chatbot."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat provided by the chatbot."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 9
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### A few notes on the choices I made 👇

- **`is_starter: true`** — since your bot likely greets first, this lets the assistant open the conversation. If your bot waits for the user, flip it to `false`.
- **`goal_style: all_answered`** with `export: true` — this runs until every goal is satisfied and exports a CSV showing which outputs were met. Handy for checking coverage. The `limit: 9` is a safety cap (5 goals, so plenty of room).
- **`interaction_style: default`** — keeps the user natural and clear, exactly as you described ("phrase questions clearly and directly"). No spelling mistakes or language switching here.
- **`temperature: 0.0`** — deterministic, so the simulated user stays on-script and doesn't wander into forbidden topics.
- **`chatbot.output`** — I used `money` for the three prices (matches your existing profiles) and `string` for hours and care advice. These fields are also what any **Sensei-check rules** would read later as `conv[i].tire_price`, etc. — so keep the names stable if you plan to write rules against them.

### One thing to double-check ⚠️
Your `chatbot.output` uses the `money` type — make sure a `types/money.yml` exists in your project (your other profiles like `qa.yml` and `appointment.yml` use it, so it should already be there). If it's missing, the extraction will fail.
