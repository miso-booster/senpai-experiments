Hey there! 😊 Happy to help you build that profile — let's get you a clean, variable-free subscription inquiry profile for the Alcampo project.

Quick heads-up first 👀: I noticed your existing `customer_service_assistance.yaml` is actually **invalid YAML** — the `user` and `goals` keys are missing their colons (`user` → `user:`, `goals` → `goals:`). Worth fixing that one separately so it doesn't break your runs!

Now, here's your new profile — no variables, just goals, following the Sensei profile structure:

```yaml
test_name: "Subscription Services and Benefits Inquiry"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer interested in learning about Alcampo's subscription
    services and the benefits they offer, so you can decide whether to sign up.
  context:
    - "You want to save time and money on your regular grocery shopping."
    - "You are curious about what a subscription actually gives you beyond normal shopping."
    - "You like clear, concrete answers about costs and perks before committing."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to explain the Alcampo Más subscription service and what it includes."
    - "Request the list of benefits you get as a subscriber."
    - "Inquire about the cost of the subscription and any delivery fees involved."
    - "Ask whether the subscription offers any exclusive promotions or discounts."
    - "Request information on how to sign up for the subscription service."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Parece que tu mensaje no es claro o no es una pregunta relacionada con Alcampo. 😊 ¿En qué puedo ayudarte hoy?"
  output:
    - subscription_service_name:
        type: string
        description: "Name of the subscription service"
    - subscription_benefits:
        type: string
        description: "Benefits included in the subscription"
    - subscription_cost:
        type: string
        description: "Cost of the subscription and any delivery fees"
    - exclusive_promotions:
        type: string
        description: "Promotions or discounts exclusive to subscribers"
    - signup_instructions:
        type: string
        description: "Steps to sign up for the subscription"

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 20
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

A few notes to keep in mind 💡:

- **No variables** — as requested, every goal is a plain string, so there's nothing to declare in the `goals` list. 
- **`chatbot.output`** fields are there so Sensei-check rules can later read them as `conv[i].<field_name>` — feel free to trim or rename them to match what you actually want to validate.
- **`single questions`** interaction style pairs nicely with this kind of step-by-step inquiry — the user asks one thing at a time.
- If you'd rather have the user ask everything at once, swap it for `all questions`.
