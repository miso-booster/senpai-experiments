### What it does and why 🔧

- **`user.role` + `context`** encode the customer persona and the hard scope limits (no appointments, models, advanced repairs, warranties, delivery, etc.), so the simulator stays on-topic.
- **`user.goals`** are the five plain-string goals you listed — no variables needed, since each request is fixed. `single questions` keeps the user asking one clear question at a time, matching "phrase questions clearly and directly."
- **`chatbot.output`** is the contract for later Sensei-check rules (per §2.1.1 of `sensei-chat-guide.md`). I used `currency` for the three prices and `string` for hours and advice, so you can later write rules like `conv[0].tire_price > 0` or check that `opening_hours` is present.
- **`conversation`** mirrors your existing `bike_information_seeker.yaml` style: `all_answered` with a safety `limit`, a run cost cap, and 3 conversations.
