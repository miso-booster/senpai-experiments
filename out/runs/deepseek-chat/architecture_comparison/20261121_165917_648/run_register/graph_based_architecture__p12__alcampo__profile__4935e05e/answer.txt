Now, let's get to your new profile. Here's a clean, runnable one that simulates a user asking the chatbot to add items to their cart:

```yaml
test_name: "add_items_to_cart"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a shopper doing your weekly grocery run on Alcampo's online store.
    You want to build up your shopping cart by asking the chatbot to add specific
    products, adjust quantities, and confirm what's already in your cart.
  context:
    - "You are planning meals for the week and want to stock up on essentials."
    - "You prefer to add items one at a time and confirm each addition."
    - "You care about getting the right quantities so you don't overbuy."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to add {{quantity}} of {{product}} to your shopping cart."
    - "Ask the chatbot to add {{product}} to your cart."
    - "Request to increase the quantity of {{product}} in your cart to {{quantity}}."
    - "Ask the chatbot to show you what items are currently in your cart."
    - "Confirm the total number of items in your cart."
    - product:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [milk, eggs, whole wheat bread, olive oil, bananas]
    - quantity:
        function: random()
        type: int
        data:
          min: 1
          max: 6
          step: 1

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Parece que tu mensaje no es claro o no es una pregunta relacionada con Alcampo. 😊 ¿En qué puedo ayudarte hoy?"
  output:
    - added_product:
        type: string
        description: "The product that was added to the cart"
    - added_quantity:
        type: int
        description: "The quantity of the product added"
    - cart_contents:
        type: list[string]
        description: "List of items currently in the shopping cart"
    - cart_item_count:
        type: int
        description: "Total number of items in the cart"
    - cart_total:
        type: currency
        description: "Total price of items in the cart"

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 20
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### A few notes on the choices I made 💡

- **`forward()` on `product`** — cycles deterministically through the product list so you get good coverage across conversations.
- **`random()` on `quantity`** — picks a single random quantity between 1 and 6 each run, keeping things varied but realistic.
- **`chatbot.output`** — I included `cart_contents` as `list[string]` and `cart_total` as `currency` so you can later write **Sensei-check rules** against them (e.g., verifying the cart count matches the items, or that the total is positive). 🎯
- **`single questions`** interaction style — keeps each turn focused on one cart action, which mirrors how a real shopper adds items.

### ⚠️ One thing to double-check
I used `currency` as a type in `chatbot.output`. Make sure you have a `types/currency.yml` in your project (your docs show one, but confirm it exists in `alcampo/types/`). If it's missing, either add it or swap `cart_total` to `type: float`. 👍
