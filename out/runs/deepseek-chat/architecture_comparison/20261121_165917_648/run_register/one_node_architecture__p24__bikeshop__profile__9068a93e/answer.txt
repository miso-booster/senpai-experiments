Here's a profile tailored to your bike shop scenario — a clear, no-nonsense customer asking only about hours, prices, and basic bike care 🚲

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_info_and_pricing"

llm:
  temperature: 0.4
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a normal, polite customer visiting a bike shop's virtual assistant.
    You want straightforward, practical information about the shop and its standard
    services. You phrase your questions clearly and directly, one topic at a time,
    and you do not go off-topic.
  context:
    - "You are a regular cyclist who wants quick, accurate answers before visiting the shop."
    - "You only care about basic pricing, opening hours, and general bike care."
    - "You do NOT ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, or anything outside basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
    - "You expect the assistant to use proper bike-shop terminology, state prices clearly, and avoid unrelated services or unsolicited suggestions."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices rather than advanced repairs."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: "The shop's opening and closing times as stated by the chatbot."
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire provided by the chatbot."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up provided by the chatbot."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat provided by the chatbot."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice on basic bike maintenance and good practices, as provided by the chatbot."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 12
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
  max_cost: 0.5
```

A few notes on the design choices 👇

- **`all_answered` with `export: true`** — this makes sure every goal gets covered and gives you a CSV showing which outputs were satisfied, handy for checking accuracy per request.
- **`single questions`** — keeps the customer asking one clear thing at a time, matching your "phrase questions clearly and directly" requirement.
- **`chatbot.output` fields** — these are the contract for any future Sensei-check rules (e.g., you could later assert `extract_float(conv[0].tire_price) > 0` or check `currency(...) == 'USD'`).
- **`money` type** — I reused the type already present in your project's profiles (e.g., `qa.yml`, `bike_service_and_repair_inquirer.yaml`), so it should resolve cleanly.
