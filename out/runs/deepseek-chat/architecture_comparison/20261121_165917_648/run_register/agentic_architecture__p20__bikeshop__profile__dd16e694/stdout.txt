```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_wheel_change_appointment"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer of a bike shop who wants to book an appointment to have
    the wheels of your bike changed. You are friendly but a bit picky about
    timing, and you want to make sure the shop can fit you in on a specific day
    and time.
  context:
    - "You commute by bike every day, so you need the wheel change done quickly."
    - "You want to know whether the shop has the wheels in stock before booking."
    - "You care about the exact date and time of the appointment and will confirm them explicitly."
    - "If the shop offers a different slot than the one you asked for, you are willing to negotiate."
  goals:
    - "Ask to book an appointment to have the wheels of your bike changed"
    - "Request the appointment for {{ap_date}} at {{ap_time}}"
    - "Confirm the appointment date and time the shop proposes"
    - "Ask whether the new wheels are included in the price"
    - ap_date:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data:
          - tomorrow
          - next Monday
          - next Saturday
          - the 15th of next month
    - ap_time:
        function: forward(ap_date)
        type: string
        data:
          - "09:00"
          - "11:30"
          - "14:00"
          - "17:45"

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - confirmed_date:
        type: date
        description: "The appointment date confirmed by the assistant"
    - confirmed_time:
        type: time("hh:mm")
        description: "The appointment time confirmed by the assistant"
    - service:
        type: string
        description: "The service booked, e.g. wheel change"
    - wheels_included:
        type: string
        description: "Whether the new wheels are included in the price or must be paid separately"
    - appointment_confirmation:
        type: string
        description: "The full confirmation message from the assistant, including date and time"

conversation:
  number: combinations
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 20
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
    - random:
        - make spelling mistakes
        - long phrases
```

### What it does & how it maps to the docs 📚

- **Date/time variables** — `ap_date` and `ap_time` are declared in `user.goals` and used via `{{ap_date}}` / `{{ap_time}}` (per §4.1). I used nested `forward()` so every date gets paired with every time, giving a full grid of day × time combinations to stress-test how the bot handles dates.
- **`conversation.number: combinations`** — since two nested `forward()` variables are declared, this uses the largest combination matrix (4 dates × 4 times = 16 conversations). You can switch to `combinations(0.5, forward)` if you want a smaller sample.
- **`chatbot.output`** — `confirmed_date` uses `date`, `confirmed_time` uses `time("hh:mm")` (both documented output types in §6), so later SENSEI-check rules can read `conv[i].confirmed_date` / `conv[i].confirmed_time` to validate the bot's date/time handling.
- **`interaction_style`** — `change your mind` is explicitly recommended in the docs for appointment-date scenarios, plus a random pick of typos/long phrases to make it realistic.
- **`all_answered` with `export: true`** — gives you a CSV showing which outputs were satisfied, handy for spotting date/time failures.
