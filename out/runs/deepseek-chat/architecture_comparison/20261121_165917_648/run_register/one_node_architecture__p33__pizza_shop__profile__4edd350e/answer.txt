Hey there! 😊 Happy to help you complete that `chatbot` section — it's the part that tells Sensei what to extract at the end of each conversation (and what your rules can later read as `conv[i].<field>`).

Since your goals ask about pizza types, Margarita price, drinks & prices, opening hours, and delivery, I'll mirror those in `chatbot.output`. I also added a real fallback message and `is_starter: false` so the bot doesn't loop endlessly.

```yaml
test_name: pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker
llm:
  temperature: 0.3
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text
user:
  language: English
  role: A customer seeking information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours before potentially placing an order.
  context:
  - 'personality: personalities/skeptical-user'
  - You're planning a casual pizza night at home with friends this weekend and are considering ordering from a local pizzeria.
  - You want to gather all the necessary information before deciding what to order and whether to pick it up or have it delivered.
  - You have a variety of preferences among your friends, so you need to find options that cater to different tastes.
  goals:
  - Ask about the available pizza types.
  - Ask about the price of a Margarita pizza.
  - Ask about the available drinks and their prices.
  - Ask about the shop's operating hours.
  - Ask if they deliver.
chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I did not get what you said. I can help you ordering predefined or custom pizzas, and then drinks."
  output:
    - pizza_types:
        type: string
        description: "A list of the pizza types offered by the shop. Look for phrases like 'We offer [pizza types]' or a list of available pizzas."
    - margarita_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a Margarita pizza, including currency if present. If multiple sizes are mentioned, extract the price for all sizes mentioned."
    - drinks_and_prices:
        type: string
        description: "A list of available drinks and their corresponding prices. Look for phrases like 'We have [drink] for [price]' or a list of drinks and prices."
    - operating_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The shop's operating hours, including opening and closing times. Look for phrases like 'Our hours are [hours]' or 'We are open from [opening time] to [closing time]'."
    - delivery_availability:
        type: string
        description: "Indication of whether the shop delivers or not. Look for phrases like 'We deliver' or 'We do not offer delivery'."
conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 20
  interaction_style:
  - single question
```

A couple of friendly notes 👀:

- **`money` type** — I kept it since your project already uses it in other profiles (e.g. `pizza_and_drink_order_placement`). If your `types/` folder doesn't actually define `money`, swap it for `currency` or `float` to avoid a validation error.
- **Field names are a contract** — if you later write a rule like `extract_float(conv[0].margarita_price) > 0`, it'll only work because `margarita_price` is exposed here. Rename it in the profile → rename it in the rule too. 🔗
- **`is_starter: false`** — correct here since the customer opens the conversation with their first question.
