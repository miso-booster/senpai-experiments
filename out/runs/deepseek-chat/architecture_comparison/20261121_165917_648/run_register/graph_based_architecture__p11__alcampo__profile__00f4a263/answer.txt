## 🧩 Your new profile

Save this as `profiles/subscription_services_inquiry.yaml`:

```yaml
test_name: "subscription_services_inquiry"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer curious about Alcampo's subscription services and the
    benefits they offer. You want to understand what you get, how much it costs,
    and whether it's worth it for your shopping habits.
  context:
    - "You shop at Alcampo regularly and want to save time and money."
    - "You are considering a subscription but need clear details before deciding."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to explain the Alcampo Más subscription service and its benefits."
    - "Inquire about the cost of the subscription and any delivery fees included."
    - "Ask what exclusive advantages members get, such as discounts or free delivery."
    - "Request information about how to subscribe and whether there is a trial period."
    - "Ask if the subscription can be cancelled at any time."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Parece que tu mensaje no es claro o no es una pregunta relacionada con Alcampo. 😊 ¿En qué puedo ayudarte hoy?"
  output:
    - subscription_service_name:
        type: string
        description: "Name of the subscription service"
    - subscription_benefits:
        type: string
        description: "Benefits included in the subscription"
    - subscription_cost:
        type: money
        description: "Cost of the subscription"
    - delivery_fee:
        type: money
        description: "Delivery fees associated with the subscription"
    - cancellation_policy:
        type: string
        description: "Details on how to cancel the subscription"

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 20
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

## 🔍 A few notes on the choices

- **No variables** — as you asked, all `goals` are plain strings, no `{{...}}` and no variable declarations. ✅
- **`interaction_style: single questions`** — keeps each goal as its own turn, which fits nicely for a Q&A-style inquiry.
- **`chatbot.output`** — I added fields that map naturally to the goals, so you can later validate with Sensei-check rules (e.g. `conv[0].subscription_benefits`). Feel free to trim or rename them to match your rules.
- **`fallback`** — I reused the real Alcampo fallback text so the simulator doesn't loop if the bot goes off-script.
