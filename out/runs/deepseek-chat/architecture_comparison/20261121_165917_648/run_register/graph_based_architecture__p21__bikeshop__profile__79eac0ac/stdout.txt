Let me walk you through a solid profile you can drop into your `profiles/` folder, and I'll explain the key choices so you can tweak it to your liking.

## Suggested profile

```yaml
test_name: "new_bike_setup_and_customization"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer who recently bought a new bike and wants the shop to
    set it up and tailor it to your liking, specifically by changing the seat
    and the handlebar grips.
  context:
    - "You just bought a new bike and haven't had it adjusted yet."
    - "The current seat is uncomfortable and the grips feel too thin for your hands."
    - "You want the shop to help you pick comfortable replacements and fit them."
    - "You're friendly but a bit unsure about what options exist, so you ask for recommendations."
  goals:
    - "Explain that you recently bought a new bike and want it set up for your comfort."
    - "Ask if the shop can help you change the seat and the handlebar grips."
    - "Ask what {{seat_type}} seats they recommend for comfort."
    - "Ask what {{grip_type}} handlebar grips they have available."
    - "Ask for the price of the {{seat_type}} seat and the {{grip_type}} grips."
    - "Ask if they can fit the new seat and grips for you, and when."
    - seat_type:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data: [gel, ergonomic, wide comfort]
    - grip_type:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data: [ergonomic, lock-on, foam, rubber]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - seat_recommendation:
        type: string
        description: "The seat(s) the chatbot recommends for comfort."
    - grip_recommendation:
        type: string
        description: "The handlebar grips the chatbot recommends or has available."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of the recommended seat."
    - grip_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of the recommended handlebar grips."
    - fitting_confirmation:
        type: string
        description: "Confirmation of whether the shop can fit the new seat and grips, and when."

conversation:
  number: combinations(0.6, pairwise)
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

## Why these choices 👇

- **`role` + `context`** paint the picture: a new-bike owner who wants comfort tweaks. This keeps the simulated user focused and natural.
- **`goals`** follow a realistic flow: explain the situation → ask about seat & grips → ask prices → ask about fitting. The `{{seat_type}}` and `{{grip_type}}` variables make it reusable across options.
- **`forward()` on `seat_type`** and **`pairwise()` on `grip_type`** give you a nice coverage matrix without exploding the number of conversations. Since you have two combination functions, `number: combinations(0.6, pairwise)` works — it uses 60% of the pairwise matrix. 🎯
- **`chatbot.output`** captures the business facts you'll likely want to validate later (recommendations, prices, fitting confirmation). These become `conv[i].seat_price`, etc., in Sensei-check rules.
- **`all_answered` with `export: true`** gives you a CSV showing which outputs got satisfied — super handy for debugging.
- **`single question`** interaction style keeps each turn focused, which fits a Q&A-style shop bot nicely.

## A couple of things to double-check ⚠️

- I used `type: money` in `chatbot.output` because your existing profiles (e.g. `qa.yml`, `bike_service_and_repair_inquirer.yaml`) use it — just make sure a `types/money.yml` exists in your project. If it doesn't, swap it for `currency` or `float`.
- The `fallback` text matches your other profiles, so the bot's real fallback is respected. 👍
