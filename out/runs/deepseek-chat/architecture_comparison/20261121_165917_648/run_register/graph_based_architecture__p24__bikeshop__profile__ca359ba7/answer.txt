## What this profile does

You're describing a **general-information customer** who asks about:
1. 🕐 Opening and closing times
2. 💰 Price of a new tire
3. 🔧 Price of a standard tune-up
4. 🪑 Price of a new seat
5. 🧼 General bike-care advice

And they must **stay in scope** — no appointments, models, warranties, etc.

## The profile YAML

Here's a complete profile you can drop into `profiles/`:

```yaml
test_name: "general_info_and_basic_services_customer"

llm:
  temperature: 0.4
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a normal, polite customer visiting a bike shop's virtual assistant.
    You want clear, direct answers about the shop's opening hours, the prices of
    a new tire, a standard tune-up, and a new seat, plus some general advice on
    how to take care of a bike. Ask your questions clearly and directly, like a
    regular customer would. Do NOT ask about appointments, specific bike models,
    custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, or anything outside
    of basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care.
  context:
    - "You are a regular customer, friendly and straightforward."
    - "You only care about basic shop info, standard prices, and everyday bike care."
    - "You keep your questions short, clear, and directly worded."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the bike shop's opening and closing times."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: "The shop's opening and closing times as stated by the chatbot."
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire provided by the chatbot."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up provided by the chatbot."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat provided by the chatbot."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General bike-care advice given by the chatbot, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 9
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

## A few notes on the choices 👀

- **`interaction_style: default`** — keeps the user natural and clear, exactly matching your "normal customer, clear and direct" requirement. No spelling mistakes or language switching here.
- **`goal_style: all_answered`** with `export: true` — this makes Sensei keep going until every goal is covered, and exports a CSV showing which outputs were satisfied. Handy for checking coverage! The `limit: 9` is a safety cap.
- **`chatbot.output`** — I used `money` for the three prices (matches your existing profiles like `qa.yml` and `bike_service_and_repair_inquirer.yaml`) and `string` for hours and care advice. These field names are stable and rule-friendly, so you can later write Sensei-check rules like `conv[0].tire_price > 0`.
- **`temperature: 0.4`** — low-ish, so the user stays on-script and doesn't wander into out-of-scope topics.
- **Scope guardrails** — I baked the "don't ask about X" list into both `role` and `context` so the simulator respects your boundaries.
