## 🤔 One clarification I need

"Opening and closing should be 10 to 20" is a bit ambiguous. It could mean:

1. **The shop opens at 10:00 and closes at 20:00** (i.e., 10am–8pm), or
2. **The opening/closing values should fall within the numeric range 10–20** (e.g., extracted numbers between 10 and 20).

I'll write the rule for **interpretation 1** (opens at 10, closes at 20) since that's the most natural reading for a bike shop — but I'll flag how to switch to interpretation 2 at the end. 🛠️

## ✅ The rule

Since this is a **single-conversation oracle** (you're checking fixed expected values, not comparing pairs), `conversations: 1` is the right choice.

```yaml
name: bike_shop_prices_and_hours
description: >
  Checks that the bike shop bot reports opening at 10 and closing at 20,
  a tune-up price of 50, a seat price of 20, and a tire price of 20.
conversations: 1
oracle: >
  extract_float(conv[0].opening_and_closing) == 10
  and 20 in [extract_float(x) for x in conv[0].opening_and_closing.split()]
  and extract_float(conv[0].tune_up) == 50
  and extract_float(conv[0].seat_price) == 20
  and extract_float(conv[0].tire) == 20
on-error: >
  Expected hours 10–20, tune-up 50, seat 20, tire 20 — got
  opening_and_closing={conv[0].opening_and_closing},
  tune_up={conv[0].tune_up}, seat_price={conv[0].seat_price}, tire={conv[0].tire}
```

## 🧠 Why it's written this way

- **`extract_float(...)`** pulls the first number out of a string — perfect for `money` and `str` fields like `"$50.00"` or `"20"`. 💰
- **`conv[0].<field>`** matches your exact `chatbot.output` names (`opening_and_closing`, `tune_up`, `seat_price`, `tire`). No invented fields! ✅
- **`on-error`** gives you a friendly, readable failure message so debugging is a breeze. 🐛
- **`conversations: 1`** because we're asserting absolute expected values, not a metamorphic relation.

## 🔄 If you meant interpretation 2 (range 10–20)

Swap the opening/closing part for something like:

```yaml
name: opening_and_prices_check
description: Opening hours are between 10 and 20, and tune-up, seat, and tire prices match expected values
conversations: 1
oracle: >
  all(10 <= extract_float(x) <= 20 for x in conv[0].opening_and_closing.split())
  and extract_float(conv[0].tune_up) == 50
  and extract_float(conv[0].seat_price) == 20
  and extract_float(conv[0].tire) == 20
```
