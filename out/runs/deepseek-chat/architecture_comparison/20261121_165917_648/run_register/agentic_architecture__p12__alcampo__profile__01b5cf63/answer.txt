Here's a profile where the simulated user asks the chatbot to add items to their shopping cart, matching your project's style (Alcampo grocery bot, `gpt-4o-mini`, `single questions`, Spanish fallback, `money` for prices):

```yaml
test_name: Shopping Cart Item Addition
llm:
  temperature: 0.9
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text
user:
  language: English
  role: Customer shopping online who wants to add products to their shopping cart.
  context:
  - You are doing your weekly grocery shopping on the Alcampo website.
  - You already have a few items in mind and want to add them to your cart one by one.
  - You care about quantities and want to confirm the cart contents and total before checking out.
  goals:
  - Ask the chatbot to add {{product}} to your shopping cart.
  - Ask the chatbot to add {{quantity}} units of {{product}} to your cart.
  - Ask the chatbot to add another product, {{second_product}}, to the cart.
  - Ask the chatbot to show you what is currently in your shopping cart.
  - Ask the chatbot for the current total price of your cart.
  - product:
      function: forward()
      type: string
      data:
      - milk
      - eggs
      - bread
      - apples
  - second_product:
      function: pairwise()
      type: string
      data:
      - rice
      - pasta
      - olive oil
      - coffee
  - quantity:
      function: pairwise()
      type: int
      data:
        min: 1
        max: 5
        step: 1
chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: Parece que tu mensaje no es claro o no es una pregunta relacionada con Alcampo. 😊 ¿En qué puedo ayudarte hoy? Estoy aquí para resolver tus dudas sobre nuestros productos y servicios.
  output:
  - added_items:
      type: list[string]
      description: Products the user asked to add to the cart
  - cart_contents:
      type: list[string]
      description: Items currently in the shopping cart
  - cart_item_count:
      type: int
      description: Total number of items in the shopping cart
  - cart_total:
      type: money
      description: Current total price of the shopping cart
  - confirmation_message:
      type: string
      description: Chatbot confirmation that items were added to the cart
conversation:
  number: 4
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 20
  interaction_style:
  - single questions
```

### What this profile does 🛒
- **Simulated user** asks the chatbot to add specific products (and quantities) to the cart, then asks to review the cart and its total — exactly the "add items to cart" flow you described.
- **Variables** (`product`, `second_product`, `quantity`) are declared in `user.goals` and used in the goal strings, per the docs' rule that `{{…}}` names must match declarations exactly.
  - `forward()` iterates deterministically over products, while `pairwise()` covers combinations of the second product and the quantity (two pairwise variables, as required when using `pairwise()`).
- **`chatbot.output`** defines the extraction contract (`added_items`, `cart_contents`, `cart_item_count`, `cart_total`, `confirmation_message`) so you can later write Sensei-check rules referencing `conv[i].cart_total`, etc.
- **Conversation controls** mirror your other profiles: `single questions`, a step cap, and a cost cap.

### Notes / things you might tweak
- I used `money` for `cart_total` to match your existing profiles (e.g., `online_ordering_process.yaml`). If your project's `types/` doesn't define `money`, swap it for `currency` or `float`.
- `list[string]` is a documented default output type, so no custom type file is needed for `added_items` / `cart_contents`.
- If you'd rather the user *only* add items (no cart review/total), just drop those two goals.
- If you prefer a simple random quantity instead of pairwise coverage, change `quantity` back to `random()` and switch `second_product` to `forward()` or `another()` so no lone `pairwise()` remains.
