Hey there! 😊 Love this idea — testing date handling is super valuable, since appointment logic is where chatbots often trip up. Let me put together a profile for you.

A couple of quick design notes so you know what I did:
- I used **nested `forward()`** on `service` → `ap_date` so you get a nice grid of date combinations, and `another()` on `hour` so times cycle without repeating until the pool runs out.
- I included a **`chatbot.output`** with `confirmed_date`, `confirmed_time`, and `confirmed_service` so you can later write Sensei-check rules against them (e.g., "confirmed date must match requested date").
- I set `is_starter: false` (the user opens the conversation) — flip it to `true` if your bot greets first.

```yaml
test_name: "wheel_change_appointment_scheduler"

llm:
  temperature: 0.5
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer who needs to book an appointment at a bike shop
    to have your wheels changed. You are friendly but focused on getting
    a specific date and time confirmed.
  context:
    - "Your current wheels are worn out and you want them replaced soon."
    - "You are flexible on the exact day but you have a clear preference each time."
    - "You expect the assistant to confirm the date, time, and service clearly."
  goals:
    - "Ask to schedule an appointment to have your wheels changed."
    - "Request the appointment for {{ap_date}} at {{hour}}."
    - "Confirm the service is a {{service}}."
    - "Ask the assistant to repeat back the confirmed date and time."
    - service:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [wheel replacement, wheel change, full wheel set replacement]
    - ap_date:
        function: forward(service)
        type: string
        data:
          - tomorrow
          - "next Monday"
          - "November 6th, 2024"
          - "the 15th of December"
          - "this Saturday"
    - hour:
        function: another()
        type: string
        data: ["09:00", "10:30", "12:00", "14:00", "15:30", "17:00"]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - confirmed_date:
        type: date
        description: "The appointment date confirmed by the assistant."
    - confirmed_time:
        type: time("hh:mm")
        description: "The appointment time confirmed by the assistant."
    - confirmed_service:
        type: string
        description: "The service to be performed, as confirmed by the assistant."

conversation:
  number: combinations(0.6, forward)
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 12
    max_cost: 0.1
  interaction_style:
    - random:
      - make spelling mistakes
      - single questions
      - long phrases
```

A few things you can tweak easily 👇
- **More date variety?** Just add entries to `ap_date.data` — the `forward()` grid will expand automatically.
- **Want full Cartesian coverage?** Change `conversation.number` to `combinations` (no fraction) — but watch your cost cap!
- **Want to stress-test rescheduling?** Add a goal like *"Then ask to move it to {{alt_date}}"* with its own variable.
