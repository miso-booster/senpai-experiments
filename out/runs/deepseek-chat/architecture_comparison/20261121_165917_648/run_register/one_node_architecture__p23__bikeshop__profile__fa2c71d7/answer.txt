## What's wrong 🔍

1. **`llm.temperature: low`** — temperature must be a **number** (e.g. `0.0`, `0.6`), not a word like `low`. This alone will fail validation.
2. **`type: str`** — the valid primitive is `string`, not `str`. Same for `money` — that's not a built-in type. You need either `float`/`currency` (if you have a custom `currency` type) or `string`. Since there's no `money` type in your `types/` folder, I'll switch to `string` (or you can define a custom type).
3. **`seat_price` and `care_bike` have no `type`** — every output field needs a `type`. Missing it will error out.
4. **`conversation.number: 5`** — this is fine on its own, but note your goals don't use any variables, so each of the 5 runs will be identical. That's allowed, just worth knowing.
5. **`interaction_style: [default]`** — valid, but `default` is a single keyword; keep it as a list item as you have it. ✅ (this one's actually fine)

## Fixed version ✅

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.0
  model: gpt-4o-mini

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: string
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: string
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: string
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

## A couple of friendly tips 💡

- **If you want real price extraction**, define a custom `currency` type in `types/currency.yml` (regex + extraction schema) and use `type: currency` for `tire`, `tune_up`, and `seat_price`. That way Sensei-check rules can later do things like `extract_float(conv[0].tire) > 0`. Right now `string` will just capture the raw text.
- **`is_starter: false`** means the *user* speaks first — good for a Q&A profile like this. ✅
- Since your goals are fixed strings, `number: 5` just repeats the same conversation 5 times. If you want variety, add a variable (e.g. `{{item}}`) with a `random()` function.
