Great question! 😊 Let me walk you through it — and I'll keep it aligned with your existing pizza shop rules.

## The idea

You want a **single-conversation oracle** that checks the chatbot actually provided an `order_id` in its output. Since your profile exposes `order_id` as a `chatbot.output` field, the cleanest check is:

- The field exists and is **not empty / not null**.

## The rule

```yaml
name: order_id_provided
description: The chatbot must provide a non-empty order_id in its output
conversations: 1
oracle: conv[0].order_id is not None and len(str(conv[0].order_id).strip()) > 0
```

## Why this works 👀

- `conv[0].order_id` reads the `order_id` slot from your profile's `chatbot.output`.
- `is not None` catches the case where the slot was never filled.
- `len(str(...).strip()) > 0` catches empty strings or whitespace-only values (and safely handles non-string types by casting to `str`).

## A couple of variations depending on your intent

**If you also want to enforce a format** (e.g., IDs like `ORD-12345`), you could add a pattern check:

```yaml
name: order_id_provided
description: The chatbot must provide a well-formed order_id (e.g. ORD-12345)
conversations: 1
oracle: conv[0].order_id is not None and str(conv[0].order_id).startswith('ORD-')
```

**If you'd rather use the built-in slot helper** (checks that *all* extracted outputs are non-null — handy if `order_id` is the only slot you care about, or you want a stricter "everything filled" check):

```yaml
name: order_id_provided
description: All extracted outputs (including order_id) are non-null
conversations: 1
oracle: data_collected() is True
```

⚠️ Just a heads-up: `data_collected()` checks **all** slots, not only `order_id`. If your profile has other optional fields, the first version (targeting `conv[0].order_id` directly) is more precise.

## One thing to double-check ✅

Make sure your profile's `chatbot.output` actually declares `order_id` — otherwise the rule can't read it. Something like:

```yaml
test_name: "pizza_order_summary"

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer ordering a pizza from a pizza shop.
    Provide your order details and confirm the final summary.
  context:
    - "You want a clear summary of your order."
  goals:
    - "Order a pizza and ask for the order summary"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, could you rephrase that?"
  output:
    - order_id:
        type: string
        description: Unique order identifier provided to the customer

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - default
```
