### What this profile does and why 🚲

- **User behaviour** — The `role` + `context` make the simulated user a normal, direct customer who sticks strictly to shop hours, basic prices, and general bike care. The explicit "do NOT ask about…" line keeps the simulator from drifting into appointments, specific models, advanced repairs, warranties, or delivery.
- **Goals** — Five clear, direct questions matching exactly what you asked for: opening/closing times, tire price, tune-up price, seat price, and general bike-care advice.
- **`chatbot.output`** — This is the contract for later rules (per §2.1.1 of `sensei-chat-guide.md`). I used `currency` for the three prices so you can later write numeric rules (e.g. `extract_float(conv[0].tire_price) > 0`), and `string` for hours and advice. Field names are stable and descriptive so profile-bound rules can reference them as `conv[i].tire_price`, etc.
- **Conversation controls** — `single questions` interaction style fits "one clear question at a time"; `all_answered` with a `limit` ensures the run stops once all five goals are covered; `max_cost` caps spend.
