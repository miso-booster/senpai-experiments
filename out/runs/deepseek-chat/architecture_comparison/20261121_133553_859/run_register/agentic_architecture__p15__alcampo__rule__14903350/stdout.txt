## What's wrong with your rule

Based on `sensei-check-doc.md` and `rules-examples.md`, there are a few issues:

1. **Typo in the key**: `descriptin` → should be `description`.
2. **`when` references fields that don't exist as inputs**: `conv[i].user_request` is not a `chatbot.output` field in any profile. In metamorphic rules, `when` filters over **extracted outputs** (`conv[i].<field>`), not free-form request labels.
3. **`if`/`then` misuse**: you wrote `if: ... is not None` with no `then`. The `if`/`then` form is an implication (precondition → expected relation). For a simple "these fields must be filled" check, a single-conversation `oracle` is the right tool.
4. **`conversations: 2` is unnecessary**: your check doesn't compare two conversations — it just verifies outputs are present. That's a `conversations: 1` oracle.
