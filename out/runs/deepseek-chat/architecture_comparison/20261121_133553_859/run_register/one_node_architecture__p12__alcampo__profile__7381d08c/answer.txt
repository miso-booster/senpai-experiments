Here's a fresh profile focused on adding items to the shopping cart. It follows the same structure as your other Alcampo profiles (English user, `single questions`, `gpt-4o-mini`, and a `chatbot.output` schema you can later reference from Sensei-check rules):

```yaml
test_name: Shopping Cart Item Addition

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer shopping online at Alcampo and want to build up your
    shopping cart by asking the chatbot which items you can add. You are
    practical and want clear guidance on how to add products to your cart.
  context:
    - "You are planning your weekly grocery shop and want to fill your cart efficiently."
    - "You prefer to ask the chatbot directly which items are available to add."
    - "You care about knowing the price and quantity of each item before adding it."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot which items are available to add to your shopping cart."
    - "Request to add {{product_name}} to your cart."
    - "Ask how many units of {{product_name}} are currently in your cart."
    - "Inquire about the price of {{product_name}} before confirming."
    - "Ask the chatbot to confirm the total number of items in your cart."
    - product_name:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [milk, eggs, olive oil, whole wheat bread, orange juice]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Parece que tu mensaje no es claro o no es una pregunta relacionada con Alcampo. 😊 ¿En qué puedo ayudarte hoy? Estoy aquí para resolver tus dudas sobre nuestros productos y servicios."
  output:
    - available_items:
        type: string
        description: Items the chatbot offers that can be added to the cart
    - added_item_name:
        type: string
        description: Name of the product the user added to the cart
    - item_quantity_in_cart:
        type: int
        description: Number of units of the product currently in the cart
    - item_price:
        type: money
        description: Price of the product being added
    - cart_total_items:
        type: int
        description: Total number of items in the shopping cart
    - cart_confirmation:
        type: string
        description: Confirmation message that the item was added to the cart

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 22
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

A few notes so it plays nicely with the rest of your project 👇

- **`chatbot.output` is your rule contract.** Fields like `added_item_name`, `item_quantity_in_cart`, and `cart_total_items` can later be referenced in Sensei-check rules as `conv[i].added_item_name`, etc. For example, a nice metamorphic rule would be: *if the same product is added twice, `cart_total_items` should increase by the added quantity.*
- **`forward()` on `product_name`** cycles deterministically through the list, so across your 3 conversations you'll get varied products — good for coverage.
- **`money` type** — I used `money` to match the convention in your other Alcampo profiles (e.g., `delivery_fee`, `checkout_total`). If your project's custom types define it as `currency` instead, just swap the name to stay consistent. 👍
