Hey there! 👋 Happy to help you fix this profile — I spotted a few things that would trip up Sensei. Let me walk you through them 😊

## What's broken 🔍

### 1. `chatbot:` section is missing (biggest issue!)
Your `is_starter`, `fallback`, and `output` are **indented under `user:`** instead of being under their own `chatbot:` key. That means Sensei won't recognize them at all — the output schema (which rules depend on!) would be lost.

### 2. `conversation.number` is missing
Every profile needs a `number` for how many conversations to run. Without it, the run won't know how many times to execute.

### 3. `export: 1` should be a boolean
`export` expects `true` or `false`, not `1`.

### 4. `type: str` should be `type: string`
In `chatbot.output`, the primitive is `string` (not `str`). `str` is only valid inside a custom type's `extraction` field.

### 5. `interaction_style: single question` → should be `single questions`
The reserved keyword is plural: `single questions`. A typo here means the style won't be applied.

### 6. Fallback has a stray backtick + troubleshooting text
That `` `\nFor troubleshooting... `` looks like it leaked from a LangChain error message. Your `fallback` should be the **real bot fallback text** — otherwise Sensei may loop. I'd trim it to just the human-readable part.

## Here's the fixed version ✅

```yaml
test_name: general_information_seeker

llm:
  temperature: 0.3
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: A potential customer seeking general information about the pizza shop before placing an order.
  context:
    - You are planning a casual pizza night with friends and want to explore options from a local pizza shop you haven't tried before.
    - Before placing an order, you want to gather some general information about their menu, services, and pricing.
  goals:
    - Ask what types of questions the chatbot can answer.
    - Ask what the daily opening hours are.
    - Ask about the estimated order time, without specifying the number of pizzas.
    - Ask what drink options are available and their prices.
    - Ask about the pricing for a custom pizza.

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I did not get what you said. I can help you ordering predefined or custom pizzas, and then drinks."
  output:
    - available_information:
        type: string
        description: The chatbot's response listing the types of questions it can answer. Look for phrases like "I can answer questions about..." or "I can provide information on...".
    - opening_hours:
        type: string
        description: The daily opening hours of the pizza shop, as stated by the chatbot.
    - estimated_order_time:
        type: string
        description: The chatbot's explanation of the estimated order time, including factors that influence it.
    - drink_options_and_prices:
        type: string
        description: A list of available drink options and their corresponding prices, as provided by the chatbot.
    - custom_pizza_pricing:
        type: string
        description: The chatbot's explanation of pricing for a custom pizza, including size and additional ingredients.

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 20
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```
