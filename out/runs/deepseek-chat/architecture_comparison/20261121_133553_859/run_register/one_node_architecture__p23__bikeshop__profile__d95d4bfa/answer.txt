Hey there! 👋 Happy to help you get this profile running. I spotted a few things that would break or misbehave — let me walk you through them and then give you the fixed version 😊

**What's off:**

1. **`llm.temperature: low`** — temperature must be a **number** (e.g. `0.0`–`1.0`), not a word. This alone will likely fail validation.
2. **`type: money`** — `money` isn't a built-in type. The docs support `int`, `float`, `string`, `time(...)`, `date(...)`, and `list[...]`, plus custom types from `types/`. If you don't have a `money` custom type defined, use `string` (or `float` if you only want the number). I'll use `string` to be safe.
3. **`type: str`** — should be `string` (the full name).
4. **Missing `type` on `seat_price` and `care_bike`** — every output field needs a `type`. I'll add `string`.
5. **`conversation.number: 5`** — that's fine on its own, but note your goals have no variables, so 5 identical runs will just repeat. That's okay for a smoke test, but worth knowing. I'll keep it at `5` since you asked for it, but you may prefer `1`–`2` to save cost. 💸

**A couple of friendly tips** 🌟

- If you *do* have a custom `money` type in your `types/` folder, you can switch `tire`, `tune_up`, and `seat_price` back to `type: money` — just make sure the file exists and the `name` matches.
- If you want to actually *validate* prices later with Sensei-check rules, `float` (or a proper `money`/`currency` custom type) is much better than `string`, since you can then use `extract_float(...)` in your rules.
- Since your goals have no `{{variables}}`, running `number: 5` gives you 5 near-identical conversations. If you just want a quick sanity check, `number: 1` or `2` is cheaper and faster.
