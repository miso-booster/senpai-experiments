Here's a profile tailored to your bike shop assistant test 🚲 — it keeps the user focused on hours, pricing, and basic bike care, with clean single-question phrasing and outputs that map directly to each request.

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_info_and_pricing"

llm:
  temperature: 0.4
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a normal, polite customer visiting a bike shop's virtual assistant.
    You want straightforward general information about the shop and its standard
    services. Ask clear, direct questions and keep the conversation focused on
    shop hours, basic prices, and general bike care.
  context:
    - "You are a regular cyclist who wants quick, practical answers before visiting the shop."
    - "You prefer clear and direct questions, one topic at a time."
    - "You are only interested in basic pricing, opening hours, and general bike care advice."
    - "Do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, or anything outside basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: "The shop's opening and closing times as stated by the chatbot."
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire provided by the chatbot."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up provided by the chatbot."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat provided by the chatbot."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "The general bike care and basic maintenance advice given by the chatbot."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 12
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
  max_cost: 0.5
```

A couple of quick notes so it behaves the way you expect 👇

- **`single questions`** keeps the user asking one thing at a time — that's what makes it easy to check each answer for accuracy and relevance.
- **`chatbot.output`** fields (`opening_and_closing`, `tire_price`, `tune_up_price`, `seat_price`, `bike_care_advice`) are the contract you can later reference in Sensei-check rules as `conv[i].tire_price`, etc. — handy if you want to add price or completeness checks later.
- **`all_answered` with `limit: 12`** gives the conversation enough room to cover all five goals without running away.
