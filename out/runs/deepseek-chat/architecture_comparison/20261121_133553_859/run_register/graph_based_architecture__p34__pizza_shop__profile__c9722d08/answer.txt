Hey there! 😊 Great idea — that's a classic "change of heart" scenario, and Sensei has a built-in interaction style for exactly this: **`change your mind`**. Let me walk you through how to build that profile.

## The key ingredient: `change your mind`

In the `conversation.interaction_style` section, adding `change your mind` tells the simulated user to eventually pivot mid-conversation. Combined with goals that cover *both* a custom pizza and a predefined one, you get the switch you're after.

## A ready-to-use profile

Here's a profile tailored to your pizza shop project. Drop it into `profiles/` as something like `custom_to_predefined_switch.yml`:

```yaml
test_name: "custom_to_predefined_switch"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer ordering a pizza. You start by asking for a custom pizza
    with your own toppings, but partway through the conversation you change your
    mind and decide to go with a predefined pizza from the menu instead.
  context:
    - "You are ordering for a casual dinner at home."
    - "You initially feel creative and want to build your own pizza."
    - "After hearing the options, you realize a predefined pizza is simpler and cheaper."
  goals:
    - "Ask to order a custom pizza with {{toppings}}."
    - "Ask what predefined pizzas are available."
    - "Switch to ordering a {{pizza_type}} pizza instead."
    - "Confirm the final order and ask for the total price."
    - toppings:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [cheese, mushrooms, pepperoni, olives]
    - pizza_type:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [Margarita, Pepperoni, Four cheese, Vegetarian]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I did not get what you said. I can help you ordering predefined or custom pizzas, and then drinks."
  output:
    - custom_pizza_request:
        type: string
        description: "The chatbot's response to the initial custom pizza request."
    - predefined_options:
        type: string
        description: "The list of predefined pizzas the chatbot offers."
    - final_order:
        type: string
        description: "The final confirmed pizza order after the user changed their mind."
    - total_price:
        type: money
        description: "The total price of the final order."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 20
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
    - single questions
```

## Why this works 🎯

- **`change your mind`** nudges the simulator to pivot during the conversation — that's the heart of your request.
- The **goals are ordered** so the user first asks for a custom pizza, then asks about predefined ones, then switches. That gives the "change of mind" a natural landing spot.
- **`single question`** keeps each turn focused, so the switch is easy to spot in the transcript.
- The **`chatbot.output`** fields capture both the custom request *and* the final predefined order, so you can verify the bot handled the pivot gracefully.

## A couple of tuning tips 👀

- If you want the switch to feel more abrupt, drop `single question` and let the user flow more naturally.
- If you'd rather test a *specific* predefined pizza every time, swap `forward()` for `random()` on `pizza_type`.
- Want to also check the bot doesn't lose the custom request context? Add an output field like `custom_request_acknowledged` with a clear description.
