Hey there! 😊 Great idea — that's a classic "change of heart" scenario, and Sensei has a built-in interaction style for exactly this: **`change your mind`**. Let me walk you through how to build it.

## The key ingredient: `change your mind`

In the `conversation.interaction_style` section, adding `change your mind` makes the simulated user naturally reconsider partway through — perfect for a "custom → predefined" pivot. 🎯

## A ready-to-use profile

Here's a profile tailored to your pizza shop project. Drop it into `profiles/` as something like `custom_to_predefined_switch.yml`:

```yaml
test_name: "custom_to_predefined_switch"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer ordering a pizza. You start by asking for a custom pizza
    with your own choice of toppings, but partway through the conversation you
    change your mind and decide to order a predefined pizza from the menu instead.
  context:
    - "You are ordering for a casual dinner at home."
    - "You initially want full control over the toppings, then realize a menu pizza is simpler."
  goals:
    - "Ask what pizza types are available."
    - "Start ordering a custom pizza with {{toppings}}."
    - "Change your mind and instead order a predefined {{pizza_type}} pizza."
    - "Confirm the final order and ask for the total price."
    - pizza_type:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [Margarita, Pepperoni, Four cheese, Hawaiian, Vegetarian]
    - toppings:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [cheese, mushrooms, pepperoni, olives, ham]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I did not get what you said. I can help you ordering predefined or custom pizzas, and then drinks."
  output:
    - pizza_options:
        type: string
        description: "The list of available pizza types the chatbot provides."
    - custom_pizza_request:
        type: string
        description: "The chatbot's response to the initial custom pizza request."
    - final_order_summary:
        type: string
        description: "The final confirmed order, which should reflect a predefined pizza, not a custom one."
    - total_cost:
        type: money
        description: "The total cost of the final order."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 20
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
    - single questions
  max_cost: 0.8
```

## Why this works 👇

- **`change your mind`** in `interaction_style` is what triggers the mid-conversation pivot — no need to script it manually in the goals. The simulator will naturally reconsider.
- The **goals** set up the *intent* (start custom, then switch to predefined), so the user has a clear reason to change direction.
- **`final_order_summary`** in `chatbot.output` lets you (and later, Sensei-check rules) verify the bot actually honored the *final* choice — a nice invariant to test! ✅
- I used `forward()` on `pizza_type` so each conversation tries a different predefined pizza, and `random()` on toppings for variety.

## A couple of tips 💡

- If you want the switch to be even more explicit, you can add a goal line like `"Say you've changed your mind and want a menu pizza instead."` — but `change your mind` usually handles it naturally.
- Keep `single question` if you want the user to ask one thing at a time (easier to follow the pivot); drop it if you prefer a more free-flowing chat.
- Since your project already has `pizza_and_drink_order_placement.yaml` using `change your mind`, this profile will feel consistent with your existing suite. 🍕
