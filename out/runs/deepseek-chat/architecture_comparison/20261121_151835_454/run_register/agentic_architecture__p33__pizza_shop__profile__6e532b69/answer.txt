## What goes in the `chatbot` field

Per the profiles guide (§3 and §6), the `chatbot` section has three parts:

- **`is_starter`** → who opens the conversation. Your simulated user is a *customer seeking information*, so the **bot should greet first** → `is_starter: true`.

- **`output`** → the schema of fields extracted at the end of each conversation. This doubles as the **contract for rules** later (`conv[i].<field_name>`). I've mapped these directly to your user goals.
