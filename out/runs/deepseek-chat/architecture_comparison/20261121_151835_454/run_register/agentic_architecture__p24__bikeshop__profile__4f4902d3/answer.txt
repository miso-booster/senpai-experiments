Here's the profile, built following the structure in `sensei-chat-guide.md` (sections 3, 4, 5, and 6):

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_info_assistant"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a normal customer visiting a bike shop's virtual assistant.
    You are polite, behave like a typical shopper, and phrase your questions
    clearly and directly. You are only interested in general information about
    the shop and its standard services: opening and closing times, basic prices,
    and general bike care advice.
  context:
    - "You are a regular customer, not an expert mechanic."
    - "You ask straightforward, clearly worded questions."
    - "You only care about basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
    - "You do NOT ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, or anything outside basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "Ask what time the bike shop opens and closes."
    - "Ask how much a new bike tire costs."
    - "Ask how much a standard bike tune-up costs."
    - "Ask how much a new bike seat costs."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices rather than advanced repairs."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, I didn't understand that. Could you rephrase your question?"
  output:
    - opening_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The shop's opening and closing times as stated by the chatbot"
    - tire_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a new bike tire"
    - tune_up_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up"
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a new bike seat"
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General bike care and basic maintenance advice given by the chatbot"

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 20
    max_cost: 0.15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### What this profile does and why 🚲

- **`user.role` & `user.context`** — Set the persona as a normal, polite customer who asks clear, direct questions and stays strictly within basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care. The explicit "do NOT ask about…" line keeps the simulator from drifting into appointments, models, advanced repairs, warranties, or delivery.
- **`user.goals`** — One goal per required request (hours, tire price, tune-up price, seat price, general care advice). No variables are needed here since the requests are fixed, so the profile stays simple and deterministic.
- **`chatbot.output`** — Defines the extraction schema so you (and any future Sensei-check rules) can validate the bot's answers. I used `currency` for the three prices and `string` for hours and advice. These field names are stable and descriptive, per the doc's best practices (§2.1.2), so you can later write profile-bound rules like `conv[i].tire_price`.
- **`conversation.goal_style: all_answered`** — Runs until every goal is addressed (with `export: true` so you get a CSV showing which outputs were satisfied), plus a per-conversation cost cap for safety.
- **`interaction_style: default`** — Keeps the customer behaving naturally, matching your "behave like a normal customer" requirement.

A couple of notes:
- I set `is_starter: false` (customer speaks first). If your bike-shop bot greets first, flip it to `true`.
- Replace the `fallback` text with the bot's **real** fallback message to avoid loops (per §9 best practices).
