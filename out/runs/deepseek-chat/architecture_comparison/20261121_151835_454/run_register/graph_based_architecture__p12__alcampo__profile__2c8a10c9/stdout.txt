I noticed something important first: the `customer_service_assistance.yaml` profile in your project has a **YAML syntax error** — the `user:` and `goals:` keys are missing their colons (`user` → `user:`, `goals` → `goals:`). That's why it failed to parse. Worth fixing separately! 😊

Now, let's create your **shopping cart profile**. Here's a clean, runnable YAML that fits right into your `profiles/` folder:

```yaml
test_name: "Shopping Cart Item Addition"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer shopping online at Alcampo and want to add various
    products to your shopping cart. Ask the chatbot how to add items,
    request specific products, and confirm what is in your cart.
  context:
    - "You are planning your weekly grocery shopping and want to build your cart efficiently."
    - "You prefer to add items one by one and confirm each addition."
    - "You want to know the running total and item count in your cart."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot how to add a product to your shopping cart."
    - "Request to add {{product_name}} to your cart."
    - "Ask for the current number of items in your cart."
    - "Inquire about the total price of the items currently in your cart."
    - "Ask how to remove an item from the cart if you change your mind."
    - product_name:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [organic bananas, whole milk, Galician beef, olive oil, fresh bread]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Parece que tu mensaje no es claro o no es una pregunta relacionada con Alcampo. 😊 ¿En qué puedo ayudarte hoy?"
  output:
    - added_product_name:
        type: string
        description: "Name of the product added to the cart"
    - cart_item_count:
        type: int
        description: "Total number of items in the shopping cart"
    - cart_total_price:
        type: money
        description: "Total price of items in the cart"
    - add_to_cart_instructions:
        type: string
        description: "Steps or guidance for adding items to the cart"
    - removal_instructions:
        type: string
        description: "Guidance on removing items from the cart"

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 20
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

## A few notes on the design 🧠

- **`forward()` on `product_name`** — this cycles deterministically through the product list across conversations, so each run tests a different item. Nice for coverage!
- **`chatbot.output` fields** — I used `money` for the total (matches your other profiles) and `int` for the item count. These become `conv[i].cart_total_price`, etc., if you later write Sensei-check rules against them.
- **`single questions`** — keeps the user asking one thing at a time, which is realistic for cart-building.
- **`fallback`** — I reused the Spanish fallback from your other Alcampo profiles so it matches your bot's real behavior.

## ⚠️ One thing to double-check

Your other profiles use `type: money` in `chatbot.output`, but the docs list `currency` as the standard custom type. If you have a `types/money.yml` defined in your project, you're golden ✅. If not, swap `money` → `currency` (or `float`) to avoid extraction errors.
