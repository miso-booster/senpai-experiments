Here's a rule that checks the CAU contact details are all present and non-empty for the `consultante_de_servicios_y_asistencia_del_cau` profile 👇

```yaml
name: cau_contact_info_complete
description: >
  The Ada UAM chatbot must provide complete CAU contact information:
  a contact email, a contact phone number, and a physical address.
  Targets the consultante_de_servicios_y_asistencia_del_cau profile
  (fields contact_email, contact_phone, physical_address from chatbot.output).
conversations: 1
active: true
oracle: |
  data_collected() is True
  and conv[0].contact_email not in (None, '')
  and conv[0].contact_phone not in (None, '')
  and conv[0].physical_address not in (None, '')
yields: |
  f"Missing CAU contact info — email: {conv[0].contact_email}, phone: {conv[0].contact_phone}, address: {conv[0].physical_address}"
```

A couple of quick notes so it behaves exactly as you expect 😊

- **`data_collected()`** already ensures *all* extracted slots are non-null, but I added explicit checks on the three fields so the rule stays meaningful even if you later add more outputs to the profile.
- The field names (`contact_email`, `contact_phone`, `physical_address`) match your profile's `chatbot.output` exactly — if you rename any of them, update the rule too.
- If you'd rather keep it minimal and rely purely on the profile schema, you can drop the three explicit checks and keep just `data_collected() is True`.
