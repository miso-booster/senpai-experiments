Here's a profile tailored to your bike shop scenario — a clear, direct customer asking only about hours, basic prices, and general bike care 🚲

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_info_and_basic_pricing"

llm:
  temperature: 0.4
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a normal, friendly customer visiting a bike shop's virtual assistant.
    You want straightforward general information about the shop and its standard
    services. Phrase your questions clearly and directly, like a regular customer
    would. Stay strictly within basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care.
  context:
    - "You are a regular cyclist who wants quick, practical answers before visiting the shop."
    - "You ask one clear question at a time and keep the tone polite and natural."
    - "You do NOT ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, or anything outside basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices rather than advanced repairs."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: "The shop's opening and closing times as stated by the chatbot."
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire provided by the chatbot."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up provided by the chatbot."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat provided by the chatbot."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 12
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
  max_cost: 0.5
```

A few notes on the design choices 👇

- **`single questions`** keeps the user asking one clear thing at a time — exactly the "normal customer" behaviour you described.
- **`all_answered`** with a `limit: 12` gives the simulator enough room to cover all five goals without running away.
- The **`chatbot.output`** fields (`opening_and_closing`, `tire_price`, `tune_up_price`, `seat_price`, `bike_care_advice`) are the contract you can later check with Sensei-check rules — e.g. verifying prices are non-null or that advice is present.
- The **`context`** explicitly lists the off-limits topics so the simulator stays in scope and doesn't drift into appointments, models, warranties, etc.
