¡Hola! 😊 Happy to help you build that profile — let's keep it clean and simple, no variables, just goals. Here's a ready-to-use YAML you can drop into your `profiles/` folder:

```yaml
test_name: "subscription_services_and_benefits_inquiry"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer interested in learning about Alcampo's subscription
    services and the benefits they offer, so you can decide whether joining
    is worth it for your weekly grocery shopping.
  context:
    - "You shop regularly at Alcampo and want to save time and money."
    - "You are curious about what a subscription actually gives you beyond regular shopping."
    - "You want clear, practical details before committing to anything."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to explain the Alcampo Más subscription service and what it includes."
    - "Request the list of benefits you get as a subscriber."
    - "Inquire about the cost of the subscription and whether there are any discounts."
    - "Ask how the subscription compares to shopping without it."
    - "Request information on how to sign up for the subscription service."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Parece que tu mensaje no es claro o no es una pregunta relacionada con Alcampo. 😊 ¿En qué puedo ayudarte hoy?"
  output:
    - subscription_service_name:
        type: string
        description: "Name of the subscription service"
    - subscription_benefits:
        type: string
        description: "Benefits included in the subscription"
    - subscription_cost:
        type: money
        description: "Cost of the subscription"
    - subscription_discounts:
        type: string
        description: "Any discounts or perks tied to the subscription"
    - signup_instructions:
        type: string
        description: "Steps to sign up for the subscription"

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 20
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

A couple of quick notes so it runs smoothly 👀:

- **`money` type** — I used it in `chatbot.output` for the cost field. If your project doesn't have a `money` custom type in `types/`, swap it for `currency` (which your other profiles use) or `float`.
- **`single questions`** keeps the simulated user asking one goal at a time — nice and natural for an info-gathering chat.
- **No variables** anywhere in `goals`, exactly as you asked ✅
