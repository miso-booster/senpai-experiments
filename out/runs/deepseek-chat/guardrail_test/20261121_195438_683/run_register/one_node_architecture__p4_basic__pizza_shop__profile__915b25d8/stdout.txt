And here's the **validation checklist** your automation should enforce on any LLM-generated profile (this is the part that actually makes it "valid"):

- ✅ Every `{{variable}}` in `goals` strings has a matching declaration in `goals` (exact, case-sensitive name).
- ✅ Every declared variable has `function`, `type`, and `data`.
- ✅ `function` is one of: `default()`, `random()`, `random(n)`, `random(rand)`, `another()`, `another(n)`, `forward()`, `pairwise()`.
- ✅ `type` in `user.goals` is only `int`, `float`, `string`, or a **custom type** defined in `types/` (no `list`, `time`, `date` here).
- ✅ `type` in `chatbot.output` can be primitives, `time(...)`, `date(...)`, `list[...]`, or custom types.
- ✅ `conversation.number` is an int, or `combinations(...)` **only if** ≥2 nested `forward()` or ≥2 `pairwise()` variables exist.
- ✅ `interaction_style` entries are only from the reserved keywords (`long phrases`, `change your mind`, `change language: [...]`, `make spelling mistakes`, `single questions`, `all questions`, `default`, `random: [...]`).
- ✅ `random(n)` / `another(n)` have `n ≤ len(data)`.
- ✅ `chatbot.fallback` is the bot's real fallback text (prevents loops).
