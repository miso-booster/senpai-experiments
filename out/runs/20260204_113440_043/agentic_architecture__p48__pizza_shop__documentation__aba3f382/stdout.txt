A metamorphic rule in the context of Sensei is a type of rule that allows you to define conditions and actions based on the outputs of the chatbot. These rules are particularly useful for ensuring that the chatbot behaves consistently across different interactions and can adapt to various contexts.

### Key Features of Metamorphic Rules:
- **Dynamic Conditions**: They can evaluate the chatbot's outputs and make decisions based on them.
- **Contextual Adaptation**: They allow the chatbot to change its behavior depending on the conversation context or user inputs.
- **Validation**: Metamorphic rules can be used to validate the correctness of the chatbot's responses by checking if they meet certain criteria.

### Example:
For instance, if a chatbot is designed to provide recommendations, a metamorphic rule could check if the recommendations change based on the user's previous choices or preferences.
