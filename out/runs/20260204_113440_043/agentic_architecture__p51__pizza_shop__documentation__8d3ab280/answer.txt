In the context of SENSEI and chatbot development, an oracle is a specific type of rule that helps determine the state or value of certain variables within the chatbot's logic. Oracles are used to evaluate conditions and provide information that can influence the flow of conversation or decision-making in the chatbot.

Here are some key points about oracles:

- **Condition Evaluation**: Oracles can assess specific conditions and return values based on those evaluations. This can be used to guide the chatbot's responses or actions.

- **Integration with Rules**: Oracles often work in conjunction with other rules, allowing for complex decision-making processes. They can be referenced within `if` conditions or other logical structures.

- **Dynamic Information**: They can provide dynamic information that may change based on user input or other factors, making the chatbot more responsive and context-aware.
