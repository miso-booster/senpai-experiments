In the context of SENSEI rules, an **oracle** is a validation condition used to determine whether a specific condition is true or false during the evaluation of a chatbot conversation. It serves as a way to check if the chatbot's responses or behaviors meet certain criteria defined in the rules.

### Key Points about Oracles:
- **Boolean Expression**: An oracle typically evaluates to a boolean value (true or false).
- **Validation Logic**: It can be used to validate various aspects of a conversation, such as ensuring that the chatbot's responses are relevant, accurate, or follow specific guidelines.
- **Integration with Rules**: Oracles are often part of the rules defined in YAML, allowing for automated checks against the outputs generated during chatbot interactions.
