Sure! Here's a simple profile that asks the chatbot for items to add to the user's shopping cart. You can customize it further based on your specific needs. Just copy and paste this into a new YAML file under your `profiles/` directory. 😊

```yaml
test_name: "Shopping Cart Addition"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking to add items to their shopping cart."
  context:
    - "You are planning your weekly grocery shopping and want to ensure you have all the necessary items."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot for recommendations on items to add to your shopping cart."
    - "Request specific categories of items, such as fruits, vegetables, or snacks."
    - "Inquire about any current promotions on items that can be added to your cart."
    - "Ask for suggestions on popular items among other shoppers."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. What items would you like to add to your cart? 😊"
  output:
    - recommended_items:
        type: string
        description: "List of recommended items to add to the shopping cart."
    - promotional_items:
        type: string
        description: "Items currently on promotion that can be added to the cart."
    - popular_items:
        type: string
        description: "Popular items among other shoppers."

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    steps: 10
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

Feel free to adjust the `goals`, `output`, or any other sections to better fit your requirements! Happy testing! 🛒✨
