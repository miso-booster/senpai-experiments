That sounds like a great profile to create! Here's a YAML profile that captures all your requirements for testing the bike shop's virtual assistant. It includes the user's goals, context, and interaction style to ensure a smooth and focused conversation. 😊

```yaml
test_name: "Bike Shop Virtual Assistant Inquiry"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer seeking general information about the bike shop and its services."
  context:
    - "You are looking for basic information about the shop's services and pricing."
    - "You want to gather details before visiting the shop."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much is a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you give me general advice on how to take care of my bike?"
  
conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 5
  interaction_style:
    - default

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, could you repeat that?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing: 
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
        type: string
    - tire_price: 
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
        type: money
    - tune_up_price: 
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
        type: money
    - seat_price: 
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
        type: money
    - maintenance_advice: 
        description: "General advice on how to properly take care of a bike."
        type: string
```

This profile is structured to ensure that the user interacts clearly and directly, focusing on the essential information about the bike shop. It also specifies the expected outputs from the chatbot, making it easier to validate the responses. Happy testing! 🚴‍♂️
