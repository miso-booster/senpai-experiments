```yaml
llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: "gpt-4"
  model_prov: "openai"
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer interested in learning about subscription services and their benefits."

    - "The user is considering various subscription options and wants to understand the advantages."

    - "Inquire about the different subscription plans available."
    - "Ask for details on the benefits associated with each plan."
    - "Request information on any promotional offers or discounts."
    - "Seek clarification on the cancellation policy for subscriptions."

  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, could you please provide more details?"

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - random:
      - single questions
      - long phrases
```

### Summary of the Profile:
- **Test Name**: Identifies the profile.
- **LLM Settings**: Specifies the model and temperature for the language model.
- **User Role**: Describes the simulated user as a customer interested in subscriptions.
- **Goals**: Lists the inquiries the user will make regarding subscription services.
- **Chatbot Settings**: Indicates that the chatbot starts the conversation and includes a fallback message.
- **Conversation Controls**: Defines the number of conversations and interaction styles.
