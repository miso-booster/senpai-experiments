A metamorphic rule in Sensei is a condition that you define to check the correctness of conversations generated by the Sensei-chat. These rules help ensure that the outputs of your chatbot behave as expected when certain inputs change. 

Here's a quick breakdown:

1. **Single vs. Multiple Conversations**: Metamorphic rules can target a single conversation (like traditional checks) or compare multiple conversations to assert how outputs should change (or remain the same) when inputs are altered.

2. **Relations Over Absolute Answers**: Instead of checking for specific answers, metamorphic rules focus on the relationships between outputs of different conversations. This approach helps alleviate the "oracle problem," where it's challenging to determine the correct answer outright.

3. **Structure**: When you write a metamorphic rule, you typically specify:
   - How many conversations it needs to evaluate.
   - A filter (`when`) to select relevant conversations.
   - The relationship to check (`if`/`then` conditions).

For example, you might have a rule that states if two pizzas have the same size and drink, then the one with more toppings should cost more.

This way, metamorphic rules help you validate the conversational logic of your chatbot across various scenarios! 😊
