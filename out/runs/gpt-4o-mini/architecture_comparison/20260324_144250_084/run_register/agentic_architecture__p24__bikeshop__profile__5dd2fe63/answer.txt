Here's the YAML profile for the bike shop's virtual assistant:

```yaml
test_name: "Bike Shop Virtual Assistant Test"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: "gpt-4"
  model_prov: "openai"
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking for general information about the bike shop and its services."
  context:
    - "The user is interested in basic services and pricing."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much for a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you give me general advice on how to take care of a bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't understand that. Can you please rephrase?"
  output:
    - opening_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - tire_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice on bike maintenance."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### Summary of the Profile
- **Test Name**: "Bike Shop Virtual Assistant Test"
- **User Role**: Simulates a customer asking about shop hours, pricing, and bike care.
- **Chatbot Output**: Extracts relevant information such as prices and advice.
- **Conversation Control**: Designed for a single interaction with a clear structure.

This profile is set up to test the chatbot's ability to provide accurate and relevant information while adhering to the specified constraints. If you need any adjustments or additional features, just let me know! 😊
