```yaml
test_name: "Shopping Cart Addition"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking to add items to their shopping cart."
  context:
    - "You want to ensure you have everything you need for your upcoming grocery shopping."
    - "You're interested in both fresh produce and pantry staples."
  goals:
    - "Add {{item}} to my shopping cart."
    - "What other items do you recommend for a balanced grocery list?"
    - "Can you suggest some snacks to add to my cart?"
    - "Please add {{quantity}} of {{item}} to my shopping cart."
    - item:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [apples, bananas, bread, milk, eggs, chicken, rice, pasta, yogurt, chips]
    - quantity:
        function: random(3)
        type: int
        data: [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm here to help you with your shopping! What would you like to add to your cart?"
  output:
    - added_items:
        type: string
        description: "List of items added to the shopping cart."

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    steps: 10
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### Explanation:
- **test_name**: This is the name of your profile.
- **llm**: Specifies the model and temperature for generating responses.
- **user**: Defines the simulated user’s role, context, and goals.
- **goals**: Lists the actions the user wants to perform, including adding items to the cart and asking for recommendations.
- **chatbot**: Contains the chatbot's fallback message and the output structure.
- **conversation**: Sets the number of interactions and how the goals will be structured.

Feel free to tweak the items and quantities as needed! Happy shopping! 🛒😊
