Sure thing! Here’s a profile that captures your requirements for testing a bike shop’s virtual assistant. It focuses on a customer seeking general information about the shop and its standard services, phrasing questions clearly and directly. Let’s dive in! 🚴‍♂️

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_information"

llm:
  temperature: 0.5
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking for general information about the bike shop and its standard services."
  context:
    - "You are interested in understanding the basic services offered by the bike shop."
    - "You want to gather information about pricing and operating hours."
    - "You are not looking for appointments, specific bike models, or advanced repairs."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much is a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you give me some general advice on how to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing: 
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - tire_price: 
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price: 
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price: 
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
    - bike_care_advice: 
        type: string
        description: "General advice on how to properly take care of a bike."

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 5
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### Breakdown of the Profile:
- **Test Name**: Clearly identifies the purpose of the profile.
- **User Role**: Describes the simulated user as a customer seeking basic information.
- **Context**: Sets the scene for the user’s intentions and limitations on topics.
- **Goals**: Lists specific questions the user will ask, ensuring clarity and directness.
- **Chatbot Output**: Defines the expected responses from the chatbot, ensuring it covers all necessary information.
- **Conversation Controls**: Limits the number of interactions to keep it focused and relevant.
