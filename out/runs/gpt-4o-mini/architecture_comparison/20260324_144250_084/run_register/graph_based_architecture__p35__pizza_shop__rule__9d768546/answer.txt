Sure! Here’s a simple rule you can use to check that the price of a small pizza is at least $10. You can add this rule to your Sensei project:

```yaml
name: small_pizza_price_check
description: Small pizzas should cost at least $10
conversations: 1
when: size == 'small'
oracle: extract_float(price) >= 10 and currency(price) == 'USD'
```

This rule checks if the size of the pizza is 'small' and ensures that the price is at least $10 in USD. If you need any adjustments or further assistance, feel free to ask! 😊
