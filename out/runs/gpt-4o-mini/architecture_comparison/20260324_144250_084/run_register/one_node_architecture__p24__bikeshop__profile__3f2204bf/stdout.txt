That sounds like a great profile to create! Below is a YAML profile that captures all your requirements for testing the bike shop’s virtual assistant. It defines the user’s context, goals, and the expected output from the chatbot.

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_virtual_assistant"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer seeking general information about the bike shop and its services."
  context:
    - "You are looking for basic information about the bike shop's services."
    - "You want to know about pricing and shop hours."
    - "You are not interested in advanced repairs, specific bike models, or appointments."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much is a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you give me some general advice on how to properly take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing_time:
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
    - maintenance_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice on how to properly take care of a bike."

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 5
  interaction_style:
    - single question
```

This profile sets up a clear and direct interaction for the user, focusing on the essential information while ensuring the chatbot stays on topic. Feel free to tweak any details to better fit your specific testing needs! Happy testing! 🚴‍♂️😊
