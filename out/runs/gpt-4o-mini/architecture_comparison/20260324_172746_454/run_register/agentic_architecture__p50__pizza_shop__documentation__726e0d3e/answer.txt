In the context of SENSEI rules, an "oracle" is a validation condition that evaluates to a boolean value (true or false). It is used to determine whether a specific condition has been met during the validation of conversations. 

Here's a breakdown of how oracles work:

- **Purpose**: Oracles are used to assert specific expectations about the chatbot's responses or the conversation flow. They help in validating whether the chatbot behaves as intended.

- **Structure**: An oracle typically consists of a condition that checks certain outputs or properties of a conversation. For example, it might check if a specific field in the chatbot's output contains a certain value.

- **Usage**: In a rule, you might see an oracle used to validate that the chatbot's response includes a required piece of information or adheres to a specific format.

In summary, oracles are essential for ensuring that the chatbot's performance aligns with the defined expectations in your rules. If you need more specific examples or details about how to implement oracles in your rules, feel free to ask! 😊
