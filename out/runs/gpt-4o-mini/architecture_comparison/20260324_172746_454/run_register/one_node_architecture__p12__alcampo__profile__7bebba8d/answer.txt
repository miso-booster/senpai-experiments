Sure! Here's a simple profile that asks the chatbot for items to add to the user's shopping cart. You can customize it further based on your specific needs.

```yaml
test_name: "Shopping Cart Inquiry"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "You are a shopper looking to add items to your shopping cart."
  context:
    - "You want to find various products available for purchase."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot for recommendations on items to add to your shopping cart."
    - "Inquire about the availability of specific products."
    - "Request information on current promotions related to the items you want to add."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm here to help! What items would you like to add to your shopping cart?"
  output:
    - product_recommendations:
        type: string
        description: "List of recommended items to add to the shopping cart."
    - product_availability:
        type: string
        description: "Information on the availability of requested products."
    - current_promotions:
        type: string
        description: "Details about current promotions related to the items."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

Feel free to adjust the goals, context, or any other parts to better fit your project! If you need more help, just let me know! 😊
