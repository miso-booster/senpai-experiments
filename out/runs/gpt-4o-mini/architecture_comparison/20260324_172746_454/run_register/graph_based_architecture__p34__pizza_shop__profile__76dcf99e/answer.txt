That sounds like a fun scenario to test! 😊 You can create a profile that incorporates the interaction style of changing opinions. Here’s a sample profile structure you can use:

```yaml
test_name: "custom_to_predefined_pizza_order"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer who initially wants to order a custom pizza but changes their mind halfway through."
  context:
    - "You are planning a pizza night and want to explore options."
    - "You start by asking for a custom pizza but then decide to go for a predefined pizza."
  goals:
    - "I would like to order a custom pizza with {{custom_toppings}}."
    - "Actually, can I get a {{predefined_pizza}} instead?"
    - custom_toppings:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [pepperoni, mushrooms, olives, bell peppers, onions]
    - predefined_pizza:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [Margherita, Pepperoni, Hawaiian, BBQ Chicken]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Can you repeat your order?"
  output:
    - order_confirmation:
        type: string
        description: "Confirmation of the pizza order, whether it's custom or predefined."
    - total_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The total price of the order."

conversation:
  number: 2
  goal_style:
    steps: 3
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
    - long phrases
```

### Explanation:
- **User Role & Context**: The user starts with the intention of ordering a custom pizza but changes their mind during the conversation.
- **Goals**: The first goal is to ask for a custom pizza, and the second goal is to switch to a predefined pizza.
- **Interaction Style**: The `change your mind` style allows the user to change their request midway through the conversation.
- **Outputs**: You can extract the order confirmation and total price at the end.

Feel free to adjust any details to better fit your testing needs! Happy testing! 🍕✨
