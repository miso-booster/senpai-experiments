### Steps for Conducting a Coverage Audit

1. **List All Profiles and Their Outputs**:
   - Go through each profile in your `profiles/` directory and document the `chatbot.output` fields. This will give you a clear view of what each profile is designed to extract.

2. **List All Rules**:
   - Review each rule in your `rules/` directory. Document the conditions (`when`, `if`, `then`, `oracle`) and the specific outputs they validate against.

3. **Map Rules to Profile Outputs**:
   - For each rule, identify which profile outputs it references. You can do this by looking for `conv[i].<field_name>` in the rules and matching them with the outputs defined in the profiles.

4. **Identify Gaps**:
   - After mapping, check if there are any outputs from the profiles that do not have corresponding rules validating them. This will help you identify gaps in your testing coverage.

5. **Review for Completeness**:
   - Ensure that all critical functionalities of your chatbot are covered by rules. This includes checking for edge cases, error handling, and any business logic that should be validated.
