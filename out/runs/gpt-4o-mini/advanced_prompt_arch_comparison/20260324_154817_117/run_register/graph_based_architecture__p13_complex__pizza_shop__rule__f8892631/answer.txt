After mapping, look for:
- **Unvalidated Outputs:** Any output fields in profiles that don’t have corresponding rules validating them.
- **Redundant Rules:** Rules that validate outputs that are not relevant to the profiles.
- **Missing Tests:** Consider if there are important conversational aspects or outputs that are not being tested at all.
