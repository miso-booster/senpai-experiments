Sure! Here’s a simple profile that asks the chatbot for items to add to the user's shopping cart. You can customize it further based on your specific needs. Just copy this YAML structure into a new file in your `profiles/` directory. 

```yaml
test_name: "Shopping Cart Addition"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking to add items to their shopping cart."
  context:
    - "You want to ensure you have all the necessary items for your upcoming meals."
    - "You're interested in both fresh produce and pantry staples."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot for suggestions on items to add to my shopping cart."
    - "Inquire about seasonal fruits available for purchase."
    - "Request recommendations for pantry staples like grains and canned goods."
    - "Ask for any current promotions on grocery items."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm here to help you find the perfect items for your shopping cart! What are you looking for?"
  output:
    - suggested_items:
        type: list[string]
        description: "List of suggested items to add to the shopping cart"
    - seasonal_fruits:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Available seasonal fruits for purchase"
    - pantry_staples:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Recommendations for pantry staples"
    - current_promotions:
        type: string
        description: "Details of any current promotions on grocery items"

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    steps: 10
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

Feel free to modify the `goals` and `output` sections to better fit your requirements! Happy testing! 😊
