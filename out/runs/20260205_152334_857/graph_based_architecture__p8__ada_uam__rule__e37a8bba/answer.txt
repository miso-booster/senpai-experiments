### Issues
1. **Redundancy**: The `when` condition and the `oracle` are essentially checking the same thing. This is not necessary because the `oracle` should define what you expect to happen, while the `when` condition should filter the conversations based on specific criteria.
2. **Structure**: The `oracle` should typically be a condition that checks the expected output, not just a repetition of the `when` condition.

```yaml
name: check_cau_information
description: Checks that the CAU provided correct information about contact details.
conversations: 1

```

### Explanation
- **`when`**: This part checks if the conversation meets the specified conditions (the correct email, phone number, and physical address).
- **`oracle`**: This part should ideally check the expected outcome of the conversation, like confirming that the CAU provided the correct information. However, in this case, it is still valid because you want to ensure that the information matches what you expect.
