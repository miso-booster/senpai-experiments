Great question! 😊 In the context of Sensei, a **metamorphic rule** is a specific type of rule used in **Sensei-check** to verify the correctness of conversational outputs across multiple generated conversations.

Here's a simple breakdown of what it entails:

1. **Definition**: A metamorphic rule states a condition that checks how outputs should change (or not) when inputs change. Instead of just checking if a single conversation is correct (like a traditional oracle), it compares pairs or tuples of conversations.

2. **Purpose**: The main goal is to alleviate the oracle problem, which is the challenge of determining the correctness of outputs. By focusing on relationships between conversations, metamorphic rules can assert how outputs should behave in relation to each other.

3. **Structure**: When you write a metamorphic rule, you typically specify:
   - How many conversations it needs (usually two or more).
   - An optional filter to select eligible conversations.
   - The relation to check, which can be expressed in an `if/then` format or as a simple boolean condition.

For example, you might have a rule that states: "If two pizzas have the same size and drink, the one with more toppings must cost more." This checks the relationship between the outputs of two conversations based on their inputs.

Overall, metamorphic rules help ensure that the chatbot behaves consistently and correctly across different scenarios! If you have more questions about how to create or use them, feel free to ask! 😊
