### Profile Comparison

#### 1. **Pizza and Drinks Information Seeker**
- **Test Name:** `pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker`
- **Role:** A customer seeking information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours.
- **Goals:**
  - Ask about available pizza types.
  - Ask about the price of a Margarita pizza.
  - Ask about available drinks and their prices.
  - Ask about the shop's operating hours.
  - Ask if they deliver.
- **Outputs:**
  - `pizza_types`, `margarita_price`, `drinks_and_prices`, `operating_hours`, `delivery_availability`.

#### 2. **Pizza Order Test Custom**
- **Test Name:** `pizza_order_test_custom`
- **Role:** User ordering a pizza to a pizza shop.
- **Goals:**
  - Order a custom pizza with size and toppings.
  - Order cans of a drink.
  - Inquire about opening and closing times.
  - Ask about the total cost.
- **Outputs:**
  - `price`, `time`, `order_id`.

### Conclusion
The **Pizza and Drinks Information Seeker** profile is better suited for a user who wants information about drinks, prices, and opening hours. It explicitly covers all the necessary queries related to drinks and prices, while the **Pizza Order Test Custom** profile focuses more on placing an order rather than gathering information.
