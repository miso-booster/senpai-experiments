A metamorphic rule in the context of SENSEI refers to a type of rule that is used to verify the consistency and correctness of chatbot outputs across different contexts or inputs. These rules typically involve conditions that check how the chatbot's responses change based on varying inputs, ensuring that the chatbot behaves as expected in different scenarios.

### Key Characteristics of Metamorphic Rules:
- **Context Sensitivity**: They assess how the output changes when the input changes, focusing on the relationship between different inputs and their corresponding outputs.
- **Validation of Consistency**: They help in validating that the chatbot's behavior remains consistent and logical across various situations.
- **Use of Conditions**: Metamorphic rules often involve `if-then` conditions that dictate how the chatbot should respond based on specific input variations.

### Example:
For instance, if a chatbot is designed to provide weather information, a metamorphic rule might check that if the input changes from "What's the weather like in New York?" to "What's the weather like in Los Angeles?", the output should reflect the respective weather conditions for each city.
