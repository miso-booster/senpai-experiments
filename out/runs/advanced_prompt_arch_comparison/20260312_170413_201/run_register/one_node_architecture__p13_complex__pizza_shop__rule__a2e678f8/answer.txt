### 1. **List All Profiles and Their Outputs**
   - Go through each profile in your `profiles/` directory.
   - Document the `chatbot.output` fields for each profile. This will help you understand what data you expect to validate.

### 2. **List All Rules**
   - Review each rule in your `rules/` directory.
   - Document the conditions and the outputs they validate.

### 3. **Map Rules to Profile Outputs**
   - For each rule, identify which profile outputs it references. 
   - Create a mapping table that shows which rules validate which outputs. This will help you visualize coverage.

### 4. **Identify Gaps**
   - Look for outputs in your profiles that do not have corresponding rules validating them.
   - Consider the business logic and requirements to ensure that all critical outputs are covered by rules.

### 5. **Review Rule Effectiveness**
   - Check if the rules are active and properly structured. Inactive rules will not contribute to your testing.
   - Ensure that the rules are meaningful and test relevant invariants or conditions based on your profile outputs.

### 6. **Consider Additional Tests**
   - Based on the gaps identified, think about what additional rules might be necessary. 
   - Consider edge cases or specific scenarios that might not be covered by existing rules.
