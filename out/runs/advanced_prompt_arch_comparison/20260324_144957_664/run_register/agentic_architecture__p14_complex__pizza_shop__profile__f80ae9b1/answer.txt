### Profile Comparison

#### 1. **pizza_order_test_custom**
- **Purpose**: Simulates a user ordering a pizza, including drinks and asking for opening times and prices.
- **Goals**:
  - Order a custom pizza with specific toppings and size.
  - Order a specified number of cans of drinks.
  - Inquire about opening and closing times.
  - Ask for the total cost.
- **Outputs**:
  - Price of the order.
  - Opening time.
  - Order ID.

#### 2. **pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker**
- **Purpose**: A customer seeking information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours before placing an order.
- **Goals**:
  - Ask about available pizza types.
  - Inquire about the price of a Margarita pizza.
  - Ask about available drinks and their prices.
  - Ask about the shop's operating hours.
  - Confirm delivery availability.
- **Outputs**:
  - List of pizza types.
  - Price of a Margarita pizza.
  - List of drinks and their prices.
  - Shop's operating hours.
  - Delivery availability.

### **Better Profile**
Given the user's intent to gather information about drinks, prices, and opening hours, **pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker** is the better profile. It directly addresses the user's needs for information without the assumption of placing an order.
