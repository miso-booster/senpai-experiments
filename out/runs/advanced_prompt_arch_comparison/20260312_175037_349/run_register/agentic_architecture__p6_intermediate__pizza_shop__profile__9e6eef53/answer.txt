### Candidate Profiles

1. **Profile: `pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker.yaml`**
   - **Purpose**: A customer seeking information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours.
   - **Outputs**:
     - `pizza_types`: A list of the pizza types offered by the shop.
     - `margarita_price`: The price of a Margarita pizza.
     - `drinks_and_prices`: A list of available drinks and their corresponding prices.
     - `operating_hours`: The shop's operating hours.
     - `delivery_availability`: Indication of whether the shop delivers or not.

2. **Profile: `pizza_and_drink_order_placement.yaml`**
   - **Purpose**: A customer placing a pizza and drink order for pickup.
   - **Outputs**:
     - `pizza_options`: The list of available pizza types.
     - `drink_options`: The list of available drinks.
     - `order_summary`: A comprehensive summary of the pizza and drink order.
     - `total_cost`: The total cost of the order.
     - `estimated_readiness_time`: The estimated time until the order is ready for pickup.
     - `pickup_location`: The address or location for pickup.
     - `order_id`: A unique identifier for the order.

### Comparison of Outputs

- **Common Elements**:
  - Both profiles address **pizza options** and **drink options**.
  
- **Unique Elements**:
  - The first profile focuses on **information gathering** (prices, operating hours, delivery availability).
  - The second profile is more about **order placement** and includes additional outputs like **total cost** and **estimated readiness time**.
