### Steps for a Coverage Audit

1. **List All Profiles and Their Outputs**:
   - Review each profile in your `profiles/` directory.
   - Note down the `chatbot.output` fields defined in each profile. This will help you understand what outputs are available for validation.

2. **List All Rules**:
   - Go through the rules in your `rules/` directory.
   - Identify the `when`, `if`, `then`, and `oracle` conditions in each rule.

3. **Map Rules to Profile Outputs**:
   - For each rule, check if it references any fields from the `chatbot.output` of your profiles.
   - Create a mapping table that shows which rules validate which outputs. 

4. **Identify Gaps**:
   - Look for outputs in your profiles that do not have corresponding rules validating them.
   - Consider the business requirements or invariants you want to test. Are there any important aspects of the conversation or outputs that are not currently covered by your rules?

### Common Gaps to Look For

- **Output Completeness**: Ensure all fields in `chatbot.output` have corresponding rules.
- **Business Logic**: Check if there are business rules or invariants that should be validated but are missing.
- **Error Handling**: Consider adding rules to validate how the chatbot handles invalid inputs or unexpected queries.
- **Performance Metrics**: If applicable, rules that check response times or conversation lengths can be valuable.

### Next Steps

Once you've mapped everything out, you can prioritize creating new rules for any uncovered outputs or business logic. If you need help drafting specific rules or understanding how to validate certain outputs, feel free to ask! 😊
