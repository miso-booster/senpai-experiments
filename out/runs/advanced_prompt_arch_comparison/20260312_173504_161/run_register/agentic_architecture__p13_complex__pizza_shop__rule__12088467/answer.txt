### Rules Overview

1. **conversation_lengths.yaml**
   - Validates: General conversation length but does not map to specific profile outputs.

2. **more_toppings_cost_more.yaml**
   - Validates: Ensures that more toppings cost more.
   - Related Profiles: Primarily relevant to **pizza_and_drink_order_placement.yaml** (order summary) and **pizzaorder_ivan_test.yml** (price).

3. **only_talks_about_pizzas.yaml**
   - Validates: Ensures the chatbot only discusses pizza-related topics.
   - Related Profiles: All profiles that deal with pizza orders.

4. **ordering_conversation_turns.yaml**
   - Validates: Checks the order of conversation turns but is currently inactive.
   - Related Profiles: Primarily relevant to **pizza_and_drink_order_placement.yaml** and **pizzaorder_ivan_test.yml**.

5. **ordering_conversation_turns_assistant.yaml**
   - Validates: Similar to the previous rule but focuses on the assistant's confirmation.
   - Related Profiles: Primarily relevant to **pizza_and_drink_order_placement.yaml**.

6. **same_number_of_toppings_cost_the_same.yaml**
   - Validates: Ensures pizzas with the same number of toppings cost the same but is currently inactive.
   - Related Profiles: Primarily relevant to **pizza_and_drink_order_placement.yaml** and **pizzaorder_ivan_test.yml**.

### Gaps Identified

1. **General Information Coverage**: 
   - The **general_information_seeker** profile outputs are not directly validated by any rules. This is a gap, as it does not ensure that the chatbot provides accurate information about general inquiries.

2. **Delivery Availability**: 
   - The **delivery_availability** output from **pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker.yaml** is not validated by any rules.

3. **Order Confirmation**: 
   - While there are rules related to ordering conversation turns, they are currently inactive. Activating and refining these rules could improve the validation of order confirmation processes.

4. **Custom Pizza Validation**: 
   - The **custom_pizza_response** output from **user_experiencing_input_issues.yaml** is not covered by any rules, which could lead to unverified responses regarding custom orders.

5. **Price Validation**: 
   - There are rules for validating price based on toppings, but there is no rule that checks if the total cost aligns with the expected price based on the selected items across all profiles.

### Recommendations

- **Create New Rules**: Develop rules to validate the outputs of the **general_information_seeker** and **pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker** profiles.
- **Activate Inactive Rules**: Review and activate the inactive rules related to conversation turns and order confirmations to ensure they are functioning correctly.
- **Enhance Custom Pizza Validation**: Introduce rules that specifically validate the responses related to custom pizzas and their pricing.
- **Cross-Profile Validation**: Consider creating rules that validate price consistency across different profiles to ensure that the pricing logic is consistent.
