### Best Profile for User Inquiry

The best profile for a user asking about drinks, prices, and opening hours is **`pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker.yaml`**. Here’s a summary of its key features:

- **Role**: A customer seeking information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours.
- **Goals**:
  - Ask about available pizza types.
  - Ask about the price of a Margarita pizza.
  - Ask about available drinks and their prices.
  - Ask about the shop's operating hours.
  - Ask if they deliver.
- **Outputs**:
  - `pizza_types`: A list of pizza types offered.
  - `margarita_price`: The price of a Margarita pizza.
  - `drinks_and_prices`: A list of available drinks and their prices.
  - `operating_hours`: The shop's operating hours.
  - `delivery_availability`: Whether the shop delivers.
