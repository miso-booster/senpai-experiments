Here’s a step-by-step process you can follow:

### 1. **List All Profiles and Their Outputs**
   - Review each profile in your project and note down the `chatbot.output` fields. This will give you a clear view of what each profile is designed to extract.

### 2. **List All Rules**
   - Go through your rules and identify what each rule is checking. Pay special attention to the `when`, `if`, and `then` statements to see what outputs they reference.

### 3. **Map Rules to Profile Outputs**
   - Create a mapping table where you can list each profile and the corresponding rules that validate its outputs. This can be done in a spreadsheet or a simple document.
   - For example:
     ```
     | Profile Name                          | Rule Name                          | Output Field(s) Validated          |
     |---------------------------------------|------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
     | pizza_and_drink_order_placement      | more_toppings_cost_more            | price                              |
     | pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker   | only_talks_about_pizzas            | pizza_types                        |
     ```

### 4. **Identify Gaps**
   - Look for any outputs from your profiles that do not have corresponding rules. This will help you identify areas that may require additional testing.
   - Consider the following questions:
     - Are there any important outputs that are not being validated?
     - Are there any rules that are not linked to any profile outputs?

### 5. **Review Rule Effectiveness**
   - Ensure that the rules you have are effectively testing the invariants you care about. For example, if you have rules checking for price monotonicity, make sure they cover all relevant scenarios.

### 6. **Consider Edge Cases**
   - Think about edge cases or scenarios that might not be covered by your existing rules. This could include:
     - Invalid inputs
     - Unexpected user behavior
     - Variations in language or phrasing

### 7. **Document Findings**
   - Once you have completed your mapping and identified gaps, document your findings clearly. This will help you prioritize which rules to create or modify.

### 8. **Plan for Updates**
   - Based on your findings, create a plan for updating your profiles and rules to ensure comprehensive coverage.
