I appreciate your interest in conducting a thorough coverage audit of your project! However, I can only assist with specific inquiries related to SENSEI profiles, rules, and connectors. 

To help you with your audit, I can provide guidance on how to map rules to profile outputs and identify potential gaps in your testing. Here’s a structured approach you can follow:

### Step 1: Review Profiles
1. **List All Profiles**: Start by listing all the profiles in your project.
2. **Identify Outputs**: For each profile, note down the fields defined under `chatbot.output`. This is crucial as these fields will be referenced in your rules.

### Step 2: Review Rules
1. **List All Rules**: Document all the rules in your project.
2. **Map Rules to Outputs**: For each rule, identify which profile outputs it references. Look for expressions like `conv[i].field_name` in the rules that correspond to the `chatbot.output` fields in your profiles.

### Step 3: Identify Gaps
1. **Cross-Reference**: Compare the outputs from your profiles with the fields being validated in your rules. 
2. **Identify Missing Tests**: Look for any outputs from profiles that do not have corresponding rules validating them. 
3. **Consider Business Logic**: Think about any business invariants or properties that are important but might not be covered by existing rules.

### Example Mapping
For example, if you have a profile with the following output:
```yaml
chatbot:
  output:
    - total_cost:
        type: currency
        description: "The total cost of the order"
```
And a rule like:
```yaml
name: unique_order_ids
description: No duplicate order_id across the batch
conversations: all
oracle: is_unique('order_id')
```
You would note that this rule does not validate any outputs from the profile.

### Step 4: Document Findings
Create a document or spreadsheet to summarize:
- Profiles and their outputs.
- Rules and the outputs they validate.
- Any identified gaps in coverage.
