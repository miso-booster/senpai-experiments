### Non-1millionbot Connector

2. **Postman Echo**
   - **Base URL:** `https://postman-echo.com`
   - **Send Message Endpoint:**
     - **Path:** `/post`
     - **Method:** `POST`
     - **Payload Template:**
       ```json
       {
         "message": "{user_msg}"
       }
       ```
   - **Response Path:** `json.message`

### Comparison of Differences

- **Base URL:**
  - **Ada UAM:** `https://api.1millionbot.com`
  - **Postman Echo:** `https://postman-echo.com`

- **Send Message Endpoint Path:**
  - **Ada UAM:** `/api/public/messages`
  - **Postman Echo:** `/post`

- **Headers:**
  - **Ada UAM:** Requires specific headers including `Content-Type` and `Authorization`.
  - **Postman Echo:** Does not require additional headers.

- **Payload Structure:**
  - **Ada UAM:** More complex payload including fields like `conversation`, `sender_type`, `sender`, `bot`, `language`, and a URL.
  - **Postman Echo:** Simpler payload with just a `message` field.

- **Response Path:**
  - **Ada UAM:** `response.0.text`
  - **Postman Echo:** `json.message`

### Summary
The **1millionbot** connector (Ada UAM) has a more complex structure with specific headers and a detailed payload template, while the non-1millionbot connector (Postman Echo) is simpler, requiring fewer headers and a straightforward payload.
