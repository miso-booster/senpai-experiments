### Profile Comparison

Let's compare the two profiles: **pizza_order_test_custom** and **pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker**.

#### 1. **pizza_order_test_custom**
- **Role**: User ordering a pizza.
- **Goals**:
  - Order a custom pizza with size and toppings.
  - Order drinks.
  - Inquire about opening and closing times.
  - Ask about the cost.
- **Outputs**:
  - Price of the order.
  - Opening time.
  - Order ID.

#### 2. **pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker**
- **Role**: Customer seeking information before placing an order.
- **Goals**:
  - Ask about available pizza types.
  - Inquire about the price of a Margarita pizza.
  - Ask about available drinks and their prices.
  - Ask about shop operating hours.
  - Ask about delivery options.
- **Outputs**:
  - List of pizza types.
  - Price of a Margarita pizza.
  - List of drinks and their prices.
  - Operating hours.
  - Delivery availability.

### Conclusion
For a user who wants information about drinks, prices, and opening hours, **pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker** is the better profile. It is specifically designed to gather information rather than place an order, making it more suitable for the user's needs.
