Here's a simple Sensei profile focused on asking about subscription services and their benefits, without variables—just fixed goals:

```yaml
test_name: "Subscription Services Inquiry"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "Customer interested in learning about subscription services and their benefits."
  context:
    - "You want clear and detailed information about subscription options."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to explain the available subscription services."
    - "Inquire about the benefits of subscribing."
    - "Request details on how to subscribe."
    - "Ask about any special offers or promotions related to subscriptions."
    - "Find out about cancellation policies and subscription management."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, I didn't understand your question about subscriptions. Could you please rephrase?"
  output:
    - subscription_service_name:
        type: string
        description: "Name of the subscription service"
    - subscription_benefits:
        type: string
        description: "Benefits of the subscription service"
    - subscription_offers:
        type: string
        description: "Current offers or promotions"
    - subscription_cancellation_policy:
        type: string
        description: "Information on cancellation and management"

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 15
```
