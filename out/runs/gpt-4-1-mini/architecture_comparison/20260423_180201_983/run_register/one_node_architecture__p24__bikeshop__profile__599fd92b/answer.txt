Here's a Sensei profile YAML for your bike shop virtual assistant test, matching your detailed requirements. The user speaks English, asks clearly about shop hours, prices for tire, tune-up, seat, and general bike care advice, while avoiding unrelated topics.

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_info"

llm:
  temperature: 0.5
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a normal customer visiting a bike shop's virtual assistant.
    Ask clear and direct questions about the shop's opening and closing times,
    the price of a new bike tire, the price of a standard bike tune-up, and the price of a new bike seat.
    Also ask for general advice on basic bike maintenance and good care practices.
    Do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, advanced repairs, warranties, delivery,
    or any topic outside basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care.
  context:
    - "You want straightforward, accurate information using appropriate bike-shop terminology."
    - "You avoid unsolicited suggestions or unrelated services."
  goals:
    - "What are your opening and closing times?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price for a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much do you charge for a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you give me some general advice on how to take care of my bike?"
  language: English

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you please rephrase?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: "The bike shop's opening and closing times."
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "Price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: money
        description: "Price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "Price of a new bike seat."
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: "General advice on basic bike maintenance and care."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 10
  interaction_style:
    - default
```
