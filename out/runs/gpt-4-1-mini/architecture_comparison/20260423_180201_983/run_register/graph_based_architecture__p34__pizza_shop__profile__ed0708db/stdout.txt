Hey! That sounds like a great use case to test how your chatbot handles users who change their minds mid-conversation 😊.

You want a Sensei profile where the user starts by asking for a custom pizza but then switches to ordering a predefined pizza. The key here is to use the `interaction_style` with `"change your mind"` so the user simulator will naturally change their request during the chat.

Here’s a minimal example profile YAML you can adapt, based on your pizza ordering context:

```yaml
test_name: "custom_to_predefined_pizza_change_mind"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer who initially wants to order a custom pizza with specific toppings,
    but halfway through the conversation, you change your mind and decide to order a predefined pizza instead.
  context:
    - "You like experimenting with pizza toppings but sometimes prefer simpler options."
  goals:
    - "I want a custom pizza with {{toppings}}."
    - "Actually, I'd like to order a {{predefined_pizza}} pizza instead."
    - toppings:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [cheese, mushrooms, pepperoni]
    - predefined_pizza:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [Margherita, Pepperoni, Hawaiian, Vegetarian]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you please repeat?"
  output:
    - final_order:
        type: string
        description: "The chatbot's confirmation of the final pizza order."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 6
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
```

### Explanation:
- The user starts with a goal to order a custom pizza with some toppings.
- Then the user changes their mind and orders a predefined pizza.
- The `interaction_style: change your mind` ensures the user simulator will switch goals mid-conversation.
- The `goals` list includes both requests with variables (`toppings` and `predefined_pizza`) declared properly.
- The `chatbot.output.final_order` field is where you expect the chatbot to confirm the final pizza choice.
