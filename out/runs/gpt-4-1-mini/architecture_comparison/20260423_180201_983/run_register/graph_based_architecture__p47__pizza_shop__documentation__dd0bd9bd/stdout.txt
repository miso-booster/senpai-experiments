Hey! Great question 😊 In the context of Sensei, a **metamorphic rule** is a special kind of test rule used in **Sensei-check** to verify chatbot behavior across multiple conversations, not just one.

Here’s the scoop:

- Instead of checking if a single chatbot response is exactly right (which can be tricky), metamorphic rules check **relationships between multiple conversations** generated by Sensei-chat.
- They compare pairs or tuples of conversations that meet certain conditions (filters) and assert how the chatbot’s outputs should change or stay consistent when inputs change.
- This approach helps solve the "oracle problem" — where it’s hard to know the exact right answer — by focusing on *relations* between outputs rather than absolute correctness.

For example, a metamorphic rule might say:

> "If two pizza orders have the same size and drink, but one has more toppings, then the price in that conversation should be higher."

This rule compares two conversations and checks a monotonic relation rather than an absolute output.

In short:  
**Metamorphic rules check how chatbot outputs relate across multiple conversations when inputs vary in controlled ways, ensuring logical consistency and robustness.**
