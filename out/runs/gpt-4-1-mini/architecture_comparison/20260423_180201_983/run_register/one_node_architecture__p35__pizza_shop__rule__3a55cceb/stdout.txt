Here's a Sensei-check rule that verifies the price of a small pizza is exactly $10:

```yaml
name: small_pizza_price_exact
description: The price of a small pizza should be exactly $10
conversations: 1
when: size == 'small'
oracle: extract_float(price) == 10 and currency(price) == 'USD'
on-error: f"Expected price 10 USD for small pizza, got {price}"
``` 

This rule assumes your profile's `chatbot.output` includes `size` and `price` fields where `price` contains currency info. Let me know if you want it adapted! 😊
