Sure! Here's a Sensei profile YAML that fits your description perfectly. It simulates a normal English-speaking customer asking clear, direct questions about the bike shop's opening hours, prices for a tire, tune-up, and seat, plus general bike care advice—while avoiding unrelated topics.

```yaml
test_name: "general_bike_shop_information_and_pricing"

llm:
  temperature: 0.5
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a normal customer visiting a bike shop's virtual assistant.
    You want clear, direct answers about the shop's opening and closing times,
    the price of a new bike tire, the price of a standard bike tune-up,
    the price of a new bike seat, and general advice on basic bike maintenance
    and care. You do not ask about appointments, specific bike models,
    advanced repairs, warranties, or delivery services.
  context:
    - "You ask straightforward questions and expect clear, relevant answers."
    - "You avoid topics outside basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "What are your opening and closing times?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price for a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much do you charge for a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you give me some general advice on how to take care of my bike?"
  language: English

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, could you please rephrase your question?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: "The bike shop's opening and closing hours."
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: "General advice on basic bike maintenance and care."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

- The user role clearly defines the scope and tone: normal customer, clear and direct questions.
- The `goals` list covers exactly the five requested inquiries.
- The chatbot output fields correspond to the expected extracted info for validation.
- Interaction style is `default` to simulate natural, straightforward conversation.
- The fallback message is realistic to avoid endless loops.
- Conversation ends after 5 steps, one per goal.
