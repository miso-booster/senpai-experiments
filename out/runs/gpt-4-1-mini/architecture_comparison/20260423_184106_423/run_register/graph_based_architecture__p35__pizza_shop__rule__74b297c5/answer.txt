Sure! Here's a Sensei-check rule that verifies the price of a small pizza is exactly $10 (assuming the price field is a string like "10 USD" or "10.00 USD"):

```yaml
name: small_pizza_price_exact
description: The price of a small pizza must be exactly $10
conversations: 1
when: size == 'small'
oracle: extract_float(price) == 10 and currency(price) == 'USD'
```

This rule:

- Filters conversations where the pizza size is `'small'`.
- Checks that the numeric price extracted from the `price` field equals 10.
- Verifies the currency is USD.
