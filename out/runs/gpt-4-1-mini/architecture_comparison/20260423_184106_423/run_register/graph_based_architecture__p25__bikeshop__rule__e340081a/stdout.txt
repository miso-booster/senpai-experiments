```yaml
  Checks that the bike shop opening and closing times are between 10 and 20,

conversations: 1

  # Parse opening and closing times from the string (assuming format like "10-20" or "10:00-20:00")

  times = re.findall(r'\d{1,2}', opening_and_closing)
  if len(times) >= 2:
      open_time = int(times[0])
      close_time = int(times[1])
  else:
      open_time = close_time = -1  # invalid fallback

  (10 <= open_time <= 20) and (10 <= close_time <= 20) and \
  (extract_float(tune_up) == 50) and \
  (extract_float(seat_price) == 20) and \
  (extract_float(tire) == 20)
```

### Explanation:

- We check both opening and closing times are between 10 and 20.
- We use `extract_float()` helper to parse prices from `tune_up`, `seat_price`, and `tire` fields, and compare them to the expected values.
- The rule applies to a single conversation (`conversations: 1`).
