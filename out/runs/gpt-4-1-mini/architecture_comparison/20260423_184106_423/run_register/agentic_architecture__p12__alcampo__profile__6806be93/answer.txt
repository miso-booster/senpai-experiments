Here is a new profile YAML that simulates a user asking a chatbot to add items to their shopping cart. It includes:

- A user role describing the shopping context.
- Goals with variables for item names and quantities.
- Chatbot output fields to extract the final cart items and total count.
- Conversation controls for a couple of interactions.
- Interaction styles with some randomness and natural phrasing.

```yaml
test_name: "shopping_cart_add_items"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a shopper adding items to your online shopping cart.
    Ask the chatbot to add various items with specific quantities.
  context:
    - "You want to buy groceries and household items."
  goals:
    - "Add {{quantity}} of {{item}} to my shopping cart"
    - item:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [apples, bananas, milk, bread, eggs, cereal, coffee]
    - quantity:
        function: random()
        type: int
        data:
          - min: 1
            max: 10
            step: 1

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, I didn't understand. Could you please repeat?"
  output:
    - cart_items:
        type: list[string]
        description: "List of items added to the cart"
    - total_quantity:
        type: int
        description: "Total quantity of items added"

conversation:
  number: 2
  goal_style:
    steps: 4
  interaction_style:
    - random:
      - long phrases
      - single questions
      - make spelling mistakes
```
