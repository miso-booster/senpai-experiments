Sure! To complete the `chatbot` section in your profile, you want to define:

- Whether the chatbot starts the conversation (`is_starter`),
- A fallback message for when the chatbot doesn't understand,
- And importantly, the `output` fields that your rules or validations will inspect after the conversation.

Since your user goals focus on information seeking about pizza types, Margarita pizza price, drinks and prices, shop hours, and delivery availability, your `chatbot.output` should include fields capturing those pieces of information.

Here's a friendly, clear completion for your `chatbot` section that matches your profile's purpose:

```yaml
chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't understand that. Could you please rephrase?"
  output:
    - pizza_types:
        type: string
        description: "List of pizza types offered by the shop."
    - margarita_price:
        type: money
        description: "Price of a Margarita pizza, including currency."
    - drinks_and_prices:
        type: string
        description: "List of available drinks and their prices."
    - operating_hours:
        type: string
        description: "Shop's opening and closing hours."
    - delivery_availability:
        type: string
        description: "Information about whether delivery is available."
```

This way, your profile extracts all the key info your user is asking about, and these fields can be referenced later in Sensei-check rules for validation.
