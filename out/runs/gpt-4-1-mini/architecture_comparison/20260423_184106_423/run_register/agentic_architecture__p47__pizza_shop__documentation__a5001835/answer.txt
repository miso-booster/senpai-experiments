A metamorphic rule in Sensei is a type of validation rule that checks relationships or invariants across multiple conversations, rather than just verifying a single conversation's correctness. Specifically:

- Metamorphic rules compare pairs or tuples of conversations that meet certain selection criteria.
- They assert how outputs should change (or remain consistent) when inputs change in controlled ways.
- This approach helps overcome the "oracle problem" by focusing on relations between conversations instead of absolute expected outputs.
- Metamorphic rules specify how many conversations they apply to (usually 2 or more), a filter to select comparable conversations (`when`), and a condition or implication (`oracle` or `if`/`then`) that must hold across those conversations.

For example, a classic metamorphic rule might say:  
"If two pizza orders have the same size, drink, and quantity, then the one with more toppings should cost more."

This would be expressed by comparing the outputs of two conversations and asserting a monotonicity relation on price.

Metamorphic rules rely heavily on the outputs defined in the SENSEI Chat profiles (`chatbot.output`), referencing fields like `price`, `size`, or `toppings` across conversations.
