Here's a friendly Sensei profile that simulates a user asking the chatbot to add items to their shopping cart. It includes variables for item names and quantities, with a simple output schema to capture what the chatbot confirms about the added items.

```yaml
test_name: "shopping_cart_add_items"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a shopper who wants to add items to your online shopping cart.
    You ask the chatbot to add specific products and quantities.
  context:
    - "You want to add groceries and household items to your cart."
  goals:
    - "Add {{quantity}} units of {{item_name}} to my shopping cart."
    - quantity:
        function: random(3)
        type: int
        data:
          - 1
          - 2
          - 3
          - 4
          - 5
    - item_name:
        function: random(5)
        type: string
        data:
          - "milk"
          - "bread"
          - "eggs"
          - "apples"
          - "toilet paper"
          - "coffee"
          - "chicken breasts"
          - "rice"
          - "orange juice"
          - "cheese"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, I didn't understand. Could you please specify the item and quantity to add to your cart?"
  output:
    - added_item:
        type: string
        description: "Name of the item the chatbot confirms added to the cart"
    - added_quantity:
        type: int
        description: "Quantity of the item the chatbot confirms added"

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    steps: 4
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

This profile will generate conversations where the user asks to add various items with quantities to their cart, and the chatbot's output extraction expects confirmation of the item and quantity added. You can adjust `number` and `steps` as needed! 😊
