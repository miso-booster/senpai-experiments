Here is a simple SENSEI profile YAML for asking about subscription services and benefits. It includes only goals as requested, without any variables:

```yaml
test_name: "subscription_services_benefits"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "You are a customer interested in learning about subscription services and their benefits."
  context:
    - "You want clear and detailed information about subscription options, pricing, and benefits."
  goals:
    - "Ask about available subscription plans."
    - "Inquire about benefits included in the subscription."
    - "Request details on pricing and payment options."
    - "Ask about cancellation policy and contract terms."
    - "Request information on any trial periods or promotions."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, I didn't understand that. Could you please rephrase?"
  output: []

conversation:
  number: 2
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - random:
      - long phrases
      - single questions
```

This profile is ready to simulate a user asking about subscription services and benefits with multiple goals but no extracted variables. Let me know if you want me to add anything else! 😊

I based this on the profile structure from `sensei-chat-guide.md`.
