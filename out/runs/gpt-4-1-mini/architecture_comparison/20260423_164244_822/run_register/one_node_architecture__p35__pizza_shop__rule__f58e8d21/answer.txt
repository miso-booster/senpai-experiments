Here's a Sensei-check rule that verifies the price of a small pizza is exactly $10 (assuming the profile exposes `size` and `price` in `chatbot.output` and that `price` includes currency):

```yaml
name: small_pizza_price_exact
description: The price of a small pizza must be exactly $10 USD
conversations: 1
when: size == 'small'
oracle: extract_float(price) == 10 and currency(price) == 'USD'
on-error: f"Expected price $10 USD for small pizza, got {price}"
``` 

Let me know if you want it adapted for a different currency or profile field names! 😊
