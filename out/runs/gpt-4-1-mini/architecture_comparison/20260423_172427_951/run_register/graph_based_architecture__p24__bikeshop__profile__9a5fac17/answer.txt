Sure! Here's a Sensei profile YAML tailored to your request. It simulates a normal English-speaking customer asking clear, direct questions about the bike shop's opening hours, prices for a tire, tune-up, and seat, plus general bike care advice — all within the scope of basic info and standard services only.

```yaml
test_name: general_bike_shop_info_and_pricing

llm:
  temperature: 0.5
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a typical customer seeking general information about a bike shop.
    You ask clear and direct questions about the shop's opening and closing times,
    prices for a new bike tire, a standard bike tune-up, and a new bike seat.
    You also ask for basic bike maintenance advice focused on good practices.
    You do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, advanced repairs,
    warranties, delivery, or unrelated topics.
  context:
    - "You want straightforward answers using appropriate bike-shop terminology."
    - "You avoid unsolicited suggestions or unrelated services."
  goals:
    - "What are your opening and closing times?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much do you charge for a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you give me some basic advice on how to take care of my bike?"
  
chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you please rephrase?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: "The bike shop's opening and closing times as stated by the chatbot."
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire as provided by the chatbot."
    - tune_up:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up as stated by the chatbot."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat as stated by the chatbot."
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: "General advice on basic bike maintenance and care given by the chatbot."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    steps: 6
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

This profile:

- Uses straightforward goals phrased as direct questions.
- Keeps the user role focused on basic info and standard services.
- Defines the chatbot outputs to extract the relevant info for validation.
- Uses a single conversation with up to 6 steps (one per goal plus possible clarifications).
- Sets a fallback to handle misunderstandings gracefully.
- Keeps interaction style normal and natural (`default`).
