A metamorphic rule in Sensei is a type of validation rule that checks relationships or invariants across multiple conversations rather than just one. Instead of verifying a single conversation's output against a fixed expected answer, metamorphic rules compare pairs or tuples of conversations to assert how outputs should change (or remain stable) when inputs change in controlled ways.

Key points about metamorphic rules in Sensei:

- They operate on multiple conversations (usually 2 or more).
- They use a `when` filter to select comparable conversation tuples.
- They express relations using `if`/`then` or `oracle` conditions over the conversations, referencing extracted outputs from the conversations as `conv[i].field_name`.
- This approach helps alleviate the oracle problem by checking *relations* between outputs rather than absolute correctness.
- They are often tied to a specific SENSEI Chat profile whose `chatbot.output` fields are referenced in the rule.

For example, a classic metamorphic rule might say:  
"If two pizza orders have the same size and drink, then the one with more toppings should cost more."  
This compares two conversations and asserts a monotonicity relation on price.
