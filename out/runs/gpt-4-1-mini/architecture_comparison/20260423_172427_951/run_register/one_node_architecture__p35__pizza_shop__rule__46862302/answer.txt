Here's a Sensei-check rule that verifies the price of a small pizza is exactly $10. It assumes your profile's `chatbot.output` includes a `size` field (string) and a `price` field (currency or string containing the price):

```yaml
name: small_pizza_price_exact
description: The price of a small pizza must be exactly $10
conversations: 1
when: size == 'small'
oracle: extract_float(price) == 10 and currency(price) == 'USD'
on-error: f"Expected price 10 USD for small pizza, got {price}"
```

This rule:

- Filters conversations where the pizza size is `'small'`.
- Checks that the extracted numeric price equals 10.
- Confirms the currency is USD.
- Provides a helpful error message if the check fails.

If your profile uses different field names or currency, just let me know and I can adjust it! 😊
