```yaml

description: Checks that the CAU provided correct information about contact details.
conversations: 1

```

- This is a single-conversation rule (`conversations: 1`).
- It references fields (`contact_email`, `contact_phone`, `physical_address`) that exist in the profile outputs.
- The `when` clause filters conversations that already have the exact correct contact details.
- The `oracle` repeats the same condition, so it only checks correctness if the conversation already has the correct info.

### What’s the issue?

- The `when` clause acts as a filter to select conversations to check.
- Having the `when` and `oracle` identical means the rule only applies when the data is already correct, making the check redundant.
- Usually, the `when` clause should select relevant conversations (e.g., those about CAU contact info), and the `oracle` should verify that the outputs are correct.
- If you want to check that the CAU contact details are correct whenever they appear, you can omit the `when` clause or use a broader filter.

### How to improve it?

- Remove the `when` clause or replace it with a condition that selects conversations about CAU contact info (if such a field exists).
- Keep the `oracle` to assert the contact details equal the expected values.
