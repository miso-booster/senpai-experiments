Here is a new SENSEI profile YAML for testing a bike shop’s virtual assistant. It simulates an English-speaking customer who asks clearly and directly about the shop’s opening hours, prices for standard items (bike tire, tune-up, bike seat), and general bike care advice focusing on basic maintenance. The user avoids topics outside the specified scope.

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_info_customer"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer visiting a bike shop's virtual assistant.
    You want clear and direct answers about the shop's opening and closing times,
    the prices of a new bike tire, a standard bike tune-up, and a new bike seat.
    You also want general advice on basic bike maintenance and good care practices.
    You do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs,
    warranties, delivery, or unrelated services.
  context:
    - "You behave like a normal customer seeking straightforward information."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price for a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much do you charge for a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you give me some general advice on how to properly take care of my bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices?"
  
chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, I didn't understand that. Could you please ask again?"
  output:
    - opening_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The shop's opening and closing times as stated by the chatbot"
    - tire_price:
        type: currency
        description: "Price of a new bike tire"
    - tuneup_price:
        type: currency
        description: "Price of a standard bike tune-up"
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: "Price of a new bike seat"
    - maintenance_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice on basic bike maintenance and care"

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

**Summary:**
- The user role and goals are tailored to the bike shop context.
- The chatbot output fields capture the key information to validate: shop hours, prices, and maintenance advice.
- The user asks only the specified questions, phrased clearly and directly.
- Interaction style is set to single questions to reflect a typical customer inquiry flow.
