Here's a Sensei-check rule that verifies the price of a small pizza is exactly $10 (assuming the profile exposes `size` and `price` in `chatbot.output` and `price` is a currency type):

```yaml
name: small_pizza_price_exact
description: The price of a small pizza must be exactly $10
conversations: 1
when: size == 'small'
oracle: extract_float(price) == 10 and currency(price) == 'USD'
yields: f"Expected price for small pizza is $10, got {price}"
```
