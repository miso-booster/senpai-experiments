Hey! 😊 I see you want to create a Sensei-check rule that verifies the outputs of your bike shop chatbot conversations. The rule should check that:

- The opening and closing time is between 10 and 20 (I assume these are hours, like 10:00 to 20:00),
- The tune-up price is 50,
- The seat price is 20,
- The tire price is 20.

Great! Let's write a single-conversation rule (since these are fixed expected values) that asserts these conditions on the chatbot's output fields.

Here’s a clear YAML rule you can use:

```yaml
name: bike_shop_price_and_hours_check
description: Checks that opening/closing times are between 10 and 20, and prices for tune-up, seat, and tire are correct.
conversations: 1
oracle: >
  # Parse opening and closing times from the string (assuming format like "10-20" or "10:00-20:00")
  import re
  times = re.findall(r'\d+', opening_and_closing)
  opening = int(times[0]) if len(times) > 0 else None
  closing = int(times[1]) if len(times) > 1 else None

  # Check times are between 10 and 20
  opening is not None and closing is not None and 10 <= opening <= 20 and 10 <= closing <= 20 and

  # Check prices (assuming prices are strings with numbers, extract floats)
  float(tune_up) == 50 and
  float(seat_price) == 20 and
  float(tire) == 20
```

### Explanation:

- We extract numbers from the `opening_and_closing` string to get opening and closing hours.
- We check both are between 10 and 20.
- We convert `tune_up`, `seat_price`, and `tire` to floats and check they match the expected values.
