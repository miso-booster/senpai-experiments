Got it! Here's a Sensei profile where the user starts by asking for a custom pizza but then changes their mind during the conversation and orders a predefined pizza instead. The profile uses the `change your mind` interaction style to simulate that behavior.

```yaml
test_name: "custom_to_predefined_pizza_change_mind"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer who initially wants to order a custom pizza with specific toppings,
    but halfway through the conversation, you change your mind and decide to order a predefined pizza instead.
  context:
    - "You like to customize your pizza but sometimes prefer ready-made options."
  goals:
    - "I want to order a {{size}} custom pizza with {{toppings}}."
    - "Actually, I changed my mind. I want a {{predefined_pizza}} pizza instead."
    - size:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [small, medium, large]
    - toppings:
        function: random(3)
        type: string
        data: [cheese, mushrooms, pepperoni, olives, bacon, onions]
    - predefined_pizza:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [Margherita, Pepperoni, Hawaiian, Vegetarian, BBQ Chicken]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I did not get what you said. Can you please clarify?"
  output:
    - final_order:
        type: string
        description: "Summary of the final pizza order confirmed by the chatbot."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    steps: 6
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
```

This profile:

- Defines two goals: first ordering a custom pizza, then changing to a predefined pizza.
- Uses `change your mind` interaction style to simulate the user changing their order mid-conversation.
- Extracts a `final_order` field from the chatbot output to confirm what was finally ordered.
