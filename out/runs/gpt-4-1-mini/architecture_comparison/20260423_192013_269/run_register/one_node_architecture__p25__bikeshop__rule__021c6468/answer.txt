Sure! Here's a Sensei-check rule that verifies the chatbot outputs for your profile. It checks that:

- The `opening_and_closing` time mentions opening at 10 and closing at 20 (interpreted as 10 to 20).
- The `tire` price is 20.
- The `tune_up` price is 50.
- The `seat_price` is 20.

Since `seat_price` is a string type, I assume it contains a price mention that we can parse as a float for comparison.

The rule will run on single conversations (`conversations: 1`).

```yaml
name: bike_shop_price_and_hours_check
description: Checks opening hours and prices of tire, tune-up, and seat in the bike shop profile outputs
conversations: 1
oracle: |
  # Check opening and closing times mention 10 and 20 (hours)
  ("10" in opening_and_closing and "20" in opening_and_closing) and \
  extract_float(str(tire)) == 20 and \
  extract_float(str(tune_up)) == 50 and \
  extract_float(seat_price) == 20
yields: |
  f"Opening hours or prices do not match expected values: opening_and_closing='{opening_and_closing}', tire={tire}, tune_up={tune_up}, seat_price='{seat_price}'"
```
