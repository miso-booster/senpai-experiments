- The **Ada UAM** connector is specifically tailored for the **1millionbot** API, featuring a more complex payload structure and specific headers for authorization.
- The **Echo Bot** connector is simpler, primarily designed for testing purposes, with a straightforward payload and response structure.
- The differences highlight the complexity and specificity of the **1millionbot** connectors compared to the more general-purpose non-1millionbot connector.
