### Rule Overview
The rule **"same_number_of_toppings_cost_the_same"** checks that pizzas with the same number of toppings cost the same. Here's how it works:

- **When**: It compares two conversations (like two orders).
- **If**: The drinks are the same, the sizes are the same, and the number of toppings is the same.
- **Then**: It checks if the prices of both pizzas are equal.

### Explanation Like You're 5
Imagine you have two friends who want to buy pizzas. If both friends want the same size pizza and the same number of toppings (like cheese and pepperoni), the rule says they should pay the same amount of money for their pizzas. 

So, if Friend A has a pizza with 2 toppings and pays $10, then Friend B, who also has a pizza with 2 toppings, should also pay $10. If they don't, something is wrong! 🍕💰
