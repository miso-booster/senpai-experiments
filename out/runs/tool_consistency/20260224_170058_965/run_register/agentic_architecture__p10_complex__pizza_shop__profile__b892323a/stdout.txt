### Profile Comparison

#### 1. **pizza_order_test_custom**
- **Purpose**: This profile simulates a user ordering a pizza and includes queries about drinks and opening times.
- **Key Outputs**:
  - `price`: Final price of the pizza order.
  - `time`: Opening time of the shop.
  - `order_id`: Unique identifier for the order.
- **Goals**:
  - Order a custom pizza with specified size and toppings.
  - Order drinks (cans).
  - Inquire about opening and closing times.
  - Ask about costs.

#### 2. **pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker**
- **Purpose**: This profile focuses on gathering information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours before placing an order.
- **Key Outputs**:
  - `pizza_types`: List of available pizza types.
  - `margarita_price`: Price of a Margarita pizza.
  - `drinks_and_prices`: List of available drinks and their prices.
  - `operating_hours`: Shop's operating hours.
  - `delivery_availability`: Information on delivery options.
- **Goals**:
  - Ask about available pizza types.
  - Inquire about the price of a Margarita pizza.
  - Ask about drinks and their prices.
  - Inquire about the shop's operating hours.
  - Confirm delivery options.

### Which Profile is Better?
- **For a user seeking information about drinks, prices, and opening hours**: The **`pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker`** profile is better suited. It explicitly includes queries about drinks, prices, and operating hours, making it comprehensive for someone gathering information before making a decision.
