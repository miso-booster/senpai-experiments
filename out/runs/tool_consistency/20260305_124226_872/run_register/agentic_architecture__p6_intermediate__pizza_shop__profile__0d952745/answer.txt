### Candidate Profiles for Drinks, Prices, and Opening Hours

I found two suitable profiles that match your requirements:

1. **Profile: `pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker.yaml`**
   - **Role**: A customer seeking information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours.
   - **Outputs**:
     - `pizza_types`: List of pizza types offered.
     - `margarita_price`: Price of a Margarita pizza.
     - `drinks_and_prices`: List of available drinks and their prices.
     - `operating_hours`: Shop's operating hours.
     - `delivery_availability`: Information on whether the shop delivers.

2. **Profile: `pizza_and_drink_order_placement.yaml`**
   - **Role**: Customer placing a pizza and drink order for pickup.
   - **Outputs**:
     - `pizza_options`: List of available pizza types.
     - `drink_options`: List of available drinks.
     - `order_summary`: Summary of the pizza and drink order.
     - `total_cost`: Total cost of the order.
     - `estimated_readiness_time`: Estimated time until the order is ready.
     - `pickup_location`: Address for pickup.
     - `order_id`: Unique identifier for the order.

### Comparison of Output Fields

- **Information Seeker Profile**:
  - Focuses on gathering information before placing an order.
  - Outputs are more about inquiry (types, prices, hours).
  
- **Order Placement Profile**:
  - Focuses on executing an order.
  - Outputs include order details and readiness, which are more transactional.
