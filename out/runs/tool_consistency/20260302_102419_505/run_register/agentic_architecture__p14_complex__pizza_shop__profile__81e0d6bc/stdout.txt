Let's compare the two profiles: **`pizza_order_test_custom`** and **`pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker`** to determine which one is better suited for a user seeking information about drinks, prices, and opening hours.

### Profile Overview

#### 1. **`pizza_order_test_custom`**
- **Role**: User ordering a pizza.
- **Goals**:
  - Order a custom pizza with specified size and toppings.
  - Request drinks (cans).
  - Inquire about opening and closing times.
  - Ask about the total cost.
- **Outputs**:
  - Price of the order.
  - Opening time.
  - Order ID.
- **Interaction Style**: Long phrases, random elements like spelling mistakes.

#### 2. **`pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker`**
- **Role**: Customer seeking information before ordering.
- **Goals**:
  - Ask about available pizza types.
  - Inquire about the price of a Margarita pizza.
  - Ask about available drinks and their prices.
  - Inquire about the shop's operating hours.
  - Ask if delivery is available.
- **Outputs**:
  - List of pizza types.
  - Price of a Margarita pizza.
  - List of drinks and their prices.
  - Shop's operating hours.
  - Delivery availability.
- **Interaction Style**: Single questions.

### Comparison
- **Relevance**: 
  - **`pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker`** is more focused on gathering information about drinks, prices, and opening hours, making it better suited for the user's needs.
  - **`pizza_order_test_custom`** is primarily focused on placing an order rather than gathering information.

- **Goals Alignment**:
  - The goals of **`pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker`** directly address the user's needs, while **`pizza_order_test_custom`** includes ordering specifics that may not be relevant at this stage.
