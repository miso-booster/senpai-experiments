### Best Profile for User Queries

I found a suitable profile for a user asking about drinks, prices, and opening hours:

**Profile: `pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker.yaml`**
- **Description**: This profile is designed for a customer seeking information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours before potentially placing an order.
- **Key Outputs**:
  - `pizza_types`: A list of the pizza types offered by the shop.
  - `margarita_price`: The price of a Margarita pizza.
  - `drinks_and_prices`: A list of available drinks and their corresponding prices.
  - `operating_hours`: The shop's operating hours.
  - `delivery_availability`: Indication of whether the shop delivers.
