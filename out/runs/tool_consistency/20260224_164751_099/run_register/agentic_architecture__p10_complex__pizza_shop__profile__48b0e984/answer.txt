### Profile Comparison

#### 1. **pizza_order_test_custom**
- **Purpose**: Designed for users ordering custom pizzas, including drinks and asking about opening times and prices.
- **Key Outputs**:
  - `price`: Final price of the pizza order.
  - `time`: Opening time of the shop.
  - `order_id`: Unique identifier for the order.
- **User Goals**:
  - Order a custom pizza with specified size and toppings.
  - Specify the number of drink cans.
  - Inquire about opening and closing times.
  - Ask about the total cost.

#### 2. **pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker**
- **Purpose**: Aims to gather information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours before placing an order.
- **Key Outputs**:
  - `pizza_types`: List of available pizza types.
  - `margarita_price`: Price of a Margarita pizza.
  - `drinks_and_prices`: List of drinks and their prices.
  - `operating_hours`: Shop's operating hours.
  - `delivery_availability`: Information on whether delivery is available.
- **User Goals**:
  - Ask about available pizza types and their prices.
  - Inquire about drinks and their prices.
  - Ask about the shop's operating hours and delivery options.

### Which Profile is Better?
For a user specifically seeking information about drinks, prices, and opening hours, **`pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker`** is the better choice. It is tailored to gather detailed information about the menu and operational details, which aligns perfectly with the user's needs.
