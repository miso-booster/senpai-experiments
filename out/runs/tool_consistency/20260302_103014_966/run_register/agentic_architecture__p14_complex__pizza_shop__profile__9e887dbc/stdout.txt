### Profile Comparison

#### 1. **pizza_order_test_custom**
- **Purpose**: This profile simulates a user ordering a pizza, including drinks and asking for opening hours.
- **Key Outputs**:
  - `price`: The final price of the pizza order.
  - `time`: The opening time.
  - `order_id`: The order ID.
- **Goals**:
  - Order a custom pizza with specified size and toppings.
  - Order a specified number of cans of a drink.
  - Inquire about the shop's opening and closing times.
  
#### 2. **pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker**
- **Purpose**: This profile is designed for a customer seeking information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours before placing an order.
- **Key Outputs**:
  - `pizza_types`: A list of available pizza types.
  - `margarita_price`: The price of a Margarita pizza.
  - `drinks_and_prices`: A list of available drinks and their prices.
  - `operating_hours`: The shop's operating hours.
  - `delivery_availability`: Information on whether the shop delivers.
- **Goals**:
  - Ask about available pizza types and their prices.
  - Ask about available drinks and their prices.
  - Ask about the shop's operating hours and delivery options.

### Which Profile is Better?
For a user specifically looking for information about drinks, prices, and opening hours, **`pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker`** is the better profile. It directly addresses the user's needs by providing structured outputs for drinks, prices, and operating hours, making it more suitable for gathering information.
