  - **Ada UAM** requires specific headers for authorization.
  - **Echo Bot** does not require any headers.
  - **Metro Madrid** requires multiple headers including `Origin` and `User-Agent`.

  - **Ada UAM** has a complex payload with multiple fields including `conversation`, `sender`, and `url`.
  - **Echo Bot** has a simple payload with just one field for the message.
  - **Metro Madrid** has a structured payload that includes project and client IDs, as well as user information.

### Summary
The **1millionbot** connector (Ada UAM) is more complex in terms of payload and requires specific headers for authorization, while the non-1millionbot connectors (Echo Bot and Metro Madrid) have simpler payloads and different endpoint structures.
