I found the rule about topping price consistency! 🎉 Here's a simple explanation of it:

### Rule Overview
- **Name**: More Toppings Cost More
- **Description**: If you add more toppings to a pizza, it should cost more money.
- **How it Works**:
  - **When**: The pizzas being compared are the same size and have the same drink.
  - **If**: One pizza has more toppings than the other.
  - **Then**: The price of the pizza with more toppings should be higher.

### Explanation Like You're 5
Imagine you have two pizzas:
- One pizza has 2 toppings (like cheese and pepperoni).
- The other pizza has 4 toppings (like cheese, pepperoni, mushrooms, and olives).

If both pizzas are the same size and come with the same drink, the pizza with 4 toppings should cost more than the one with just 2 toppings. 🍕💰

So, this rule helps make sure that when you add more yummy toppings to your pizza, you also pay a little more for it!
