### Profile Comparison

Let's compare the two profiles based on their goals and outputs:

#### 1. **pizza_order_test_custom**
- **Goals:**
  - Order a custom pizza with size and toppings.
  - Order drinks (cans).
  - Inquire about opening and closing times.
  - Ask about the total cost.
  
- **Outputs:**
  - `price`: Final price of the pizza order.
  - `time`: Opening time.
  - `order_id`: ID for the order.

#### 2. **pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker**
- **Goals:**
  - Ask about available pizza types.
  - Inquire about the price of a Margarita pizza.
  - Ask about available drinks and their prices.
  - Ask about the shop's operating hours.
  - Confirm delivery options.

- **Outputs:**
  - `pizza_types`: List of pizza types offered.
  - `margarita_price`: Price of a Margarita pizza.
  - `drinks_and_prices`: List of drinks and their prices.
  - `operating_hours`: Shop's operating hours.
  - `delivery_availability`: Delivery confirmation.

### Which Profile is Better?
For a user seeking information about drinks, prices, and opening hours, **`pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker`** is the better choice. It directly addresses all the user's needs by providing specific outputs related to drinks, prices, and operating hours.
