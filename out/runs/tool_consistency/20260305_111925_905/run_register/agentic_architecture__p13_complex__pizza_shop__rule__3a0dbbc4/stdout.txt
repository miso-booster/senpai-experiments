Here are the rules and their descriptions:

1. **conversation_lengths.yaml**
   - Ensures the number of turns of the user and the chatbot equals the conversation length.

2. **more_toppings_cost_more.yaml**
   - Validates that adding more toppings to a custom pizza costs more.

3. **only_talks_about_pizzas.yaml**
   - Ensures the chatbot only talks about pizzas.

4. **ordering_conversation_turns.yaml**
   - Validates that the user always orders a pizza and then drinks.

5. **ordering_conversation_turns_assistant.yaml**
   - Validates that the assistant confirms the pizza order after the user orders a pizza.

6. **same_number_of_toppings_cost_the_same.yaml**
   - Validates that pizzas with the same number of toppings cost the same.
