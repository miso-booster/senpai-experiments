### Best Profile for Drinks, Prices, and Opening Hours

I found the most suitable profile for a user asking about drinks, prices, and opening hours:

**Profile: `pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker.yaml`**
- **Role**: A customer seeking information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours.
- **Goals**:
  - Ask about the available pizza types.
  - Ask about the price of a Margarita pizza.
  - Ask about the available drinks and their prices.
  - Ask about the shop's operating hours.
  - Ask if they deliver.
- **Outputs**:
  - `pizza_types`: List of available pizza types.
  - `margarita_price`: Price of a Margarita pizza.
  - `drinks_and_prices`: List of available drinks and their prices.
  - `operating_hours`: Shop's operating hours.
  - `delivery_availability`: Indication of whether the shop delivers.
