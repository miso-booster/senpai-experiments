### 2. **Rules Overview**
Here are the rules available in the project:

1. **conversation_lengths.yaml**
   - Checks if the number of turns of the user and the chatbot equals the conversation length.

2. **more_toppings_cost_more.yaml**
   - Validates that adding more toppings to a custom pizza costs more.

3. **only_talks_about_pizza.yaml**
   - Ensures the chatbot only talks about pizzas.

4. **ordering_conversation_turns.yaml**
   - Checks that the user always orders a pizza before drinks.

5. **ordering_conversation_turns_assistant.yaml**
   - Ensures the assistant confirms the pizza order after the user orders a pizza.

6. **same_number_of_toppings_cost_the_same.yaml**
   - Validates that pizzas with the same number of toppings cost the same.

### 3. **Mapping Rules to Profile Outputs**
Here's how the rules relate to the outputs of the profiles:

- **more_toppings_cost_more.yaml**
  - Validates outputs related to pizza pricing (e.g., `price` from profiles that deal with pizza orders).
  
- **only_talks_about_pizza.yaml**
  - Relates to all profiles that involve pizza-related queries (e.g., `general_information_seeker`, `pizza_and_drink_order_placement`, etc.).

- **ordering_conversation_turns.yaml** and **ordering_conversation_turns_assistant.yaml**

- **same_number_of_toppings_cost_the_same.yaml**

### 4. **Identifying Gaps**
After reviewing the profiles and rules, here are some identified gaps:

- **General Information Validation**: There are no rules validating the outputs of `general_information_seeker.yaml`, such as `available_information`, `opening_hours`, or `estimated_order_time`. This profile could benefit from rules that check if the chatbot provides accurate information about its services.

- **Delivery Queries**: The `delivery_availability` output in `pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker.yaml` is not validated by any rules. A rule could be created to ensure that the chatbot correctly states whether delivery is available.

- **Custom Pizza Pricing**: The `custom_pizza_pricing` output in `general_information_seeker.yaml` is also untested. A rule could be added to validate the pricing information provided for custom pizzas.
