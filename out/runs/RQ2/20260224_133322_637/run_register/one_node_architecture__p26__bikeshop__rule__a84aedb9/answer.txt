name: bike_shop_prices_and_times
description: Check the bike shop's opening times and prices for tire, tune-up, and seat.
conversations: 1
oracle: |
  (10 <= extract_float(opening_and_closing) <= 20) and

  (extract_float(seat_price) == 20)

- **`name`**: A descriptive name for the rule.
- **`description`**: A brief explanation of what the rule checks.
- **`conversations`**: Set to 1 since we are checking the outputs from a single conversation.
- **`oracle`**: This is where we define the conditions:
  - It checks if the opening and closing times are between 10 and 20.
  - It verifies that the tire price is 20, the tune-up price is 50, and the seat price is 20.
