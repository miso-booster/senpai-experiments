Sure thing! Here’s a profile you can use to test the bike shop’s virtual assistant. This profile focuses on a customer seeking general information about the shop and its services while ensuring clear and direct communication. Let’s dive in! 🚴‍♂️

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_information"

llm:
  temperature: 0.5
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking for general information about the bike shop and its standard services."
  context:
    - "You want to gather basic information before visiting the shop."
    - "You are interested in understanding the pricing for common items and services."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much is a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you provide general advice on how to properly take care of a bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing: 
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - tire_price: 
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price: 
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price: 
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
    - maintenance_advice: 
        type: string
        description: "General advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance."

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 5
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### Explanation of the Profile:

- **Test Name**: Clearly identifies the purpose of the profile.
- **User Role**: Describes the user as a customer looking for basic information, ensuring the interaction is straightforward.
- **Context**: Sets the stage for the user’s intent and expectations.
- **Goals**: Lists specific questions the user will ask, focusing on shop hours, pricing, and basic bike care.
- **Chatbot Output**: Defines what the chatbot should provide in response to each question, ensuring clarity and relevance.
- **Conversation Controls**: Limits the number of interactions to keep the conversation focused and ensures all goals are addressed.

Feel free to tweak any part of this profile to better fit your testing needs! Happy testing! 😊
