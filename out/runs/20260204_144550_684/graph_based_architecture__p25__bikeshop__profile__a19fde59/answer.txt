Sure! Here’s a profile tailored for testing a bike shop’s virtual assistant. This profile captures the essence of a customer seeking general information about the shop and its standard services, while ensuring that the interaction remains focused and relevant. 🎉

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_info"

llm:
  temperature: 0.5
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "You are a customer looking for general information about the bike shop and its standard services."
  context:
    - "You want to know about opening hours, prices for basic services, and general bike care."
    - "You will ask clear and direct questions."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much is a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you give me some general advice on how to take care of my bike?"
  
chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: str
        description: "The opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire"
    - tune_up_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up"
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat"
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: str
        description: "General advice on how to properly take care of a bike"

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 5
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### Key Features of This Profile:
- **User Role**: Clearly defined as a customer seeking basic information.
- **Goals**: Focused on specific questions about shop hours, prices, and bike care.
- **Output Fields**: Well-structured to capture the necessary information for validation.
- **Conversation Control**: Ensures that all questions are answered while maintaining a clear interaction style.
