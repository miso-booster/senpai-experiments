Sure thing! Here’s a profile designed to test a bike shop’s virtual assistant, focusing on general information and standard services. This profile ensures that the user behaves like a typical customer, asking clear and direct questions. Let’s dive in! 🚴‍♂️

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_information"

llm:
  temperature: 0.5
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer seeking general information about the bike shop and its services."
  context:
    - "You are interested in gathering basic information about the bike shop."
    - "You want to know about the shop's opening and closing times, pricing for standard services, and general bike maintenance tips."
  goals:
    - "What are the opening and closing times of the bike shop?"
    - "How much does a new bike tire cost?"
    - "What is the price of a standard bike tune-up?"
    - "How much is a new bike seat?"
    - "Can you give me some general advice on how to properly take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry, it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing: 
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - tire_price: 
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price: 
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price: 
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
    - maintenance_advice: 
        type: string
        description: "General advice on how to properly take care of a bike."

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 5
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### Profile Breakdown:
- **User Role**: The user is a customer looking for basic information, ensuring the interaction is straightforward and relevant.
- **Goals**: The user asks specific questions about shop hours, prices for standard items, and maintenance advice, avoiding unrelated topics.
- **Chatbot Output**: The expected outputs are clearly defined, ensuring the chatbot provides accurate and relevant information.
- **Conversation Control**: The conversation is set to ensure all questions are answered, with a limit to maintain focus.
