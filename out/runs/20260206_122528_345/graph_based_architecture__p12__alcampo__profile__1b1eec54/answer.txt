Sure! Here’s a simple profile that asks the chatbot for items to add to the user's shopping cart. You can customize it further based on your specific needs. Just copy and paste this into a new YAML file in your `profiles/` directory:

```yaml
test_name: "Shopping Cart Addition"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking to add items to their shopping cart."
  context:
    - "You are planning your grocery shopping and want to ensure you have everything you need."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot for suggestions on items to add to my shopping cart."
    - "Request specific categories of items, such as fruits, vegetables, or snacks."
    - "Inquire about any current promotions on items that can be added to the cart."
    - "Ask for recommendations based on dietary preferences, like vegan or gluten-free options."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. What items would you like to add to your shopping cart?"
  output:
    - suggested_items:
        type: string
        description: "List of suggested items to add to the shopping cart."
    - promotion_details:
        type: string
        description: "Details about current promotions on suggested items."
    - dietary_options:
        type: string
        description: "Recommendations based on dietary preferences."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```
