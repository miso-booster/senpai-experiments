### Best Profile for User Inquiry

The best profile for a user asking about drinks, prices, and opening hours is **`pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker.yaml`**. Here’s a brief overview of its structure:

- **User Role**: A customer seeking information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours.
- **Goals**:
  - Ask about available pizza types.
  - Ask about the price of a Margarita pizza.
  - Ask about available drinks and their prices.
  - Ask about the shop's operating hours.
  - Ask if they deliver.
- **Outputs**:
  - `pizza_types`: List of pizza types.
  - `margarita_price`: Price of a Margarita pizza.
  - `drinks_and_prices`: List of available drinks and their prices.
  - `operating_hours`: Shop's operating hours.
  - `delivery_availability`: Information on delivery options.

### Current Rules and Coverage

I found the following rules that relate to the profile's outputs:

1. **`same_number_of_toppings_cost_the_same.yaml`**
   - **Description**: Pizzas with the same number of toppings cost the same.
   - **Active**: No
   - **Key Logic**: Compares prices based on the number of toppings.

2. **`only_talks_about_pizzas.yaml`**
   - **Description**: The chatbot only talks about pizzas.
   - **Active**: Yes
   - **Key Logic**: Ensures the conversation stays focused on pizza-related topics.

3. **`more_toppings_cost_more.yaml`**
   - **Description**: Adding more toppings to a custom pizza costs more.
   - **Active**: Yes
   - **Key Logic**: Compares prices based on the number of toppings.

### Missing Rule Coverage

- **Missing Coverage**: 
  - There are currently no rules validating the outputs related to **drinks and prices**, **operating hours**, and **delivery availability**. This means that while the rules cover pizza-related aspects, they do not validate the specific outputs required by the user’s inquiry about drinks and shop hours.
