```yaml
name: drink_cost_increase
description: Adding a drink should increase the total price by 2 dollars
conversations: 2
when: conv[0].pizza_size == conv[1].pizza_size and conv[0].selected_pizza_type == conv[1].selected_pizza_type
if:   conv[0].drink_quantity_requested == 0 and conv[1].drink_quantity_requested > 0
then: extract_float(conv[1].pizza_order_confirmation) == extract_float(conv[0].pizza_order_confirmation) + 2
```

### Explanation:
- **`when`**: Ensures both conversations are for the same pizza size and type.
- **`if`**: Checks that the first conversation has no drinks and the second has at least one drink.
- **`then`**: Asserts that the price in the second conversation is exactly 2 dollars more than in the first.
