Creating a profile for an Erasmus student is a fantastic use case. Since they are international students, it's a great opportunity to use interaction styles like `make spelling mistakes` or `change language` to simulate that authentic "just arrived and slightly confused" experience! 🌍🎒

To make sure you can validate the support later with Sensei-check rules, I have defined a robust `chatbot.output` section. It extracts the exact steps for Moodle, Sigma, Wi-Fi, and the IT support contact. I also included the exact fallback message from your `ada_uam` project so it fits right in.

Here is your ready-to-use profile:

```yaml
test_name: "erasmus_digital_setup"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: Spanish
  role: >
    You are an Erasmus exchange student who just arrived at the university. 
    You are feeling a bit overwhelmed and don't know how to set up your digital university accounts like Moodle, Sigma, or the Wi-Fi.
  context:
    - "You need clear, step-by-step instructions because you are not familiar with the university's systems."
    - "You want to know where to get in-person help if you can't figure it out."
  goals:
    - "Ask how to log into Moodle for the first time to see your classes."
    - "Ask how to access the Sigma platform to check your academic record."
    - "Ask how to connect your {{device}} to the university Wi-Fi (eduroam)."
    - "Ask for the location or contact of the IT support desk for international students."
    - device:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [laptop, smartphone, tablet]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "¿Puedes preguntármelo de otra forma? o, si lo prefieres, puedes consultar nuestro\ncatálogo de servicios\n.\nRespondo a preguntas dirigidas al\nCentro de Atención a Usuarios del servicio de Tecnologías de la Información."
  output:
    - moodle_setup_steps:
        type: string
        description: "Step-by-step instructions provided to access Moodle."
    - sigma_access_info:
        type: string
        description: "Information or link provided to access the Sigma platform."
    - wifi_instructions:
        type: string
        description: "Instructions on how to connect to the university Wi-Fi."
    - support_contact:
        type: string
        description: "Contact information or physical location of the IT support desk."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 20
  interaction_style:
    - random:
      - single questions
      - make spelling mistakes
      - change language:
        - English
        - French
        - Italian
```

### 👀 Why this works perfectly for your needs:
1. **Minimal Inputs:** We only used one simple variable (`{{device}}`) to keep the setup lightweight, just as you asked.
2. **Erasmus Behavior:** The `interaction_style` randomly applies spelling mistakes or switches the language to English, French, or Italian, perfectly mimicking an international student!
3. **Rule-Ready Outputs:** Because we defined fields like `moodle_setup_steps` and `support_contact` in the `chatbot.output`, you can easily write a Sensei-check rule later like `oracle: data_collected() == True` or `oracle: conv[0].support_contact is not None` to ensure the bot is actually providing the help they need.
