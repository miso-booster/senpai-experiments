That is a fantastic scenario to test! It’s really common for users to just want advice without the pressure of booking an appointment right away. This profile will help you check if your chatbot can provide helpful, empathetic advice and explain its services without being overly pushy.

I've set up the profile to cycle through different types of pain (like lower back or knee pain) using the `forward()` function, so you get a variety of conversations. I also added a field in `chatbot.output` called `appointment_pushed` so you can easily track if the bot tries to force a booking! 👀

Here is the complete YAML for your new profile:

```yaml
test_name: bike_fit_and_comfort_inquirer

llm:
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  temperature: 0.5
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A cyclist experiencing discomfort while riding, looking for advice on bike fit and worn components."
  context:
    - "You are experiencing discomfort and pain during your rides."
    - "You are unsure if your bike is improperly fitted to you or if some components (like the saddle or grips) are just worn out."
    - "You want to know what adjustments or services the shop offers to help resolve this."
    - "You are just gathering information and do NOT want to book an appointment right now."
  goals:
    - "Explain that you are experiencing {{pain_area}} pain while riding."
    - "Ask if this is usually caused by a poor bike fit or worn-out components."
    - "Inquire about what specific services or adjustments the shop offers to fix this issue."
    - pain_area:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [lower back, knee, saddle, neck and shoulder]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, could you repeat that?"
  output:
    - advice_given:
        type: string
        description: "The chatbot's explanation of whether the issue might be related to bike fit or worn components."
    - services_suggested:
        type: string
        description: "The specific services or adjustments the shop offers to help with the user's discomfort (e.g., professional bike fit, saddle replacement)."
    - appointment_pushed:
        type: string
        description: "Note if the chatbot explicitly tried to push or schedule an appointment despite the user only asking for advice."

conversation:
  number: 4
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - single question
```

You can save this as `bike_fit_and_comfort_inquirer.yaml` inside your `profiles/` folder. Let me know if you'd like to tweak the pain areas, or if you need help writing a Sensei Rule to validate the chatbot's responses! 🚲✨
