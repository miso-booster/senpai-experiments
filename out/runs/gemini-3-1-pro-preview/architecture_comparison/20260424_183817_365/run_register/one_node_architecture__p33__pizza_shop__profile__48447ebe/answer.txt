In Sensei, the `chatbot` section is super important because it defines how the conversation starts, what happens if the bot gets confused, and—most importantly—what information Sensei needs to **extract** at the end of the chat (the `output`). 

Since your user's goals are all about gathering specific information (pizza types, Margarita price, drinks, hours, and delivery), we should set up the `output` to extract exactly those details! This way, you can later write Sensei-check rules to verify that the chatbot gave the correct answers. 👀

Here is your fully completed profile. I added the `chatbot` section with a nice fallback message and all the necessary output fields mapped right to your goals:

```yaml
test_name: pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker
llm:
  temperature: 0.3
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text
user:
  language: English
  role: A customer seeking information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours before potentially placing an order.
  context:
  - 'personality: personalities/skeptical-user'
  - You're planning a casual pizza night at home with friends this weekend and are considering ordering from a local pizzeria.
  - You want to gather all the necessary information before deciding what to order and whether to pick it up or have it delivered.
  - You have a variety of preferences among your friends, so you need to find options that cater to different tastes.
  goals:
  - Ask about the available pizza types.
  - Ask about the price of a Margarita pizza.
  - Ask about the available drinks and their prices.
  - Ask about the shop's operating hours.
  - Ask if they deliver.

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. I can help you with information about our menu, prices, hours, and delivery!"
  output:
    - pizza_types:
        type: string
        description: "A list of the pizza types offered by the shop."
    - margarita_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a Margarita pizza."
    - drinks_and_prices:
        type: string
        description: "A list of available drinks and their corresponding prices."
    - operating_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The shop's operating hours, including opening and closing times."
    - delivery_availability:
        type: string
        description: "Indication of whether the shop delivers or not."

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 20
  interaction_style:
  - single question
```

### What I added:
* **`is_starter: false`**: This just means the simulated user will send the first message, not the chatbot.
* **`fallback`**: A realistic message the bot might say if it doesn't understand the user.
* **`output`**: I created a field for every single one of your goals! Notice how `margarita_price` uses the `currency` type, which is perfect for extracting prices. 🍕💸
