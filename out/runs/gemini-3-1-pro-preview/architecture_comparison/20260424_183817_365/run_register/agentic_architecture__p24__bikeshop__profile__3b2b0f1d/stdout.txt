Here is the SENSEI profile tailored for testing the bike shop's virtual assistant. 

I have structured the file based on the SENSEI Profiles documentation (`sensei-chat-guide.md`). It uses the `context` section to strictly enforce the negative constraints (no appointments, no advanced repairs, etc.) and sets up the `chatbot.output` schema so that you can later write rules to validate the accuracy, terminology, and absence of unsolicited suggestions.

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_inquiry"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a normal customer looking for general information about a bike shop and its standard services.
    You phrase your questions clearly and directly.
  context:
    - "Do not ask about appointments."
    - "Do not ask about specific bike models."
    - "Do not ask about custom or advanced repairs."
    - "Do not ask about warranties or delivery services."
    - "Do not ask about any topic outside of basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices rather than advanced repairs."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't understand that. Could you please rephrase?"
  output:
    - opening_hours_provided:
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times provided by the chatbot."
    - tire_price:
        type: string
        description: "The quoted price for a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: string
        description: "The quoted price for a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: string
        description: "The quoted price for a new bike seat."
    - maintenance_advice:
        type: string
        description: "Summary of the basic maintenance advice provided by the chatbot."
    - unrelated_topics_mentioned:
        type: string
        description: "Any unrelated services or unsolicited suggestions introduced by the chatbot."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### How this meets your requirements:
* **User Persona & Behavior**: The `role` and `interaction_style: default` ensure the simulator acts like a normal customer asking clear, direct questions in English.
* **Negative Constraints**: The `context` list explicitly forbids the simulator from bringing up appointments, specific models, warranties, deliveries, or advanced repairs.
* **Specific Questions**: The `goals` section lists out the exact inquiries you requested (hours, three specific prices, and basic maintenance advice).
* **Evaluation Focus**: The `chatbot.output` section is designed to extract exactly what the chatbot answers for each goal, plus a specific field (`unrelated_topics_mentioned`) to catch if the bot introduces unsolicited services. This sets up a perfect contract for any SENSEI rules you write later to validate the bot's performance! 😊
