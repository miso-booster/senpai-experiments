Hello there! 😊 I would be absolutely delighted to explain what a metamorphic rule is in the world of Sensei! 

In traditional testing, you usually check if a specific input gives you one exact, expected output (like asking "What is 2+2?" and expecting "4"). But chatbots are dynamic, and sometimes we don't know the *exact* right answer in advance. This is known as the "oracle problem."

**Metamorphic rules** solve this by looking at the **relationship between multiple conversations** instead of just one! ✨

Instead of asking "Is the price exactly $15?", a metamorphic rule asks: "If I change the input in a specific way, how should the output change?" 

### 🍕 A Simple Everyday Analogy
Imagine you are testing a pizza-ordering chatbot. 
* **Conversation A:** You order a medium pizza with **2 toppings**.
* **Conversation B:** You order a medium pizza with **4 toppings**.

Even if you don't know the exact price of the pizzas, you know a fundamental rule: **Conversation B should cost more than Conversation A.** 

That is a metamorphic relation! You are comparing a pair of conversations that share similar traits (same size) but have one specific difference (number of toppings) to see if the chatbot's logic holds up.

### 🛠️ How it works in Sensei-check
When you write a metamorphic rule in Sensei (using a simple YAML file), you define three main things:
1. **Conversations:** How many conversations you want to compare (usually 2 or more).
2. **Filter (`when`):** The conditions that make the conversations comparable (e.g., "when the pizza sizes are the same").
3. **Relation (`if` / `then`):** The rule that must be true (e.g., "if pizza B has more toppings, then pizza B must cost more").

It’s a super clever and powerful way to make sure your chatbot behaves consistently and logically across different scenarios! 🚀
