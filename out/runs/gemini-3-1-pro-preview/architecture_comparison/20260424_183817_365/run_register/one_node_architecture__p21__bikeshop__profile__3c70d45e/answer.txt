Hello there! 😊 I'd be happy to help you create a new Sensei profile for your bikeshop project. 

This profile will simulate a customer who just got a new bike and wants to customize it by changing the seat and handlebar grips. I've set up the `user.context` and `user.goals` to reflect this specific scenario, and I've added corresponding fields in the `chatbot.output` so you can extract the shop's responses about customization, parts, and estimated time.

Here is the YAML for your new profile:

```yaml
test_name: bike_customization_setup

llm:
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  temperature: 0.6
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: A customer who recently bought a new bike and wants to customize it for better comfort and fit.
  context:
    - "You recently purchased a new bike but it doesn't feel quite right yet."
    - "You want to tailor it to your liking by changing the seat and the handlebar grips."
    - "You are looking for recommendations and want to know the cost and time required for these changes."
  goals:
    - "Ask if the shop offers custom bike setups or fittings."
    - "Inquire about options and prices for a new, more comfortable seat."
    - "Ask about options and prices for new handlebar grips."
    - "Ask how long it would take to install the new seat and grips."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - customization_offered:
        type: string
        description: "Confirmation from the chatbot regarding whether they offer custom bike setups or fittings."
    - seat_information:
        type: string
        description: "Information or pricing provided by the chatbot regarding new bike seats."
    - grips_information:
        type: string
        description: "Information or pricing provided by the chatbot regarding new handlebar grips."
    - estimated_time:
        type: string
        description: "The estimated time provided by the chatbot to complete the installation of the seat and grips."

conversation:
  number: 2
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - single question
```

You can save this as `bike_customization_setup.yaml` in your `profiles/` folder. Let me know if you'd like to add any specific variables (like different types of grips or seats) using the `forward()` or `random()` functions, or if you need help writing a Sensei rule to test this profile! 🚲✨
