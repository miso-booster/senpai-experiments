Based on the SENSEI Profiles documentation (`sensei-chat-guide.md`), there are a few formatting issues and missing required fields in your YAML that are preventing it from working correctly.

Here are the main issues I found:
1. **`llm.temperature`**: It was set to `"low"`, but it must be a float number (e.g., `0.2`).
2. **`chatbot.output` types**: 
   - `str` is not a valid default type; it should be `string`.
   - `money` is not a default type (unless you created a custom type for it in the `types/` folder). I've changed it to `float` to be safe.
   - `seat_price` and `care_bike` were completely missing the `type` field, which is required for extraction.
3. **`conversation.goal_style`**: This section was missing. Sensei needs to know when to stop the conversation (e.g., after a fixed number of steps, or when all goals are answered). I added `all_answered` with a safety limit.

Here is the corrected and fully working YAML:

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.2
  model: gpt-4o-mini

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: False
  fallback: I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: float
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: float
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: float
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      limit: 10
  interaction_style:
    - default
```
