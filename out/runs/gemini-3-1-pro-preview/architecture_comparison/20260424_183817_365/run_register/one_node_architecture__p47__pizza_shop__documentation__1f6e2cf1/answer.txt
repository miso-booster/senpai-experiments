In the context of Sensei-check, a **metamorphic rule** is a super clever way to test your chatbot by comparing **two or more conversations** against each other, rather than just looking at one conversation in isolation.

Normally, in traditional testing, you check if an exact input produces an exact output (like a math test). But chatbots can be unpredictable and their answers vary! Metamorphic testing solves this (what we call the "oracle problem") by checking the *relationship* between different conversations instead of looking for one absolute right answer.

**🍕 A Simple Everyday Analogy:**
Imagine you are testing a pizza-ordering chatbot. You might not know the exact price of every single pizza combination. However, you *do* know this logical rule: 
> *If two people order the exact same size of pizza, but one person adds more toppings, that person's pizza should cost more.*

A metamorphic rule in Sensei lets you write exactly that! It filters for comparable conversations (e.g., same size, same drinks) and asserts how the outputs should change when the inputs change.

Here is what that exact rule looks like in Sensei:

```yaml
name: toppings_monotonicity
description: Adding more toppings to a custom pizza costs more (same size/drink/qty)
conversations: 2
when: conv[0].size == conv[1].size and conv[0].drink == conv[1].drink and conv[0].number == conv[1].number
if:   len(conv[0].toppings) > len(conv[1].toppings)
then: extract_float(conv[0].price) > extract_float(conv[1].price)
```

In short: it’s all about testing the **logic and consistency** of your chatbot across multiple scenarios!
