Aquí tienes el código YAML de ese perfil para que lo revises de un vistazo ✨:

```yaml
test_name: Recipe Management and Cooking Support

llm:
  temperature: 0.9
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: A user looking for recipe assistance, meal planning, and ingredient management for cooking at home.
  context:
    - "personality: personalities/conversational-user"
    - "You're excited to explore new recipes as part of your commitment to healthier home cooking and meal planning."

    - "Balancing convenience with healthy eating, you're eager to enhance your culinary skills by finding the right resources and ingredients needed for your meals."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the capabilities of the 'La Cocina de Alcampo' feature."
    - "Inquire how to search for recipes by season and product category."
    - "Request information on adding ingredients from a selected recipe to a shopping list."
    - "Check if there are prepared vegetable mixes available for purchase."
    - "Ask for details about Alcampo's commitment to sustainable fishing practices, including certifications."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "🤩 ¡Qué ilusión ver tu entusiasmo por aprender! 🧑🏻‍🎓 En Alcampo tenemos una sección de libros muy variada 📚 —seguro que encuentras algo que te encante."

    - favorite_recipe_feature_info:
        type: string

    - prepared_vegetable_mixes_availability:
        type: string

  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 22

```

### 💡 Tip de Senpai:
Si quieres hacer este perfil aún más potente, podrías usar **variables** en la sección `goals` para probar diferentes tipos de dietas o ingredientes. Por ejemplo, podrías cambiar uno de los objetivos a:
`- "Ask for a recipe recommendation that is {{diet_type}} and uses {{main_ingredient}}"`
Y luego definir esas variables usando funciones como `forward()` o `random()` para que Sensei pruebe combinaciones como "vegan" + "tofu" o "gluten-free" + "chicken".
