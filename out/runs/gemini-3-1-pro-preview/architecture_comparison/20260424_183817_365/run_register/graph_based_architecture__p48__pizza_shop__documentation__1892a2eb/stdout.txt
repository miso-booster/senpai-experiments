Hello there! 😊 I'd be happy to help you with SENSEI PROFILES! 

When creating your YAML profile, you actually have a lot of flexibility. While you need the core structure to tell the simulated user what to do, there are several fields that are **not mandatory**:

*   **The entire `llm` block:** Believe it or not, you don't have to define the `llm` section at all! If you leave it out, Sensei will simply apply its default settings (like a default model and temperature) and assume a standard text format. 🤖
*   **`user.context.personality`:** You aren't required to link to a separate personality YAML file in your `personalities/` folder. You can just write freeform text directly in the `context` list to describe the user's situation!
*   **Cost limits (`conversation.max_cost` & `goal_style.max_cost`):** These are optional safety caps for your budget. You can set them to limit how much you spend on the entire run or per individual conversation, but they are completely optional. 💸
*   **`interaction_style`:** If you don't want to test specific user behaviors (like making spelling mistakes, writing long phrases, or changing languages), you can just use `default` or omit complex styles for a standard, natural conversation flow.
*   **`goal_style.all_answered.export` and `limit`:** If you choose the `all_answered` goal style, the `export` flag (which saves a CSV dataframe) and the `limit` (which caps the number of turns) are optional. If you skip the limit, Sensei will automatically default to a safety stop of 30 turns. 🛑

**A quick tip:** While `chatbot.fallback` is technically something you *can* skip, it is **highly recommended** to include your real bot's fallback message there. It prevents the simulator from getting stuck in endless loops if the bot doesn't understand something! 👀
