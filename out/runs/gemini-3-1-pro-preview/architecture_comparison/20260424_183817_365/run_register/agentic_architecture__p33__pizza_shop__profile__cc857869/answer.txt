Based on the SENSEI documentation (specifically the `sensei-chat-guide.md`), the `chatbot` field is crucial because it defines the contract for what the simulator expects from the bot and what information it should extract at the end of the conversation.

Here is what we need to add to the `chatbot` section:
1. **`is_starter`**: A boolean (`true` or `false`) indicating whether the chatbot sends the first message. Usually, this is `false` unless your bot has a proactive greeting.
2. **`fallback`**: The exact fallback message your real chatbot uses when it doesn't understand something (e.g., "I'm sorry, I didn't understand that."). This helps the simulator avoid endless loops.
3. **`output`**: A schema of fields to extract at the end of the conversation. Since your user's goals are to ask about pizza types, the price of a Margarita, drinks, shop hours, and delivery, we should define outputs to capture those specific details.
