Hi there! 😊 I would absolutely love to help you with that! Making sure your chatbot speaks the user's language is super important for a smooth and welcoming user experience. 

In **Sensei-check**, we actually have a handy built-in helper function made exactly for this called `responds_in_same_language()`. 

Because this rule just looks at the chat history and doesn't rely on specific extracted data (like a pizza size or a price), it's considered a **global rule**. That means you can use it across any of your SENSEI Chat profiles without needing to set up special `chatbot.output` fields! 🙌

Here is the ready-to-use YAML snippet for your rule:

```yaml
name: match_user_language
description: Ensures the assistant replies in the same language the user speaks.
conversations: 1
oracle: responds_in_same_language() is True
```

### A quick breakdown of how it works:
* **`conversations: 1`**: This tells Sensei-check to look at each conversation individually (as a traditional oracle) rather than comparing multiple conversations together.
* **`oracle: responds_in_same_language() is True`**: This uses our LLM-backed helper to analyze the dialogue turns and verify that the assistant's language matches the user's language.

Just save that into your rules folder, and Sensei-check will handle the rest! 🕵️‍♀️✨
