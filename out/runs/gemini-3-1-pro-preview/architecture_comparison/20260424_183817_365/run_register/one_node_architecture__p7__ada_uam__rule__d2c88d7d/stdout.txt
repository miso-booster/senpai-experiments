Here is a rule to verify that the Ada UAM chatbot provides the correct contact information for the CAU! 😊

Since the physical address is quite long and the chatbot might extract it with slight variations (like translating "Higher Polytechnic School" to Spanish or changing the formatting), I recommend using the `semantic_content` helper for the address. For the email and phone number, a simple inclusion check (`in`) works perfectly.

Here is the YAML for your rule:

```yaml
name: cau_correct_information
description: Checks that the CAU contact information (email, phone, and address) is correct.
conversations: 1
active: true
oracle: |
  'cau@uam.es' in contact_email and '91 497 4029' in contact_phone and semantic_content(physical_address, 'Higher Polytechnic School, C/ Francisco Tomás y Valiente nº 11, Building B, first floor, office TI-107')
yields: f"Incorrect CAU info. Got Email: {contact_email}, Phone: {contact_phone}, Address: {physical_address}"
```

### How it works:
* **`conversations: 1`**: This is a standard single-conversation oracle because we are checking absolute facts rather than comparing multiple conversations.
* **`'cau@uam.es' in contact_email`**: Ensures the correct email is present in the extracted string.
* **`'91 497 4029' in contact_phone`**: Ensures the correct phone number is present.
* **`semantic_content(...)`**: Uses an LLM under the hood to verify that the extracted `physical_address` means the same thing as the expected address, making your test much more robust against minor formatting differences! 

Just save this in your `rules/` folder (for example, as `rules/cau_correct_information.yaml`) and you'll be good to go! Let me know if you need any adjustments. 👀
