Since you requested **no variables**, I have kept the `user.goals` as plain, straightforward sentences. This is a fantastic way to do a quick and easy test to see if your chatbot understands general questions about subscriptions and benefits. 📝✨

Here is a complete, ready-to-run Sensei Profile tailored to your request:

```yaml
test_name: "subscription_services_benefits"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a frequent shopper who is curious about joining the store's subscription program. 
    You want to know if the perks and benefits make it worth the cost.
  context:
    - "You are looking for ways to save money on delivery fees."
    - "You want clear, straightforward answers about what the subscription includes."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to explain the available subscription services."
    - "Inquire about the main benefits and perks of becoming a subscribed member."
    - "Ask if there is a free trial available for the subscription."
    - "Find out the monthly or annual cost of the subscription."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you please rephrase your question?"
  output:
    - subscription_names:
        type: string
        description: "Names of the subscription plans mentioned by the bot"
    - subscription_benefits:
        type: string
        description: "List of benefits and perks included in the subscription"
    - subscription_cost:
        type: string
        description: "The pricing details for the subscription"
    - free_trial_available:
        type: string
        description: "Information on whether a free trial is offered"

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    steps: 8
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### 👀 A quick look at what's happening here:
* **`user.goals`**: Just plain text! Sensei will read these instructions and naturally weave them into the conversation one by one.
* **`chatbot.output`**: I added a few fields here so that at the end of the conversation, Sensei will extract the exact subscription names, benefits, and costs the bot provided. This is super helpful if you want to write **Sensei Rules** later to verify the bot gave the correct price! 💸
* **`conversation`**: It will run exactly 1 conversation for 8 steps using a natural (`default`) interaction style.

You can save this as `subscription_benefits.yml` inside your `profiles/` folder and run it right away.
