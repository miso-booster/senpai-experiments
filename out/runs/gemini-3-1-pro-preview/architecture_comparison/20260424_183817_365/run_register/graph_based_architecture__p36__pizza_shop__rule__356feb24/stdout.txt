The reason your rule isn't working is that it is missing the **`if`** clause! 

In Sensei-check, when you use a `then` statement to define a metamorphic relation, it must always be paired with an `if` statement. Right now, your rule says *"when the size and drink are the same... THEN the price is greater."* But it forgot to specify *what* triggers that higher price (which, based on your description, is having more toppings 🍕).

Here is the corrected version of your rule:

```yaml
name: more_toppings_cost_more
description: Adding more toppings to a custom pizza costs more
active: true
conversations: 2
when: conv[0].size == conv[1].size and conv[0].drink == conv[1].drink
if: len(conv[0].toppings) > len(conv[1].toppings)
then: extract_float(conv[0].price) > extract_float(conv[1].price)
```

### Why this works:
* **`when`**: This is your filter. It ensures we only compare apples to apples (or in this case, pizzas of the same `size` and `drink`).
* **`if`**: This is your precondition. It looks for a pair of conversations where one order has *more* toppings than the other.
* **`then`**: This is your expected relation. It asserts that the pizza with more toppings must have a higher `price`.
