Here is a SENSEI Chat profile tailored to your scenario. I've included a couple of variables (`bike_type` and `body_part`) so that the simulator generates varied conversations (e.g., lower back pain on a road bike, or knee pain on a mountain bike). 

I also added an explicit instruction in the `context` to ensure the simulated user doesn't ask to book an appointment, while still asking for advice.

```yaml
test_name: "bike_discomfort_advice"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a cyclist experiencing discomfort while riding your bike. You are unsure if the issue is due to a poor bike fit or if some of your components are worn out.
  context:
    - "You want advice on possible adjustments or services the shop offers to fix your discomfort."
    - "You must NOT explicitly ask to book an appointment."
    - "You are riding a {{bike_type}} bike."
    - "The discomfort is mainly in your {{body_part}}."
  goals:
    - "Explain your discomfort in your {{body_part}} while riding your {{bike_type}} bike."
    - "Ask if the issue might be related to bike fit or worn components."
    - "Inquire about what adjustments or services the shop can offer to help."
    - bike_type:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [road, mountain, gravel, hybrid]
    - body_part:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [lower back, knees, neck, saddle area, wrists]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you rephrase?"
  output:
    - suggested_service:
        type: string
        description: "The service or adjustment suggested by the chatbot (e.g., bike fit, component replacement)"
    - appointment_offered:
        type: string
        description: "Whether the chatbot proactively offered to book an appointment (yes/no)"

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### 📝 Summary of the Profile

*   **Role & Context:** The user is instructed to act as a cyclist with discomfort, specifically asking for advice on adjustments or services without asking to book an appointment.
*   **Goals & Variables:** The simulator will randomly pick a `bike_type` and a `body_part` for each conversation to test how the chatbot handles different types of complaints.
*   **Chatbot Output:** At the end of the conversation, the profile extracts two fields:
    *   `suggested_service`: To see what advice the chatbot gave.
    *   `appointment_offered`: To check if the chatbot took the initiative to offer an appointment, even though the user didn't explicitly ask for one. This is great for writing validation rules later!
*   **Conversation Controls:** It will run 5 conversations (`number: 5`) and will stop naturally once all the goals are answered (`all_answered`).
