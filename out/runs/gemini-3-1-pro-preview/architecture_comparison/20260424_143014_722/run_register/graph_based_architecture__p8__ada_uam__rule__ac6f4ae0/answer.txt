### What did we change and why? 🛠️

1. **Removed the `conv[0].` prefix:**
   In Sensei-check, the `conv[i]` syntax (like `conv[0]` or `conv[1]`) is specifically used for **metamorphic rules** where you are comparing multiple conversations at once (i.e., when `conversations: 2` or more). Since your rule is a traditional oracle checking a single conversation (`conversations: 1`), you don't need the array prefix! You can just call the fields directly by their names: `contact_email`, `contact_phone`, etc.

2. **Removed the `when` clause (The Tautology Trap!):**
   In your original rule, your `when` filter and your `oracle` condition were exactly the same. 
   * Think of `when` as the bouncer at a club: it filters *which* conversations get tested. 
   * Think of `oracle` as the test itself.
   If you tell the bouncer, *"Only let in conversations that have the correct CAU email and phone,"* and then your test asks, *"Does this conversation have the correct CAU email and phone?"*... well, it will always pass! But it will completely ignore any conversations where the chatbot made a mistake. By removing the `when` clause, we ensure that **all** conversations generated for this profile are actually tested by the oracle.
