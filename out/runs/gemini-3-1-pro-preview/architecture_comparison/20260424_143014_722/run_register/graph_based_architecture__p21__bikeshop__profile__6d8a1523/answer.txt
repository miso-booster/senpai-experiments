Getting a new bike tailored to your liking is such a relatable scenario, and it makes for a fantastic test case! 🚴‍♂️✨ 

To make this work in Sensei, we will create a **Profile** (a YAML file) that defines this specific user persona. We'll set up the `user.context` to explain their situation (new bike, wants a new seat and grips) and list out their `user.goals` so the simulator knows exactly what to ask the chatbot. We will also define the `chatbot.output` so Sensei can extract the shop's answers for you to validate later.

Here is a ready-to-use YAML profile tailored for your scenario. You can save this as `bike_setup_and_customization.yml` inside your `profiles/` folder:

```yaml
test_name: "bike_setup_and_customization"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer who recently bought a new bike and wants to get it tailored to their liking."
  context:
    - "You recently got a new bike and want to make it more comfortable."
    - "You specifically want to change the seat and the handlebar grips."
    - "You need to know if the shop offers this setup service and what the options or prices are."
  goals:
    - "Ask if the shop offers bike setup and customization services."
    - "Inquire about the options and prices for new bike seats."
    - "Inquire about the options and prices for new handlebar grips."
    - "Ask to schedule an appointment to get the seat and grips changed."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - setup_service_offered:
        type: string
        description: "Confirmation of whether the shop offers bike setup and customization services."
    - seat_information:
        type: string
        description: "Information or pricing provided by the chatbot about bike seats."
    - grips_information:
        type: string
        description: "Information or pricing provided by the chatbot about handlebar grips."
    - appointment_scheduled:
        type: string
        description: "Details of the scheduled appointment for the bike setup, if successful."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### 🧐 A quick breakdown of what's happening here:
* **`user.context` & `user.goals`**: This is the brain of our simulated user! It tells Sensei exactly *why* they are chatting and *what* they need to achieve (asking about services, seats, grips, and booking an appointment).
* **`chatbot.output`**: At the end of the conversation, Sensei will extract these specific fields. This is super helpful if you want to write **Sensei Rules** later to verify that the bot actually provided prices or successfully booked the appointment! 📝
* **`goal_style: all_answered`**: This ensures the simulated user won't stop chatting until the bot has addressed all of their goals (or until it hits the safety limit of 15 messages).
