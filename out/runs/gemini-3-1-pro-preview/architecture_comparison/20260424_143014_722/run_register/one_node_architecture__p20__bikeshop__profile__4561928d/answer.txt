Hello there! 😊 I'd be happy to help you create a profile to test how your bike shop chatbot handles appointment scheduling. 

To test the dates and times thoroughly, I've set up a profile where the user wants to change their wheels and asks for specific days and times. I used the `forward()` function for the `appointment_day` and nested another `forward()` for the `appointment_time`. By setting `conversation.number` to `combinations`, Sensei will automatically generate a test matrix covering all the different day and time pairings! 🚲🗓️

Here is your ready-to-use profile:

```yaml
test_name: "wheel_change_appointment"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a cyclist whose bike wheels are worn out and need to be replaced.
    You want to schedule an appointment at the bike shop to get them changed.
  context:
    - "You rely on your bike for commuting, so you need the replacement done soon."
    - "You have specific days and times you are available."
  goals:
    - "Ask if they can replace your bike wheels."
    - "Request to schedule the appointment for {{appointment_day}} at {{appointment_time}}."
    - appointment_day:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: ["Monday", "Wednesday", "Friday", "next Saturday"]
    - appointment_time:
        function: forward(appointment_day)
        type: string
        data: ["09:00 AM", "01:30 PM", "05:00 PM"]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you repeat?"
  output:
    - confirmed_day:
        type: string
        description: "The day of the appointment confirmed by the chatbot."
    - confirmed_time:
        type: time("hh:mm a")
        description: "The time of the appointment confirmed by the chatbot."
    - service_type:
        type: string
        description: "The service confirmed by the chatbot (e.g., wheel replacement)."

conversation:
  number: combinations
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### A quick tip from Senpai 👀:
Because we are extracting `confirmed_day` and `confirmed_time` in the `chatbot.output` section, you can easily write **Sensei Rules** later to verify that the bot actually booked the correct day and time requested by the user!
