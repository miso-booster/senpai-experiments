Since you requested **no variables**, we will keep the `user.goals` section super clean and straightforward using only plain text. This is a great way to do a quick "smoke test" to see how well your chatbot explains its subscription plans and perks! 🚀

Here is a complete, ready-to-use Sensei Profile for asking about subscription services and benefits:

```yaml
test_name: "subscription_services_benefits"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "You are a potential customer interested in signing up for a subscription service."
  context:
    - "You want to know if the subscription will save you money in the long run."
    - "You prefer clear, straightforward answers about benefits and cancellation policies."
  goals:
    - "Ask what subscription services or plans are currently available."
    - "Inquire about the main benefits and perks of becoming a subscriber."
    - "Ask if there is a free trial or a discount for the first month."
    - "Find out what the cancellation process looks like."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you repeat your question about our services?"
  output:
    - available_plans:
        type: string
        description: "The subscription plans mentioned by the bot"
    - main_benefits:
        type: string
        description: "The perks or benefits of the subscription"
    - trial_info:
        type: string
        description: "Information regarding free trials or initial discounts"
    - cancellation_policy:
        type: string
        description: "How the user can cancel their subscription"

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### 👀 A quick look at what's happening here:
* **`user.goals`**: Just simple, direct sentences! The simulated user will ask these questions naturally during the conversation.
* **`chatbot.output`**: At the end of the chat, Sensei will extract the plans, benefits, trial info, and cancellation policy so you can easily review what your bot answered (or use them later in Sensei Rules!).
* **`goal_style: all_answered`**: This tells Sensei to keep chatting until all your goals have been addressed by the bot, with a safety limit of 15 steps.
* **`interaction_style: single questions`**: The user will ask one thing at a time, making it easier for the chatbot to digest and answer clearly.
