To check the exact price of a small pizza, we will create a **single-conversation rule** (also known as an oracle). This rule will filter the conversations to only look at the ones where the user ordered a "small" pizza, and then it will verify that the extracted price is exactly $10.

Here is the YAML for your rule:

```yaml
name: small_pizza_price_exact
description: Checks that the price of a small pizza is exactly $10.
conversations: 1
active: true
when: size == 'small'
oracle: extract_float(price) == 10 and currency(price) == 'USD'
on-error: f"Expected small pizza to cost $10, but got {price}"
```

### 👀 How it works:
* **`conversations: 1`**: Tells Sensei-check that we are evaluating one conversation at a time.
* **`when: size == 'small'`**: This acts as a filter so the rule only runs if the `size` field in your profile's `chatbot.output` is "small".
* **`oracle: ...`**: This is the condition that must be true! We use the handy `extract_float()` helper to grab the number from the text, and `currency()` to ensure it's in US Dollars. 

Just make sure that your SENSEI Chat profile exposes `size` and `price` in its `chatbot.output` section (which looks like it does in your `pizza_order_test_custom` profile!). 🍕
