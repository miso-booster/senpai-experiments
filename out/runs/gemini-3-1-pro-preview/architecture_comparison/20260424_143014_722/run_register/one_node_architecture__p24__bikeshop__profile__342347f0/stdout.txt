To make sure the simulated user behaves exactly as you described, I've placed your negative constraints (like not asking about appointments or specific models) directly into the `user.context` section. I also set up the `chatbot.output` section to extract the specific prices and advice the bot gives, which is perfect if you want to run Sensei-check rules on them later! 🚲

Here is the complete YAML profile. You can save this in your `profiles/` folder (for example, as `bike_shop_general_info.yml`):

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_info"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer looking for general information about a bike shop and its standard services.
  context:
    - "You must behave like a normal customer and phrase questions clearly and directly."
    - "Do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, or any topic outside of basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you rephrase?"
  output:
    - shop_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - tire_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice provided by the chatbot on how to properly take care of a bike."

conversation:
  number: 2
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### A few quick notes on this setup 👀:
* **`goal_style: all_answered`**: This ensures the conversation won't end until the user has successfully asked all the questions in the `goals` list.
* **`type: currency`**: I used the built-in `currency` type for the prices so that Sensei will automatically extract the numeric value and the currency symbol (like `$15.00` or `20 EUR`).
* **`conversation.number: 2`**: It will run 2 conversations to give you a slightly broader test sample, but you can easily change this to `1` or more!
