That’s a great scenario to test! Chatbots often struggle when users change their minds mid-conversation, so it's a perfect use case for Sensei. 

To achieve this, we can use a combination of explicit instructions in the `user.context` and `user.goals`, along with the built-in `change your mind` option in the `interaction_style` section. This will prompt the simulated user to start with a custom pizza, naturally hesitate or backtrack, and finally settle on a predefined one.

Here is a ready-to-use profile for your project:

```yaml
test_name: "change_mind_custom_to_predefined"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A hungry but indecisive customer ordering a pizza."
  context:
    - "You initially want to build your own custom pizza."
    - "Halfway through the conversation, you change your mind and decide to order a predefined pizza from the menu instead."
  goals:
    - "Start by ordering a custom pizza with {{toppings}}."
    - "Change your mind and ask for a {{predefined_pizza}} pizza instead."
    - "Ask for the total price of the new order."
    - toppings:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: 
          - "extra cheese and mushrooms"
          - "pepperoni, onions, and olives"
          - "bacon and extra tomato sauce"
    - predefined_pizza:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: 
          - "Margherita"
          - "Pepperoni"
          - "Vegetarian"
          - "Hawaiian"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you repeat your order?"
  output:
    - final_pizza_ordered:
        type: string
        description: "The final pizza type the user decided to order after changing their mind."
    - total_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The final price of the order."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
```

### 👀 Why this works:
1. **`interaction_style: [change your mind]`**: This built-in feature specifically instructs the Sensei user simulator to act indecisively and alter its choices during the chat.
2. **Context & Goals**: We explicitly tell the user simulator *how* to change its mind (starting with the `{{toppings}}` variable for the custom pizza, and switching to the `{{predefined_pizza}}` variable).
3. **Output Extraction**: The `chatbot.output` is set up to extract the `final_pizza_ordered` so you can verify that the chatbot successfully updated the order and didn't charge them for the custom pizza!
