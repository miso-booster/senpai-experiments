Hello there! 😊 I would be absolutely delighted to help you create this Sensei Profile. 

Based on your description, we need a profile where the simulated user asks clear, direct questions about shop hours, specific prices (tires, tune-ups, seats), and basic bike care, while strictly avoiding topics like appointments or advanced repairs. 

Here is the complete, ready-to-run YAML profile tailored exactly to your needs:

```yaml
test_name: "general_bike_shop_inquiry"

llm:
  temperature: 0.4
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer looking for general information about the bike shop and its standard services. 
    You behave like a normal customer and phrase your questions clearly and directly.
  context:
    - "Do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, or any topic outside of basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - "Ask about the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask about the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask about the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - shop_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - tire_price:
        type: string
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: string
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: string
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice provided by the chatbot on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### 🚲 A quick breakdown of how this works:
* **`user.context`**: This is where we place your negative constraints. By explicitly telling the LLM *not* to ask about warranties, appointments, or specific models, we keep the conversation tightly focused on your goals.
* **`user.goals`**: We listed your exact requirements here. The user will naturally work through asking about the hours, the three specific prices, and the maintenance advice.
* **`chatbot.output`**: At the end of the conversation, Sensei will extract these specific fields. This is super helpful because you can later write **Sensei Rules** to verify that the `tire_price` or `tune_up_price` isn't empty, ensuring the chatbot actually provided the pricing! *(Note: I used `string` for the prices to keep it simple, but if you have a custom `currency` or `money` type defined in your `types/` folder, you can easily swap that in!)*
* **`goal_style.all_answered`**: This tells Sensei to keep the conversation going until all the goals have been addressed, with a safety limit of 15 steps. Setting `export: true` will also generate a handy CSV file showing which goals were successfully met.

Just save this as a `.yml` file inside your project's `profiles/` folder, and you'll be ready to test! Let me know if you need any tweaks or if you'd like to learn how to write some Rules to validate these outputs. Happy testing! 🚀👀
