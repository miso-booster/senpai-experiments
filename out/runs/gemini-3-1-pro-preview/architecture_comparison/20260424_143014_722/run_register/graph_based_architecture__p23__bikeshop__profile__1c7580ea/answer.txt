I took a look at your YAML, and there are just a few small tweaks needed to get it running perfectly with Sensei. Here is a quick summary of what was causing the hiccups:

1. **`llm.temperature`**: This needs to be a number (a float) rather than a word. So instead of `"low"`, we can use `0.0` or `0.2`.
2. **Missing `type` in outputs**: Every item in `chatbot.output` needs a defined `type`. `seat_price` and `care_bike` were missing theirs!
3. **`str` vs `string`**: The default text type in Sensei is `string`, so we just need to change `str` to `string` for `opening_and_closing`.
4. **Missing `goal_style`**: Sensei needs to know when to stop the conversation! We have to add a `goal_style` (like `all_answered` or `steps: 5`) under the `conversation` section.
5. **A quick note on `money`**: I see you are using `type: money` for the tire and tune-up. That is totally fine as long as you have a custom type file named `money.yml` inside your `types/` folder! If you don't, you might want to change those to `string` or `float`.

Here is the corrected and ready-to-use profile:

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.0  # Changed from "low" to a float
  model: gpt-4o-mini

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string  # Changed from 'str'
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: money  # Make sure you have a types/money.yml file!
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: money
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: string  # Added missing type
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: string  # Added missing type
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:  # Added so Sensei knows when to finish the chat!
      export: true
      limit: 10
  interaction_style:
    - default
```
