Since you want to verify that the virtual assistant returns specific, fixed values for a single chat, we can create a **single-conversation oracle** rule. 

To make this super robust, we'll use a couple of handy built-in helpers from the Sensei-check library:
* `extract_float()`: This is perfect for grabbing the exact number from your price fields (`tune_up`, `seat_price`, and `tire`), even if the bot adds currency symbols or extra text.
* `semantic_content()`: Since opening hours can be phrased in many ways (like "10 AM to 8 PM" or "10:00 - 20:00"), this LLM-backed helper will smartly check if the meaning matches "10 to 20".

Here is the ready-to-use YAML rule for your project:

```yaml
name: bike_shop_expected_values
description: Verifies that the bike shop returns the correct prices for items and the proper opening hours.
conversations: 1
oracle: extract_float(tune_up) == 50 and extract_float(seat_price) == 20 and extract_float(tire) == 20 and semantic_content(opening_and_closing, '10 to 20')
```
