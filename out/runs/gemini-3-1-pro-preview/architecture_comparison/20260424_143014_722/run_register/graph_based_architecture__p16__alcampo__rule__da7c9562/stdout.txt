### 2. Metamorphic Rule: Price Monotonicity 🥛
In the real world, specialized items like lactose-free products usually cost the same or slightly more than their regular counterparts. This metamorphic rule compares *two* conversations asking for the exact same product, ensuring the lactose-free version isn't somehow cheaper than the regular one!

```yaml
name: lactose_free_price_monotonicity
description: A lactose-free product should cost the same or more than its regular counterpart.
conversations: 2
when: conv[0].product_name == conv[1].product_name
```
