To make the simulated user change their mind during the conversation, Sensei has a super handy built-in feature just for this! We can use the **`change your mind`** option inside the `interaction_style` section. When you use this, the user simulator will naturally alter its decisions mid-conversation.

Here is a complete, ready-to-run YAML profile tailored exactly to what you need. The user will start by asking for a custom pizza and then pivot to a predefined one:

```yaml
test_name: "pizza_change_mind_test"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a hungry customer ordering dinner. You initially want to build your own custom pizza, 
    but halfway through the process, you decide it's too much work and change your mind to order 
    a predefined pizza instead.
  context:
    - "You are a bit indecisive today."
  goals:
    - "Start by ordering a custom pizza with {{custom_toppings}}."
    - "Change your mind and order a predefined {{predefined_pizza}} pizza instead."
    - "Ask for the final price in {{currency}}."
    - custom_toppings:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [extra cheese and mushrooms, pepperoni and olives, ham and pineapple]
    - predefined_pizza:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [Margherita, Pepperoni, Hawaiian, Four Cheese]
    - currency:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [USD, EUR]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, it's a bit loud in the kitchen. Could you repeat your order?"
  output:
    - final_pizza_ordered:
        type: string
        description: "The actual pizza the user ended up ordering after changing their mind."
    - total_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The final price of the order."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
```

### 🧐 Why does this work so well?

1. **`interaction_style: [change your mind]`**: This is the magic ingredient! 🪄 It tells the Sensei user simulator to actively rethink its choices during the chat. It's perfect for testing if your chatbot can gracefully handle a user who scraps their current order to start a new one.
2. **The `role` and `goals`**: We explicitly tell the user persona *how* we want them to change their mind (from custom to predefined). Sensei will use the `custom_toppings` first, and then switch over to the `predefined_pizza` variable.
3. **`chatbot.output`**: At the end of the conversation, Sensei will extract the `final_pizza_ordered` so you can verify that your chatbot successfully registered the *new* predefined pizza instead of the canceled custom one! 

Just save this as a `.yml` file inside your `profiles/` folder, and you're good to go! Let me know if you need help running it or if you'd like to add any Sensei Rules to check the outputs! 😊🙌
