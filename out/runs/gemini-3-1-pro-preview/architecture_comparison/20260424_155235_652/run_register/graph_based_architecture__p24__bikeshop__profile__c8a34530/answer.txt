Hello there! 😊 I would be absolutely delighted to help you put together this Sensei Profile. 

Based on your description, we want a straightforward, clear-spoken customer who sticks strictly to the basics: hours, standard prices, and general bike care. We'll use the `context` section to enforce the negative constraints (no appointments, no specific models, etc.) and the `chatbot.output` section to extract exactly what we are testing for.

Since I noticed your project already uses a custom `money` type in other profiles, I've included it here for the price extractions! 🚲✨

Here is the complete YAML for your new profile:

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_qa"

llm:
  temperature: 0.5
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a normal customer looking for general information about a bike shop and its standard services. 
    You phrase your questions clearly and directly.
  context:
    - "Do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, or delivery services."
    - "Stick strictly to basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - shop_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice provided on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance."

conversation:
  number: 2
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### 👀 A quick breakdown of what we did:
* **`user.context`**: This is where we placed your strict boundaries. Telling the LLM exactly what *not* to talk about is the best way to keep the simulated user on track.
* **`chatbot.output`**: We defined specific fields for the hours, the three prices, and the maintenance advice. This will allow you to easily write Sensei-check rules later (e.g., `conv[0].tire_price is not None`) to verify the bot didn't dodge the pricing questions.
* **`interaction_style: [single questions]`**: This ensures the user asks one thing at a time, mimicking a natural, step-by-step inquiry rather than dumping all five questions into a single massive message.
* **`goal_style.all_answered`**: The conversation will naturally conclude once the chatbot has successfully provided answers to all the goals.
