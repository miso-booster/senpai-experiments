Since we want to verify that the chatbot provides the exact, correct information in a specific interaction, we will create a **single-conversation rule** (`conversations: 1`). 

To make our rule robust, we can use a mix of exact matching (for the email) and the `semantic_content()` helper function for the address and phone. The `semantic_content` helper is super handy here because the chatbot might format the address or phone number slightly differently (like adding a "+34" country code or translating "first floor" to Spanish), and this helper uses an LLM under the hood to check if the meaning matches your expected text! 🧠✨

Here is the ready-to-use YAML rule for your project:

```yaml
name: check_cau_information
description: Verifies that Ada UAM provides the correct CAU location, email, and phone number.
conversations: 1
oracle: contact_email == 'cau@uam.es' and semantic_content(contact_phone, '91 497 4029') and semantic_content(physical_address, 'Higher Polytechnic School, C/ Francisco Tomás y Valiente nº 11, Building B, first floor, office TI-107')
```

### How it works:
* **`conversations: 1`**: Tells Sensei-check to evaluate this oracle on individual conversations.
* **`contact_email == 'cau@uam.es'`**: Does a strict check to ensure the email is exactly right.
* **`semantic_content(...)`**: Flexibly checks that the `contact_phone` and `physical_address` outputs from your profile match the facts you provided, even if the bot phrases them a little differently.
