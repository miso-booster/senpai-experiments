Since you want to focus purely on asking about subscription services and benefits without using any variables, we can keep the `user.goals` section nice and simple with plain text instructions. 

Here is a complete, ready-to-run YAML profile you can save in your `profiles/` folder (for example, as `subscription_benefits.yml`):

```yaml
test_name: "subscription_services_and_benefits"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a curious customer who is considering signing up for the company's subscription service. 
    You want to make sure it's worth the money before committing.
  context:
    - "You are looking for ways to save money on regular purchases or deliveries."
    - "You value clear information about cancellation policies."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to explain the available subscription services."
    - "Inquire about the specific benefits, perks, or discounts included in the subscription."
    - "Ask about the pricing and whether there are monthly or annual billing options."
    - "Find out how easy it is to cancel or pause the subscription if you change your mind."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you rephrase your question about our services?"
  output:
    - subscription_plans:
        type: list[string]
        description: "The names of the subscription plans offered by the bot."
    - benefits_summary:
        type: string
        description: "A summary of the perks and benefits of subscribing."
    - pricing_info:
        type: string
        description: "Details about how much the subscriptions cost."
    - cancellation_policy:
        type: string
        description: "Information provided about how to cancel or pause the service."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### A quick breakdown of what's happening here 👀:
* **`user.goals`**: As requested, these are just straightforward sentences. Sensei will read these and naturally guide the conversation to achieve each one without needing any complex data lists or variables.
* **`chatbot.output`**: I added a few fields here like `benefits_summary` and `pricing_info`. Once the conversation ends, Sensei will extract this information so you can easily verify if your chatbot gave the right answers! 📝
* **`goal_style`**: I set it to `all_answered`. This means Sensei will keep chatting until all four of your goals have been addressed by the bot (or until it hits the safety limit of 15 steps).

Just drop this into your project, and you'll be ready to test how well your bot sells those subscriptions! Let me know if you need help tweaking anything else. Happy testing! 😊🥋
