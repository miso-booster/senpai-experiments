To check the exact price of a small pizza in a single conversation, we can use a **single-conversation oracle**. We'll use the `when` filter to make sure we are only checking conversations where the pizza size is "small", and then use the `extract_float` and `currency` helpers in the `oracle` to verify the price is exactly $10.

Here is the rule ready for your project:

```yaml
name: small_pizza_price_exact
description: The price of a small pizza must be exactly $10
conversations: 1
when: size == 'small'
oracle: extract_float(price) == 10 and currency(price) == 'USD'
```

### 👀 A quick tip:
Make sure that the fields `size` and `price` exactly match the names you defined in the `chatbot.output` section of your SENSEI Chat profile.
