Hello there! 😊 I'd be happy to help you create this profile for your bike shop's virtual assistant. 

To make sure the simulated user behaves exactly as you described, I've added your specific constraints (like avoiding appointments or advanced repairs) directly into the `user.context`. I also set up the `chatbot.output` to extract the prices as `currency` types and the advice/hours as `string` types, which will be super helpful if you want to write Sensei-check rules for them later! 🚲

Here is the complete YAML profile:

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_info"

llm:
  temperature: 0.5
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A normal customer looking for general information about the bike shop and its standard services."
  context:
    - "You phrase your questions clearly and directly."
    - "Focus on basic maintenance and good practices rather than advanced repairs."
    - "Do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, or any topic outside of basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, it's a little loud in the shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - shop_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - tire_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of a new bike seat."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice provided on how to properly take care of a bike."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### A quick breakdown of what's happening here: 👀
* **`user.context`**: This acts as the guardrails for the LLM simulating the user. It explicitly tells it what *not* to talk about, ensuring it stays on topic.
* **`interaction_style: [single questions]`**: This ensures the user asks one thing at a time rather than dumping all the questions into a single massive paragraph, which feels much more like a natural customer interaction.
* **`chatbot.output`**: Once the conversation finishes, Sensei will extract the prices and advice so you can easily verify if the chatbot gave accurate information without introducing unrelated services!
