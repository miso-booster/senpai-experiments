In the world of Sensei-check, a **metamorphic rule** is a super clever way to test your chatbot by comparing *multiple* conversations against each other, rather than just looking at one conversation in isolation. 

Instead of needing to know the *exact* right answer for every single scenario (which is often really hard and called the "oracle problem"), metamorphic rules let you check the **relationship** between inputs and outputs. 

**Let's use a simple pizza analogy! 🍕**
Imagine you don't know the exact menu prices of a pizza shop, but you do know how the world works. 
* **Conversation A:** You order a medium pizza with 2 toppings.
* **Conversation B:** You order a medium pizza with 4 toppings.

Even without knowing the exact price of either order, a **metamorphic rule** can assert: *"If the size is the same, the pizza with more toppings must cost more."* 📈

In Sensei, you write these rules in YAML using a very natural `when`, `if`, and `then` structure:
* **`when`**: Filters the conversations to find comparable ones (e.g., "when the pizza size and drinks are the same").
* **`if`**: The condition that changes (e.g., "if conversation A has more toppings than conversation B").
* **`then`**: The expected outcome (e.g., "then conversation A's price must be higher than conversation B's price").

It's a fantastic way to ensure your chatbot behaves logically and consistently across different scenarios!
