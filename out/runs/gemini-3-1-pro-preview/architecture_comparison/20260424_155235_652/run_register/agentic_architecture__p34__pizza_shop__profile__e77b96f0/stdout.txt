Here is a SENSEI profile tailored to your scenario! 

To achieve this, we can explicitly instruct the simulated user in the `role` and `goals` to start with a custom pizza and then switch to a predefined one. We can also use SENSEI's built-in `change your mind` interaction style, which naturally encourages the LLM to alter its decisions during the conversation.

Here is the YAML profile (you can save this as `pizza_change_mind.yml` in your `profiles/` directory):

```yaml
test_name: "pizza_change_mind_scenario"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer ordering a pizza. You start by asking for a custom pizza with specific toppings, 
    but halfway through the conversation, you change your mind and decide to order a predefined pizza from the menu instead.
  context:
    - "You are very hungry but a bit indecisive."
    - "You want to make sure the predefined pizza doesn't take too long to prepare."
  goals:
    - "Start by ordering a custom pizza with {{custom_toppings}}"
    - "Halfway through the conversation, change your mind and ask for a {{predefined_pizza}} instead"
    - custom_toppings:
        function: random(3)
        type: string
        data: [pepperoni, mushrooms, onions, sausage, bacon, extra cheese, black olives, green peppers]
    - predefined_pizza:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [Margherita, BBQ Chicken, Hawaiian, Meat Lovers, Veggie Supreme]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you repeat your pizza order?"
  output:
    - final_pizza_ordered:
        type: string
        description: "The final pizza the user decided to order after changing their mind"
    - initial_custom_toppings:
        type: string
        description: "The custom toppings the user initially asked for before changing their mind"

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
```

### 🍕 How this profile works:
* **`user.goals`**: We define two variables. `{{custom_toppings}}` picks 3 random toppings for the initial request, and `{{predefined_pizza}}` picks 1 random predefined pizza for the pivot. The text prompts explicitly tell the simulator *when* to use them.
* **`interaction_style`**: By including `- change your mind`, we trigger a specific SENSEI behavior where the simulated user naturally hesitates or backtracks, making the transition feel like a real, indecisive human.
* **`chatbot.output`**: At the end of the conversation, SENSEI will extract both the `initial_custom_toppings` and the `final_pizza_ordered`. This is super useful if you later want to write a **SENSEI Rule** to verify that your chatbot correctly discarded the initial custom toppings and only charged for the final predefined pizza!
