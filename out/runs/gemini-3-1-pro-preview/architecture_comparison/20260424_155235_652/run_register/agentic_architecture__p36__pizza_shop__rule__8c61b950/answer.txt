The reason your rule isn't working is that it is missing the **`if`** clause! 

In SENSEI-check, when you use a `then` statement for a metamorphic rule, it needs to be paired with an `if` statement to form a logical implication ("If X happens, then Y should happen"). 

Right now, your rule uses `when` to filter for comparable conversations (same size and drink), but it jumps straight to `then` without actually comparing the toppings. Because of this, it's trying to assert that *any* two pizzas of the same size and drink will have different prices, which doesn't make sense and will fail.

Here is the corrected rule based on the `sensei-check-doc.md` guidelines:

```yaml
name: more_toppings_cost_more
description: Adding more toppings to a custom pizza costs more
active: True
conversations: 2
when: conv[0].size == conv[1].size and conv[0].drink == conv[1].drink
if: len(conv[0].toppings) > len(conv[1].toppings)
then: extract_float(conv[0].price) > extract_float(conv[1].price)
```

### What changed:
* **Added `if: len(conv[0].toppings) > len(conv[1].toppings)`**: This is the crucial precondition. It tells the rule to look specifically at pairs of conversations where the first pizza has *more* toppings than the second one.
* **How it works together**: 
  * `when` ensures we are comparing apples to apples (same size, same drink).
  * `if` checks our variable of interest (more toppings).
  * `then` asserts the expected outcome (higher price).
