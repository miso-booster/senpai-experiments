Looking at your project, I see you have a couple of profiles that deal with scheduling services, like **`appointment.yml`** and **`bike_repair_and_maintenance_scheduler.yaml`**. 

In Sensei-check, the best way to verify if the chatbot confirmed the appointment is to check if the specific fields defined in your profile's `chatbot.output` were successfully extracted during the conversation.

Here is a rule designed specifically for your **`appointment.yml`** profile. It checks that the chatbot successfully captured and confirmed the date, time, and service:

```yaml
name: appointment_confirmation_check
description: The chatbot successfully confirms the appointment date, time, and service.
conversations: 1
active: true
oracle: confirmed_date is not None and confirmed_time is not None and confirmed_service is not None
```

### How it works:
* **`conversations: 1`**: This is a standard oracle rule that evaluates each conversation individually.
* **`oracle`**: It looks directly at the `confirmed_date`, `confirmed_time`, and `confirmed_service` fields from your `appointment.yml` profile's output. If the chatbot successfully scheduled the appointment, these fields will be filled with data (not `None`).
