I took a look at your YAML file, and I found a few small details that are keeping it from working properly. Here is a quick breakdown of what needs to be adjusted:

1. **LLM Temperature:** The `temperature` field needs to be a number (a float, like `0.2` or `0.7`) rather than a word like `"low"`. 
2. **Missing `goal_style`:** Sensei needs to know when to end the conversation! We have to add a `goal_style` under the `conversation` section (for example, using `all_answered` or a fixed number of `steps`).
3. **Output Types:** 
   * The default type for text is `string`, not `str`. 
   * The `seat_price` and `care_bike` fields in your `chatbot.output` are missing their `type` declarations. Every output needs both a `type` and a `description`.
   * *Note on `money`:* I see you are using `type: money` for the tire and tune-up. That is perfectly fine **if** you have a custom type file named `money.yml` inside your project's `types/` folder! If you don't, you'll want to change those to `string` or `float`.

Here is the corrected and ready-to-run version of your profile:

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.2  # Changed from 'low' to a numeric float
  model: gpt-4o-mini

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string  # Changed 'str' to 'string'
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: money  # Ensure you have a 'money.yml' in your types/ folder!
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: money
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: string  # Added the missing type
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: string  # Added the missing type
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:    # Added goal_style so Sensei knows how to finish the chat
      export: true
      limit: 10
  interaction_style:
    - default
```
