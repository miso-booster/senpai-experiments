Here is your completed profile:

```yaml
test_name: pizza_and_drinks_information_seeker
llm:
  temperature: 0.3
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text
user:
  language: English
  role: A customer seeking information about pizza types, prices, available drinks, and shop hours before potentially placing an order.
  context:
  - 'personality: personalities/skeptical-user'
  - You're planning a casual pizza night at home with friends this weekend and are considering ordering from a local pizzeria.
  - You want to gather all the necessary information before deciding what to order and whether to pick it up or have it delivered.
  - You have a variety of preferences among your friends, so you need to find options that cater to different tastes.
  goals:
  - Ask about the available pizza types.
  - Ask about the price of a Margarita pizza.
  - Ask about the available drinks and their prices.
  - Ask about the shop's operating hours.
  - Ask if they deliver.

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't understand that. Could you rephrase?"
  output:
    - available_pizzas:
        type: list[string]
        description: "The types of pizzas available at the shop."
    - margarita_price:
        type: float
        description: "The price of a Margarita pizza."
    - available_drinks:
        type: list[string]
        description: "The drinks available at the shop and their prices."
    - operating_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The shop's operating hours."
    - offers_delivery:
        type: string
        description: "Whether the shop offers delivery (e.g., yes or no)."

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 20
  interaction_style:
  - single questions
```

### What I added to the `chatbot` field:
1. **`is_starter: false`**: This tells Sensei that the simulated user should send the first message. (Change this to `true` if your chatbot is the one that greets the user first).
2. **`fallback`**: A standard fallback message. It helps Sensei recognize if the chatbot gets stuck in a loop.
3. **`output`**: I created 5 extraction fields (`available_pizzas`, `margarita_price`, `available_drinks`, `operating_hours`, and `offers_delivery`) that map directly to your user's `goals`. I used default types like `list[string]`, `float`, and `string` to capture the data appropriately.
