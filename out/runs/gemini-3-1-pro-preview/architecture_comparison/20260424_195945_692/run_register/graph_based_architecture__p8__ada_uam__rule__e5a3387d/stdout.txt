### The Fixed Rule


### What changed and why? 🛠️

1. **Removed the `conv[0].` prefix:** 
   In Sensei-check, the `conv[0]`, `conv[1]` syntax is exclusively used for **metamorphic rules** where you are comparing multiple conversations (i.e., `conversations: 2` or more). Because your rule is a traditional oracle checking a single conversation (`conversations: 1`), you can just use the field names directly! 

2. **Removed the `when` clause:**
   In your original rule, your `when` filter and your `oracle` condition were exactly the same. 
   * Think of `when` as the bouncer at a club: it filters *which* conversations are allowed to be tested by this rule. 
   * If you tell the bouncer, "Only let in conversations where the email is cau@uam.es and the phone is 91 497 4029," then your `oracle` (the test itself) will *always* pass, because you already filtered out any conversations that got it wrong! 
   * By removing the `when` clause, the rule will now actually evaluate the conversation and catch the chatbot if it makes a mistake.

### One quick Senpai tip! 💡
Just remember that for this rule to work perfectly, your SENSEI Chat profile must expose `contact_email`, `contact_phone`, and `physical_address` in its `chatbot.output` schema. Sensei-check will look for those exact field names to run the test.
