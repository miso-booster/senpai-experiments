To check that adding a drink costs exactly $2 extra, we need to compare **two** conversations (which is the magic of metamorphic testing! ✨). We want to find two conversations where everything else about the order is exactly the same, but one has a drink and the other doesn't. 

Here is the YAML rule you can use:

```yaml
name: drink_adds_two_dollars
description: Adding a drink costs exactly 2 dollars extra when all other order details are the same.
conversations: 2
when: conv[0].size == conv[1].size and conv[0].toppings == conv[1].toppings and conv[0].number == conv[1].number
if: conv[0].drink and not conv[1].drink
then: extract_float(conv[0].price) == extract_float(conv[1].price) + 2.0
```

### 🧠 How this works:
* **`conversations: 2`**: Tells Sensei-check we are comparing a pair of conversations.
* **`when`**: This is our filter! It ensures we are comparing apples to apples 🍎. We only look at pairs where the pizza `size`, `toppings`, and `number` (quantity) are identical. *(Note: Make sure these field names match what you actually have in your SENSEI Chat profile's `chatbot.output`!)*
* **`if`**: This is our precondition. It looks for a pair where `conv[0]` has a drink (the value is not empty/None) and `conv[1]` does *not* have a drink.
* **`then`**: This is the actual check! It extracts the numeric value from the price strings and asserts that the conversation with the drink (`conv[0]`) costs exactly $2 more than the one without (`conv[1]`).

Make sure your SENSEI Chat profile exposes `drink` and `price` in its `chatbot.output` for this to work perfectly.
