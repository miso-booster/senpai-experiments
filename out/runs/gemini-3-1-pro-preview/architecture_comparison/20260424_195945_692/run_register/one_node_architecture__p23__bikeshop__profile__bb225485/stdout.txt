I took a look at your YAML, and I found a few little issues that are preventing it from working correctly:

1. **`llm.temperature`**: This needs to be a number (a float like `0.0` or `0.5`), rather than a word like `"low"`.
2. **Missing `type` in outputs**: Every item in `chatbot.output` needs a `type`. `seat_price` and `care_bike` were missing theirs. 
3. **`str` vs `string`**: The default text type in Sensei is `string`, not `str`.
4. **Missing `goal_style`**: The `conversation` section needs a `goal_style` so the simulator knows when to end the conversation (for example, stopping when all questions are answered).

    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"
