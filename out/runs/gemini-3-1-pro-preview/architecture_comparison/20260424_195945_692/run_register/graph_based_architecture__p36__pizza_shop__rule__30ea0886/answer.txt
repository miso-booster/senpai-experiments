Hi there! I see exactly what's going on here. 👀 Your rule is super close to being perfect, but it's missing one key ingredient: the `if` clause! 

In Sensei-check, whenever you use a `then` statement for a metamorphic rule, it needs an `if` statement to pair up with it. Think of it like cause and effect:
* **`when`** sets the stage (e.g., finding two conversations where the pizza size and drink are the same).
* **`if`** is the *cause* or precondition (e.g., one pizza has more toppings than the other).
* **`then`** is the *effect* or expected result (e.g., the one with more toppings costs more).

Right now, your rule knows *when* to compare the pizzas and *what* the final price result should be, but it's missing the trigger (`if` it has more toppings). 

Here is the fixed version of your rule with the `if` clause added in:

```yaml
name: more_toppings_cost_more
description: Adding more toppings to a custom pizza costs more
active: True
conversations: 2
when: conv[0].size == conv[1].size and conv[0].drink == conv[1].drink
if: len(conv[0].toppings) > len(conv[1].toppings)
then: extract_float(conv[0].price) > extract_float(conv[1].price)
```

Now, Sensei-check will properly look for two conversations with the same size and drink, check **if** the first one has more toppings, and **then** verify that its price is higher. 🍕💸
