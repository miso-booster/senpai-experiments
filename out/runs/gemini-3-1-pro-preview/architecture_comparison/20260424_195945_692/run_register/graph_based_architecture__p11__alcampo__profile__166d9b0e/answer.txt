Since you requested no variables, we will keep the `user.goals` as simple, plain-text instructions. This is a great way to do a straightforward test to see how well your chatbot explains its subscription services and perks.

Here is a complete, ready-to-run YAML profile tailored to your request:

```yaml
test_name: "subscription_services_and_benefits"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a frequent shopper who wants to learn about the store's subscription 
    services and the perks of becoming a member to save money.
  context:
    - "You want to know if the subscription is worth the cost."
    - "You are particularly interested in free delivery and exclusive discounts."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to explain the available subscription services."
    - "Inquire about the specific benefits and perks of becoming a subscribed member."
    - "Ask how much the subscription costs and if there is a free trial."
    - "Find out if the subscription includes free delivery for online orders."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you repeat your question about our services?"
  output:
    - subscription_name:
        type: string
        description: "The name of the subscription service offered"
    - subscription_cost:
        type: currency
        description: "The price of the subscription"
    - free_delivery_included:
        type: string
        description: "Whether free delivery is included in the subscription"
    - member_benefits:
        type: string
        description: "A summary of the perks and benefits for members"

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### 👀 A quick look at what's happening here:
* **`user.goals`**: Just as you asked, these are plain strings without any `{{variables}}`. Sensei will read these and naturally ask the chatbot about each point!
* **`chatbot.output`**: I added a few fields here like `subscription_cost` (using the `currency` type) and `member_benefits`. At the end of the conversation, Sensei will extract this information so you can easily verify if the chatbot gave the right answers.
* **`conversation`**: I set `number: 1` because without variables to combine, one conversation is usually enough to test this specific flow. I also used `single questions` for the interaction style so the simulated user asks about the goals one by one.

You can save this as `subscription_benefits.yml` inside your `profiles/` folder and run it right away! 🚀
