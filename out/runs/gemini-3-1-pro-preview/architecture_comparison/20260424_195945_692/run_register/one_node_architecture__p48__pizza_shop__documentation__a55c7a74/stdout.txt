Hello there! 😊 I'd be happy to help you with your Sensei profiles. 

While a profile needs a core structure to know what to do (like the `user.goals` and `chatbot.output`), there are several fields that are completely **optional**. You can safely leave them out to keep your profile simple!

Here are the main non-mandatory fields:

1. **The entire `llm` block:** You don't actually have to define this at all! If you omit it, Sensei will simply apply its default settings (like a default model, temperature, and text format).
2. **Cost limits (`max_cost`):** Both `conversation.max_cost` (which caps the spend for the whole run) and `goal_style.max_cost` (which caps the spend per conversation) are optional. They are just handy safety nets for your budget 💸.
3. **Personalities (`user.context.personality`):** You don't have to link to a separate personality YAML file. You can just write freeform sentences in the `context` list to describe the user's situation.
4. **Interaction styles (`conversation.interaction_style`):** If you don't specify any interaction styles (like `make spelling mistakes` or `change language`), Sensei will just use the `default` style, meaning the simulated user will chat in a standard, natural way.
5. **Exporting and limits in `all_answered`:** If you use the `all_answered` goal style, the `export: true/false` and `limit` fields are optional. If you leave `limit` out, it defaults to a safety stop of 30 steps.

If you ever want to start small, you can just focus on defining who the user is, what their goals are, and what the chatbot should output. 

Let me know if you'd like to see an example of a super minimal profile, or if you have any other questions! 🥋✨
