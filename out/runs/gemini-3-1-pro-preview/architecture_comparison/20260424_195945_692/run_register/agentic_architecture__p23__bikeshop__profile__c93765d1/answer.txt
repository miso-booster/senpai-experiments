Based on the SENSEI Profiles documentation (`sensei-chat-guide.md`), I found a few structural issues in your YAML that are preventing it from working correctly. 

Here is a summary of what needed to be fixed:
1. **`llm.temperature`**: This needs to be a number (float) like `0.2`, rather than a word like `"low"`.
2. **Missing `goal_style`**: Your profile didn't tell the simulator when to end the conversation. I added `goal_style: all_answered:` so it knows to stop once all your goals are met.
3. **Invalid or Missing Output Types**: 
   - `str` is not a recognized default type; it must be spelled out as `string`.
   - `money` is not a default type. Unless you have explicitly created a custom type file for it in a `types/` folder, it will fail. I changed these to `string` to be safe (you can also use `float`).
   - `seat_price` and `care_bike` were completely missing their `type` definitions, which are required for every output field.
4. **LLM Provider**: I added `model_prov: openai` under the `llm` section, which is best practice when using `gpt-4o-mini`.

Here is your corrected and ready-to-use profile:

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.2
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: string
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: string
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: string
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```
