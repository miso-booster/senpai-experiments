Here is the SENSEI profile tailored for testing your bike shop's virtual assistant. 

I have structured it based on the `sensei-chat-guide.md` documentation, ensuring the simulated user behaves exactly as you described and that the chatbot's responses are extracted properly for future validation.

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_info"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer looking for general information about a bike shop and its standard services.
    You behave like a normal customer and phrase your questions clearly and directly.
  context:
    - "Do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, or any topic outside of basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - "Ask about the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask about the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask about the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices rather than advanced repairs."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you rephrase?"
  output:
    - shop_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times provided by the chatbot."
    - tire_price:
        type: string
        description: "The quoted price for a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: string
        description: "The quoted price for a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: string
        description: "The quoted price for a new bike seat."
    - maintenance_advice:
        type: string
        description: "The general advice provided on basic bike maintenance."
    - unrelated_topics_introduced:
        type: string
        description: "Any unrelated services, advanced repairs, or unsolicited suggestions introduced by the chatbot. Should be none or empty."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### 🚲 How this profile matches your requirements:

*   **Role & Tone**: The `user.role` and `interaction_style: [default]` ensure the simulator acts like a normal customer asking clear, direct questions in English.
*   **Strict Boundaries**: The `user.context` explicitly forbids the simulator from asking about appointments, specific models, warranties, delivery, or advanced repairs. This keeps the conversation tightly focused on your test parameters.
*   **Specific Inquiries**: The `user.goals` list covers all your required questions (hours, tire price, tune-up price, seat price, and basic maintenance advice).
*   **Evaluation Readiness**: The `chatbot.output` section is designed to extract exactly what you want to evaluate. It pulls the prices, the shop hours, the maintenance advice, and specifically checks if any `unrelated_topics_introduced` slipped into the chatbot's replies. This makes it very easy to write SENSEI rules later to validate the bot's accuracy and focus! 😊
