To check that adding a drink costs exactly $2 extra, we need to compare **two** conversations. The secret to a great metamorphic rule is keeping everything else the same (like the pizza size, toppings, and quantity) so we know the price difference is *only* because of the drink! 

Here is the ready-to-use YAML for your rule:

```yaml
name: drink_costs_two_dollars_extra
description: Adding a drink increases the total price by exactly 2 dollars (assuming same size, toppings, and quantity).
conversations: 2
when: conv[0].size == conv[1].size and conv[0].toppings == conv[1].toppings and conv[0].number == conv[1].number
if: conv[0].drink and not conv[1].drink
then: extract_float(conv[0].price) == extract_float(conv[1].price) + 2
```

### 🧠 How it works:
* **`conversations: 2`**: Tells Sensei-check we are comparing a pair of conversations.
* **`when`**: This is our filter! It ensures we only compare orders that have the exact same `size`, `toppings`, and `number` of pizzas.
* **`if`**: This sets up our scenario. It looks for a pair where the first conversation (`conv[0]`) has a `drink`, and the second conversation (`conv[1]`) does *not* have a drink.
* **`then`**: This is the magic check! It extracts the numeric price from both conversations and asserts that the first one is exactly $2 more than the second one.
