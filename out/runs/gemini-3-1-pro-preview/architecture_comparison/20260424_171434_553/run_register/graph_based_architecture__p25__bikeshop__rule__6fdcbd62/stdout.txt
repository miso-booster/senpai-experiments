Since you are looking to verify exact, absolute values from the chatbot's answers, we will create **single-conversation oracles** (using `conversations: 1`).

Here are two rules to cover your requirements. I've split them up to keep things neat: one for the prices using our `extract_float()` helper, and one for the schedule using our `semantic_content()` helper!

### 1. Checking the Prices 🏷️
Because prices can sometimes include currency symbols or text (like "$50" or "50 USD"), the `extract_float()` helper is your best friend here. It will grab just the number so we can do a direct math comparison.

```yaml
name: check_bike_shop_prices
description: Verifies that the tune-up is 50, seat is 20, and tire is 20.
conversations: 1
oracle: extract_float(tune_up) == 50 and extract_float(seat_price) == 20 and extract_float(tire) == 20
```

### 2. Checking the Opening Hours ⏰
For the opening and closing times, the chatbot might answer in a few different ways (e.g., "From 10 to 20", "10:00 - 20:00", or "We open at 10 and close at 20"). To handle this natural variation gracefully, we can use the `semantic_content()` helper. It uses a quick LLM check under the hood to ensure the meaning matches your requirement!

```yaml
name: check_opening_hours
description: Verifies that the opening and closing times are semantically equivalent to 10 to 20.
conversations: 1
oracle: semantic_content(opening_and_closing, '10 to 20')
```
