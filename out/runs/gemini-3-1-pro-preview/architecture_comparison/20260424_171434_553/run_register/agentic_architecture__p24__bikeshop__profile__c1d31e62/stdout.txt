Here is the SENSEI profile tailored for testing the bike shop's virtual assistant. I have structured it based on the SENSEI Chat documentation (`sensei-chat-guide.md`).

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_basic_inquiry"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a normal customer interacting with a bike shop's virtual assistant.
    You are looking for general information about the shop and its standard services.
    You must phrase your questions clearly and directly.
  context:
    - "Do NOT ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, or delivery services."
    - "Do NOT ask about any topic outside of basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices rather than advanced repairs."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't understand that. Could you please rephrase?"
  output:
    - shop_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times provided by the chatbot."
    - tire_price:
        type: string
        description: "The price quoted for a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: string
        description: "The price quoted for a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: string
        description: "The price quoted for a new bike seat."
    - maintenance_advice:
        type: string
        description: "The general advice provided on basic bike maintenance and good practices."
    - unrelated_topics_introduced:
        type: string
        description: "Any unrelated services, advanced repairs, or unsolicited suggestions introduced by the chatbot."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### How this profile meets your requirements:

1. **Role & Language**: The `user.language` is set to English, and the `user.role` establishes the persona of a normal customer asking clear, direct questions about standard services.
2. **Strict Constraints**: The `user.context` explicitly forbids the simulator from asking about appointments, specific models, custom/advanced repairs, warranties, delivery, or anything outside the allowed scope.
3. **Specific Inquiries**: The `user.goals` list maps exactly to the 5 questions you want the simulated user to ask (hours, tire price, tune-up price, seat price, and basic maintenance advice).
4. **Validation-Ready Outputs**: The `chatbot.output` section is designed to extract exactly what you want to test. It pulls out the hours, the three specific prices, the maintenance advice, and importantly, a field called `unrelated_topics_introduced`. This makes it very easy to write SENSEI Rules later to verify that prices are clearly stated and that no unsolicited services were pushed!
5. **Execution Controls**: The `goal_style` is set to `all_answered` to ensure the conversation naturally concludes only once all 5 questions have been addressed by the chatbot.

Let me know if you'd like to adjust any of the extraction fields or if you'd like to write some SENSEI Rules to validate these outputs! 😊
