Hello there! 😊 I'd be happy to help you create this profile for your bike shop's virtual assistant. 

Based on your requirements, I've set up the `user.context` to strictly enforce the boundaries (no appointments, no specific models, etc.) and ensure the simulated customer asks clear, direct questions. I also mapped your requested information into the `chatbot.output` section so that you can easily extract and validate the prices and advice the bot provides.

Here is the ready-to-use YAML profile:

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_info_and_pricing"

llm:
  temperature: 0.5
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    A normal customer looking for general information about the bike shop and its standard services.
  context:
    - "You phrase your questions clearly and directly."
    - "Do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, or delivery services."
    - "Stick strictly to basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "Ask about the opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you rephrase?"
  output:
    - opening_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The opening and closing times of the bike shop."
    - tire_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price quoted for a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price quoted for a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The price quoted for a new bike seat."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General advice provided on how to properly take care of a bike."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### A few quick notes on this setup 👀:
* **`goal_style: all_answered`**: This ensures the conversation won't end until the user has successfully asked all the questions in the `goals` list.
* **`export: true`**: This will generate a handy CSV dataframe at the end of the run, showing you exactly which outputs were successfully satisfied!
* **`type: currency`**: I used the built-in currency type for the prices so that Sensei can cleanly extract the numerical values and their currency symbols for any future Sensei-check rules you might want to write.
