Here is a SENSEI profile tailored to your cyclist persona. 

I have designed the profile so the simulated user will explain their discomfort, ask whether it might be a fit issue or worn components, and inquire about shop services—all while strictly avoiding asking for an appointment.

```yaml
test_name: "cyclist_discomfort_advice"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a cyclist who is experiencing discomfort while riding your bike.
    You are not sure whether the issue is that your bike is not fit correctly,
    or if some of your components are worn out. You want to get advice from the
    bike shop on possible adjustments or services they offer to help you.
  context:
    - "You are experiencing pain or discomfort during your rides."
    - "You want to understand what might be causing the issue (fit vs. wear)."
    - "You want to know what services or adjustments the shop can provide."
    - "You MUST NOT explicitly ask to book an appointment."
  goals:
    - "Explain your discomfort in your {{body_part}} while riding your {{bike_type}} bike."
    - "Ask if the issue could be due to bike fit or worn components like the {{component}}."
    - "Ask what services or adjustments the shop recommends."
    - body_part:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [lower back, knees, neck, saddle area, wrists]
    - bike_type:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [road, mountain, gravel, hybrid]
    - component:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [saddle, cleats, handlebars, pedals, chain]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you rephrase?"
  output:
    - recommended_service:
        type: string
        description: "The service or adjustment recommended by the shop (e.g., bike fit, component replacement)."
    - mentioned_cause:
        type: string
        description: "Whether the shop suggested the issue was fit-related, wear-related, or both."
    - appointment_offered:
        type: string
        description: "Did the shop offer to book an appointment? (yes/no)"

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### 🚲 How this profile works:

1. **Role & Context**: The `role` sets up the exact scenario you described. The `context` reinforces the constraints, explicitly instructing the LLM **not** to ask for an appointment.
2. **Goals & Variables**: The user will dynamically mention different body parts (e.g., lower back, knees), bike types (e.g., road, gravel), and components (e.g., saddle, cleats) using the `random()` function. This ensures you get varied conversations across the 3 runs (`conversation.number: 3`).
3. **Chatbot Output**: I've defined three fields in `chatbot.output` that will be extracted at the end of the conversation:
   - `recommended_service`: To see what the shop actually suggested.
   - `mentioned_cause`: To check if the bot addressed the fit vs. wear dilemma.
   - `appointment_offered`: A great field to use later in SENSEI RULES to verify if your chatbot proactively offered an appointment even though the user didn't ask for one!
