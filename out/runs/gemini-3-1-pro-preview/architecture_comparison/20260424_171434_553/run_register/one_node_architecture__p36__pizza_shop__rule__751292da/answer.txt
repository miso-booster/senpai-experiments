The reason your rule isn't working is that it's missing the `if` clause! In Sensei-check, metamorphic rules that use a `then` statement must always be paired with an `if` statement to establish the condition. 

Right now, your rule says "Then the price should be higher," but it forgets to check "If the pizza has more toppings" first! 🍕 Without the `if` condition, the rule doesn't know what triggers the price increase, and the syntax becomes invalid.

Here is the corrected version of your rule (which also matches the logic intended in your project's `more_toppings_cost_more.yaml` file):

```yaml
name: more_toppings_cost_more
description: Adding more toppings to a custom pizza costs more
active: True
conversations: 2
when: conv[0].size == conv[1].size and conv[0].drink == conv[1].drink
if: len(conv[0].toppings) > len(conv[1].toppings)
then: extract_float(conv[0].price) > extract_float(conv[1].price)
```

Now it perfectly expresses the metamorphic relation: **When** the size and drinks are the same, **If** conversation 0 has more toppings than conversation 1, **Then** conversation 0 should cost more.
