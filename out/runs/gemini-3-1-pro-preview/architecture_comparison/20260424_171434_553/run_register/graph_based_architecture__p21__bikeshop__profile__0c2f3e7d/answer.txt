Creating a profile for a user who wants to customize their new bike is a fantastic use case! We'll set up the **User** section so the simulator knows exactly what its backstory is (new bike, needs a new seat and grips), and we'll configure the **Chatbot Output** section to extract the important details your bot replies with, like pricing and availability.

Here is a complete, ready-to-use YAML profile tailored exactly to your scenario. You can save this as `new_bike_customization.yml` inside your `profiles/` folder:

```yaml
test_name: "new_bike_customization"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    A customer who recently bought a new bike and wants to customize it for a better fit and feel.
  context:
    - "You just bought a new bike, but the current setup isn't quite comfortable for you."
    - "You specifically want to change the seat and the handlebar grips to something more tailored to your liking."
    - "You are eager to get this done soon so you can start riding comfortably."
  goals:
    - "Explain that you recently got a new bike and need help setting it up."
    - "Ask if the shop sells and installs custom seats and handlebar grips."
    - "Inquire about the estimated price for replacing both the seat and the grips, including labor."
    - "Ask to schedule an appointment to bring the bike in for these upgrades."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - customization_availability:
        type: string
        description: "Confirmation from the chatbot regarding whether the shop offers seat and grip replacements."
    - parts_and_labor_price:
        type: money
        description: "The estimated price provided by the chatbot for the new seat, grips, and installation."
    - appointment_confirmation:
        type: string
        description: "Details of the scheduled appointment, if the chatbot helps book one."

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### 🛠️ A quick breakdown of what we did:
* **`user.context` & `user.goals`**: This gives the simulated user their motivation. They will naturally explain their situation and ask the questions you specified.
* **`chatbot.output`**: I added fields like `customization_availability` and `parts_and_labor_price` (using the `money` type, which matches your other bikeshop profiles!). This is the "contract" for your tests—Sensei will extract these values at the end of the conversation so you can easily check if your bot gave the right answers.
* **`conversation`**: I set it to run 3 times (`number: 3`) using the `all_answered` goal style, meaning the conversation will naturally end once the user has asked all their questions.
