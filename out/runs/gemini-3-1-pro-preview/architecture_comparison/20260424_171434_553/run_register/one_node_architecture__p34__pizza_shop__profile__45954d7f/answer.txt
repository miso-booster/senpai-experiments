To create a scenario where the user changes their mind, we can use a combination of explicit instructions in the `user.goals` and the built-in `change your mind` interaction style. This will make the simulated user start with a custom pizza request and then pivot to a predefined one!

Here is a complete, ready-to-use profile for your project:

```yaml
test_name: "custom_to_predefined_pizza_change"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A hungry but indecisive customer ordering a pizza."
  context:
    - "You are chatting with your local pizza shop to place an order."
    - "You initially want to build your own pizza, but you realize it's too complicated and decide to switch to a classic option."
  goals:
    - "Start by asking for a {{size}} custom pizza with {{custom_toppings}}."
    - "Change your mind halfway through and tell the assistant you want to cancel the custom pizza and just get a {{size}} {{predefined_pizza}} instead."
    - "Ask for the total price of the new order."
    - size:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [medium, large, extra large]
    - custom_toppings:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [pepperoni and mushrooms, extra cheese and jalapeños, ham and olives]
    - predefined_pizza:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [Margherita, Pepperoni, Four Cheese, Vegetarian]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't catch that. Would you like a custom or predefined pizza?"
  output:
    - final_pizza_type:
        type: string
        description: "The final pizza type the user settled on after changing their mind."
    - size:
        type: string
        description: "The size of the pizza ordered."
    - total_price:
        type: currency
        description: "The final price of the order."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
```

### 👀 Why this works:
1. **`interaction_style: [change your mind]`**: This tells the Sensei user simulator to naturally hesitate or alter its choices during the conversation.
2. **Explicit Goals**: By explicitly stating the transition from `{{custom_toppings}}` to `{{predefined_pizza}}` in the goals, we guarantee the user will execute this exact scenario.
3. **`chatbot.output`**: We extract the `final_pizza_type` so you can easily verify later (perhaps with a Sensei Rule!) that the chatbot successfully registered the predefined pizza and discarded the custom one.
