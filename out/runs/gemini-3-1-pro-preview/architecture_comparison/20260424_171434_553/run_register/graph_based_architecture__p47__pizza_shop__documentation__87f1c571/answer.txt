In the world of Sensei-check, a **metamorphic rule** is a super clever way to test your chatbot by comparing *multiple conversations* against each other, rather than just looking at one conversation in isolation.

Instead of asking, "Did the bot give the exact right absolute answer?" (which is often hard to know!), a metamorphic rule asks, "If I change the input in a specific way, does the output change in the logical way I expect?" 

🍕 **A quick everyday analogy:**
Imagine you are testing a pizza-ordering chatbot. You might not know the exact price of every single pizza combination. However, you *do* know that if you order a medium pizza with **2 toppings**, and then order the exact same medium pizza but with **4 toppings**, the second order should cost more! 

You don't need to know the exact prices to know that the relationship (more toppings = higher price) must be true. That's metamorphic testing in a nutshell! 💡

**How it works in Sensei:**
You write these rules in YAML format. A typical metamorphic rule will define:
1. **How many conversations** it needs to compare (usually 2).
2. **A filter (`when`)** to select comparable conversations (e.g., "when the pizza size and drinks are the exact same").
3. **The relation (`if` / `then`)** to check (e.g., "if conversation A has more toppings than conversation B, then conversation A's price must be higher").

This approach is fantastic because it alleviates what we call the "oracle problem"—you don't need a perfect answer key for every possible user input, you just check the logical relationships between different scenarios! 🧠✨
