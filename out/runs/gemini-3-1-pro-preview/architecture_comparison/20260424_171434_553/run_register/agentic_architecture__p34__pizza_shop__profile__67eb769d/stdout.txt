Here is a SENSEI profile that perfectly matches your scenario. I've designed it so the simulated user starts by ordering a custom pizza but then changes their mind to a predefined one.

To achieve this, I used a combination of explicit instructions in the `user.context` and `user.goals`, along with the built-in `change your mind` interaction style.

```yaml
test_name: "pizza_change_mind_test"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer ordering a pizza from a chatbot.
  context:
    - "You start by ordering a custom pizza with your choice of toppings."
    - "Halfway through the conversation, you change your mind and decide you just want a predefined pizza instead."
  goals:
    - "Start by ordering a custom pizza with {{custom_toppings}}"
    - "Change your mind and ask for a {{predefined_pizza}} pizza instead"
    - custom_toppings:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: 
          - "mushrooms, onions, and extra cheese"
          - "pepperoni, jalapenos, and black olives"
          - "ham, bacon, and sausage"
    - predefined_pizza:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: 
          - "Margherita"
          - "Pepperoni"
          - "Hawaiian"
          - "BBQ Chicken"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't understand that. Could you repeat?"
  output:
    - final_pizza_ordered:
        type: string
        description: "The final predefined pizza the user decided to order"
    - initial_custom_toppings:
        type: string
        description: "The custom toppings the user initially asked for before changing their mind"

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 15
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
```

### How this profile works:

1. **`user.context` & `user.goals`**: We explicitly tell the LLM the narrative arc of the conversation. It starts by trying to fulfill the first goal (ordering the custom pizza with random toppings) and then moves to the second goal (changing to a random predefined pizza).
2. **`interaction_style: [change your mind]`**: This is a built-in SENSEI interaction style that naturally encourages the simulated user to hesitate, correct themselves, or change their previous inputs during the conversation.
3. **`chatbot.output`**: At the end of the conversation, SENSEI will extract both the `initial_custom_toppings` they asked for and the `final_pizza_ordered`. This is very useful if you later want to write a **SENSEI Rule** to verify that your chatbot successfully discarded the custom toppings and only charged for the predefined pizza!
