I took a look at your YAML, and I found a few small issues that are preventing it from working properly:

1. **`temperature` must be a number:** In the `llm` section, `temperature` expects a float (like `0.0` or `0.7`) rather than a word like `"low"`.
2. **Missing `type` in outputs:** Every item in `chatbot.output` needs both a `description` and a `type`. `seat_price` and `care_bike` were missing their types.
3. **`str` vs `string`:** The default text type in Sensei is `string`, so we need to change `str` to `string`. *(Note: I left `money` as is, assuming you have a custom type defined for it in your `types/` folder!)*
4. **Missing `goal_style`:** The `conversation` section needs a `goal_style` so the simulator knows when to end the chat (for example, after a certain number of steps, or when all goals are answered).

Here is the corrected and ready-to-use profile:

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.0
  model: gpt-4o-mini

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: money
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: money
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: string
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      limit: 10
  interaction_style:
    - default
```
