Looking at your rule and your project's `customer_service_assistance.yaml` profile, there are a few little things causing it to break:

1. **Typo in the key:** `descriptin` needs to be spelled `description`.
2. **Missing a `then` clause:** In Sensei-check, if you use an `if` condition, you must pair it with a `then` statement to tell the system what the expected outcome is.
3. **Undefined fields:** Your rule checks for `user_request`, but if we look at your `customer_service_assistance.yaml` profile, `user_request` is not defined in the `chatbot.output` section. Sensei-check can only read fields that are explicitly extracted by the profile!

Since your profile is designed to ask for both the contact options and the customer service number in the same chat, this is actually a perfect candidate for a **single-conversation rule** (an oracle) rather than a 2-conversation metamorphic rule. 

Here is the corrected and optimized version of your rule:

```yaml
name: customer_service_contact_info
description: Ensure the chatbot provides correct contact options and information for customer service inquiries.
conversations: 1
oracle: contact_options is not None and customer_service_number is not None
```

### Why this works better:
* We changed `conversations: 2` to `conversations: 1` because both pieces of information are extracted in the same conversation.
* We replaced `when` and `if` with a simple `oracle` that checks if both `contact_options` and `customer_service_number` (which *are* valid fields in your profile's output) were successfully collected by the chatbot.
