### How we built this:
* **`base_url` & `path`**: Taken directly from your `Host` and `POST` headers.
* **`headers`**: We included the essential ones for authentication and routing (`Authorization`, `Content-Type`, `Origin`, and `Referer`).
* **`payload_template`**: We copied your JSON request and replaced the hardcoded `"hola"` with our magic SENSEI placeholder `"{user_msg}"`.
* **`response_path`**: Since the bot's reply is inside a list called `response` at the first index (`0`), and the actual message is under the key `text`, the path is `response.0.text`. 🕵️‍♂️
