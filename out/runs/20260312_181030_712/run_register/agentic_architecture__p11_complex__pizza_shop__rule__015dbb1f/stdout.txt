### Rules Overview

   - **Active:** True
   - **Condition:** 
     - When the sizes and drinks of two orders are the same.
     - If the first order has more toppings than the second.
   - **Then:** The price of the first order should be greater than the second.

   ```yaml
   when: conv[0].size == conv[1].size and conv[0].drink == conv[1].drink

   ```

