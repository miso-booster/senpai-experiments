Sure! Here’s a simple profile that asks the chatbot for items to add to the user's shopping cart. You can customize it further based on your specific needs. 😊

```yaml
test_name: "Shopping Cart Addition"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: "A customer looking to add items to their shopping cart."
  context:
    - "You are planning to do your grocery shopping online."
    - "You want to ensure you have all the necessary items for your meals this week."
  goals:
    - "Add {{item}} to my shopping cart."
    - "What other items do you recommend for my shopping list?"
    - item:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data: [milk, eggs, bread, cheese, fruits, vegetables, chicken, pasta]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. What items would you like to add to your cart?"
  output:
    - added_item:
        type: string
        description: "The item that was added to the shopping cart."
    - recommendations:
        type: string
        description: "Recommended items for the shopping cart."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

Feel free to modify the `data` list under `item` to include any specific items you want to focus on! Happy testing! 🛒✨
