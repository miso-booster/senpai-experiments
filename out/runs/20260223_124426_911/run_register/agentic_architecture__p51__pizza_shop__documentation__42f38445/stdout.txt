In the context of SENSEI and chatbot development, an "oracle" is a specific type of rule that is used to evaluate conditions and provide outputs based on those evaluations. Oracles can help determine the flow of conversation or the responses that a chatbot should give based on the input it receives.

Here’s a simple breakdown of how oracles work:

- **Condition Evaluation**: An oracle checks certain conditions or variables in the conversation.
- **Output Generation**: Based on the evaluation, it can produce specific outputs or trigger actions.
- **Decision Making**: Oracles can guide the chatbot's behavior by deciding which path to take in a conversation based on the conditions met.
