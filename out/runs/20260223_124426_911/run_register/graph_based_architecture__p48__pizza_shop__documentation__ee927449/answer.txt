A metamorphic rule in Sensei is a condition used to check the correctness of conversations generated by your chatbot. Unlike traditional oracles that verify single outputs, metamorphic rules compare pairs or tuples of conversations to assert how outputs should change (or remain the same) when inputs are modified. This approach helps to alleviate the oracle problem by focusing on the relationships between outputs rather than just their absolute values.

Here's a quick breakdown of how it works:

1. **Generate Conversations**: First, you create conversations with Sensei-chat, which includes various inputs and outputs.

2. **Write Rules in YAML**: You then define metamorphic rules in a YAML format, specifying how many conversations to compare, any filters for selecting conversations, and the relationships you want to check.

3. **Evaluate Rules**: Sensei-check evaluates these rules against the generated conversations and reports any failures.

For example, you might have a rule that states if two pizzas are the same size and drink, the one with more toppings should cost more. This is a simple way to ensure that your pricing logic is consistent across different scenarios! 🍕✨
