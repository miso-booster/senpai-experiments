Here’s a simple Sensei-check rule that makes sure your chatbot actually returns an `order_id` in its `chatbot.output`. This one works on single conversations and just checks that the field isn’t missing or blank:

```yaml
name: order_id_is_present
description: Chatbot must return a non-empty order_id in its output
conversations: 1
oracle: order_id is not None and str(order_id).strip() != ''
```

You can drop this into your rules folder (for example, alongside your other YAML files) and Sensei-check will flag any conversation where the `order_id` slot ends up empty. Let me know if you’d like to pair it with a global uniqueness rule too!
