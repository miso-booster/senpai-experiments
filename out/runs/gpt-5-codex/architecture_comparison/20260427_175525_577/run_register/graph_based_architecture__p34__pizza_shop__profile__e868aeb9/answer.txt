Hey there! This scenario sounds fun to test 😊  
I’ve drafted a Sensei profile that makes the simulated user start a custom build, then pivot to a predefined pizza mid-conversation. It uses the `change your mind` interaction style so the simulator naturally performs that switch. You can drop it into your project’s `profiles/` folder (for example, as `profiles/custom_to_menu_switch.yaml`).

---

### 📄 Suggested profile: `custom_to_menu_switch.yaml`

```yaml
test_name: custom_to_menu_switch

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You’re a customer who usually builds custom pizzas, but you’re in a hurry
    tonight and might switch to an easier predefined option if it sounds better.
  context:
    - You're planning dinner after a long day at work.
    - You want enough food for two people.
    - You prefer savory toppings but will compromise if needed.
  goals:
    - "I want to build a custom pizza: {{custom_size}} size, {{custom_crust}} crust, with extra {{custom_toppings}}."
    - "Could you confirm any sauces or extras that come with that custom pizza?"
    - "Actually, I changed my mind—can I order your predefined {{menu_pizza}} pizza instead?"
    - "Please confirm the final pizza details and let me know the total price."
    - "How long will it take before the order is ready for pickup?"
    - custom_size:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [small, medium, large]
    - custom_crust:
        function: forward(custom_size)
        type: string
        data: [thin crust, thick crust, stuffed crust]
    - custom_toppings:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - pepperoni and mushrooms
          - spicy sausage and onions
          - spinach and feta
          - roasted vegetables
    - menu_pizza:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data: [Margarita, Pepperoni, Vegetarian, BBQ Chicken]

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, I didn't catch that. I can help with custom or predefined pizzas, and drinks."
  output:
    - custom_build_summary:
        type: string
        description: "How the chatbot acknowledged or summarized the initial custom pizza request."
    - final_menu_selection:
        type: string
        description: "The predefined pizza the chatbot confirmed after the user changed their mind."
    - total_cost:
        type: money
        description: "Total cost of the final order, including any extras."
    - readiness_time:
        type: time
        description: "Estimated time until the pizza is ready for pickup."
    - order_id:
        type: string
        description: "Order confirmation or tracking identifier."

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.8
  goal_style:
    steps: 6
  interaction_style:
    - change your mind
    - long phrases
```

---

### 🛠️ Notes & tips

- **Change of heart baked in:** The goals lead with a custom build and explicitly ask to switch to a predefined pizza. Pairing that with `interaction_style: change your mind` nudges Sensei to play out the flip convincingly.
- **Outputs for validation:** The `chatbot.output` fields grab what you’ll likely want to inspect with Sensei-check rules (confirmation of both phases, pricing, readiness time, etc.).
- **Coverage:** With `conversation.number: 3` and `forward()` on the key variables, you’ll cycle through different sizes, crusts, and menu pizzas across runs without going too wild on cost.

Feel free to tweak the context, toppings, or predefined options to match your bot’s menu. If you’d like a variant (say, with drink add-ons or multilingual twists), just let me know! 🍕✨
