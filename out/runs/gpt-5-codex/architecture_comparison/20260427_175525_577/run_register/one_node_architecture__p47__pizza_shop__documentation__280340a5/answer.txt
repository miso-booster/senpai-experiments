In Sensei, a **metamorphic rule** (defined in the Sensei-check DSL) is a way to validate chatbot behavior by comparing *relationships* across multiple conversations instead of checking a single answer in isolation.

Here’s the idea:
- Sensei generates lots of conversations using your profiles.
- A metamorphic rule looks at two or more of those conversations (often with similar inputs) and specifies how their outputs should relate.
- For example: “If both orders have the same size and drink, but one has more toppings, then its price must be higher.” That rule compares two conversations and enforces a logical relation between their outputs.

This approach is super helpful when there’s no single “perfect” answer to expect, but you still know how the bot should behave when inputs change in predictable ways (like higher toppings → higher price). Instead of needing a ground-truth response, you assert invariants or monotonic relations across the dataset.
