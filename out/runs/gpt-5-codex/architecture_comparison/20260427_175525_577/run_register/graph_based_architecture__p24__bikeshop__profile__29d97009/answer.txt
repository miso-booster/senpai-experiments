It keeps the conversation on-topic and evaluates whether the chatbot responds with relevant, well-phrased information.

```yaml
test_name: bike_shop_basic_info_check

llm:
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  temperature: 0.4
  format:
    type: text

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - shop_hours:
        type: string
        description: "Opening and closing times provided by the chatbot."
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "Price quoted for a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: money
        description: "Price quoted for a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "Price quoted for a new bike seat."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General bike care and basic maintenance advice provided."

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    A straightforward customer asking about the bike shop's hours, standard service prices,
    and basic bike care tips.
  context:
    - "You’re planning to visit the bike shop soon and want essential information first."
    - "Stay focused on shop hours, standard pricing, and general maintenance advice."
    - "Avoid asking about appointments, specific models, custom repairs, warranties, or deliveries."
  goals:
    - "Ask for the bike shop's opening and closing times."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

conversation:
  number: 2
  max_cost: 0.4
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 12
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```
