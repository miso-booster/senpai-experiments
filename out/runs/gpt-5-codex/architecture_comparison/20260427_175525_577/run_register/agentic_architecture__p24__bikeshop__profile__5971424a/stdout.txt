Here’s a ready-to-use profile that captures the bike-shop scenario exactly as you described:

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_info_profile"

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer who plans to visit a neighborhood bike shop soon.
    You want basic information about the shop's hours, standard services, and simple bike care tips.
  context:
    - "Behave like a normal customer and keep your tone polite and straightforward."
    - "Ask only about shop hours, prices for standard services, and basic bike maintenance practices."
    - "Do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom repairs, warranties, deliveries, or other advanced or unrelated services."
  goals:
    - "Greet the assistant and explain you are looking for general information about the bike shop."
    - "Ask about the shop's opening and closing times."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Ask for general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, could you rephrase that?"
  output:
    - opening_hours:
        type: string
        description: "Stated opening and closing times for the bike shop."
    - tire_price:
        type: currency
        description: "Quoted price for a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: currency
        description: "Quoted price for a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: "Quoted price for a new bike seat."
    - maintenance_advice:
        type: list[string]
        description: "General bike maintenance practices recommended by the chatbot."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      limit: 12
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### Why it fits
- The **goals** line up with every question you need the simulated customer to ask.
- The **context** section keeps the user on-topic and blocks the forbidden areas.
- The **chatbot.output** fields capture hours, each price, and the maintenance tips so future rules can validate accuracy and relevance.
- `interaction_style: single questions` encourages clear, direct queries—great for the “normal customer” vibe.

This layout follows the structure and best practices from `sensei-chat-guide.md`, especially around defining user behaviour and setting up `chatbot.output` as the contract for downstream checks. Let me know if you’d like to tweak the tone, add variables, or expand the outputs! 🚲
