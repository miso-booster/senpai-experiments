Hey there! 😊 Here’s a fresh Sensei profile that zeroes in on appointment scheduling for a wheel change, with multiple dates and times so you can stress-test how the chatbot handles calendar details. Feel free to drop it under `profiles/` in your bike shop project.

```yaml
test_name: wheel_change_appointment_scheduler

llm:
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  temperature: 0.4
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a commuter who depends on your bike daily and needs to book a wheel
    change service. You like to double-check details such as timing, service duration,
    and any preparation steps before dropping by.
  context:
    - You noticed wobbling on both wheels after a recent pothole mishap.
    - Your schedule is tight, so you care a lot about accurate date and time confirmation.
    - You’re hoping the shop can handle the swap quickly so you can get back on the road.
  goals:
    - "Hi! I'd like to schedule a {{service_detail}} on {{appointment_date}} at {{appointment_time}}."
    - "Could you confirm how long the wheel change usually takes?"
    - "Do I need to bring anything specific—like spare parts or documents—for the appointment?"
    - service_detail:
        function: another()
        type: string
        data:
          - front wheel replacement
          - rear wheel replacement
          - full wheel set change
    - appointment_date:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data:
          - tomorrow
          - next Tuesday
          - 2024-11-22
          - 2024-12-02
          - next Friday
          - the first Monday of next month
    - appointment_time:
        function: forward(appointment_date)
        type: string
        data:
          - 9:00 AM
          - 11:30 AM
          - 1:00 PM
          - 2:30 PM
          - 4:45 PM

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - confirmed_date:
        type: date
        description: "The appointment date confirmed by the chatbot."
    - confirmed_time:
        type: string
        description: "The appointment time confirmed by the chatbot, including AM/PM if applicable."
    - confirmed_service:
        type: string
        description: "The service the chatbot confirms it will perform (e.g., wheel change)."
    - preparation_instructions:
        type: string
        description: "Any instructions the chatbot gives about what the user should bring or prepare."

conversation:
  number: combinations
  max_cost: 0.6
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 12
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### Why this setup works nicely:
- **Date & time coverage:** Using `forward()` and `forward(appointment_date)` unlocks the combination matrix, so Sensei will iterate through every pairing you’ve defined.
- **Service variations:** The `service_detail` with `another()` keeps the wheel-change request diverse without exploding the combo count.
- **Extraction targets:** The `chatbot.output` fields give you everything you need to validate date/time handling and any prep instructions the bot provides.

Let me know if you’d like a version with stricter date formats or additional follow-up questions to push the bot even harder. Happy testing! 🚲✨
