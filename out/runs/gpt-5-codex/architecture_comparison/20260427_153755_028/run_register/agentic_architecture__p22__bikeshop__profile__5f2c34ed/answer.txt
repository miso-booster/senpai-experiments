```yaml
test_name: "bike_fit_discomfort_advice"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a dedicated cyclist who wants professional guidance because riding
    has become uncomfortable. You’re hoping for clear advice on whether the
    issue is fit-related or due to worn components.
  context:
    - "You recently upgraded some bike parts but still feel uncomfortable."
    - "You want expert input on adjustments or services before committing to anything."
    - "You prefer to understand your options without booking an appointment yet."
  goals:
    - "Hi, I'm feeling {{symptom_pattern}} in my {{discomfort_area}} when riding my {{bike_type}} bike."
    - "I typically ride for {{ride_frequency}}, and lately it's been uncomfortable."
    - "I'm not sure if the bike fit is off or if parts like the {{suspected_component}} are wearing out."
    - "Could you walk me through any adjustments I could try at home?"
    - "What services does your shop offer that might help diagnose or fix this?"
    - "I'd like to understand options without booking anything yet."
    - discomfort_area:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - knees
          - lower back
          - wrists
          - shoulders
    - symptom_pattern:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - numbness after 20 minutes
          - dull ache during climbs
          - sharp pain when pushing hard
          - tingling whenever I hit bumps
    - bike_type:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - road
          - gravel
          - mountain
    - ride_frequency:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - daily commute
          - weekend group rides
          - touring trips every month
    - suspected_component:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - saddle
          - bottom bracket
          - handlebar tape
          - drivetrain

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Could you please rephrase that?"
  output:
    - possible_causes:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Hypotheses the bot gives about what might be causing the discomfort"
    - recommended_adjustments:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Bike fit tweaks or at-home adjustments suggested to the user"
    - recommended_services:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Shop services or diagnostics the bot recommends"
    - appointment_push:
        type: string
        description: "Any mention of booking or scheduling follow-up actions"

conversation:
  number: combinations(0.6, pairwise)
  goal_style:
    steps: 6
    max_cost: 0.15
  interaction_style:
    - long phrases
```

### Why this works (grounded in `sensei-chat-guide.md`)
- The user persona mirrors the guide’s structure: language, role, and context set expectations for how the simulated user speaks and what they care about.
- Goals use `{{variables}}` declared later, with two `pairwise()` variables so `conversation.number` can leverage `combinations(0.6, pairwise)` as documented.
- `chatbot.output` defines the fields future rules can inspect—especially whether the bot offers actionable adjustments, shop services, or pushes appointments—aligning with the contract guidelines in the doc.
- Interaction style (`long phrases`) nudges the simulator toward detailed queries, matching the scenario’s conversational tone.
