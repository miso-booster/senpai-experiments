Hey! I noticed the original rule referenced `user_request`, which doesn’t exist in your profile’s `chatbot.output`, so Sensei-check couldn’t evaluate it. I simplified the rule to focus on the two fields the profile actually exposes—`contact_options` and `customer_service_number`—and added a tiny guard against empty strings.

Here’s the cleaned-up version:

```yaml
name: customer_service_contact_info
description: Ensure the chatbot provides customer service contact options and phone number.
conversations: 1
oracle: contact_options is not None and contact_options.strip() != "" and customer_service_number is not None and customer_service_number.strip() != ""
on-error: f"contact_options={contact_options!r}, customer_service_number={customer_service_number!r}"
```

Drop this into your `rules/` folder and you should be all set. If you bump into anything else, I’ve got your back! 😊
