Hi there! Happy to help you get this profile back on track 😊. Based on the guidance in `sensei-chat-guide.md`, here’s what I fixed:

- **`llm.temperature` must be a number** (the doc specifies a float), so I set it to `0.2` and added `model_prov: openai` to match the OpenAI model you’re calling.
- In `chatbot.output`, every field needs a valid `type`. I swapped the non-standard `money` type for `currency` (per the doc’s example) and added `type: string` where it was missing so the extractor knows what to capture.

Here’s the corrected YAML:

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.2
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a customer speaking with the virtual assistant of a bike shop.
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: The opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: currency
        description: The price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: currency
        description: The price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: The price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: Actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  interaction_style:
    - default
```
