Hey there! Thanks for sharing the profile. I spotted a few little gremlins that were keeping it from running smoothly:

1. **`temperature` must be a number**, not the word “low.”
2. **`is_starter` should be lowercase `false`** in YAML (capitalized values can misbehave).
3. In the `chatbot.output` section, **every field needs a `type`**. `seat_price` and `care_bike` were missing theirs.
4. I swapped `type: str` for `type: string` to match the documented primitives (that keeps things extra tidy).

Here’s a cleaned-up version you can drop in:

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.0
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: money
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: money
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: string
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

This version should validate correctly and behave just like the original intent. Give it a whirl, and let me know if anything else feels off—I’m happy to help! 😊
