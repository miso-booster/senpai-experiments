Here’s a profile tailored to that “bike discomfort, not sure if fit or wear” scenario. I leaned on `sensei-chat-guide.md` for the structure and cross-checked `bike_service_and_repair_inquirer.yaml` to keep naming consistent but avoided overlap.

```yaml
test_name: bike_fit_discomfort_information

llm:
  temperature: 0.35
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a dedicated cyclist who recently started feeling discomfort while riding.
    You want to figure out whether it comes from bike fit issues or worn components,
    and you’re gathering advice before committing to any service.
  context:
    - "You ride your bike almost daily for commuting and longer weekend endurance rides."
    - "You are unsure whether poor fit or worn components are causing the discomfort."
    - "You prefer to evaluate recommendations first instead of booking an appointment right away."
    - "You take detailed notes so you can compare suggestions later."
  goals:
    - "Explain that you're experiencing discomfort in your {{discomfort_area}} while riding your {{bike_type}}."
    - "Mention that you recently {{recent_change}} and are unsure whether the issue is a fit problem or worn components."
    - "Ask for specific bike fit adjustments or positioning tips you can try at home to relieve the discomfort."
    - "Ask how to check whether any components might be worn or causing the discomfort."
    - "Ask which diagnostic services or evaluations the shop offers to help identify fit or component issues."
    - "Ask about typical pricing or turnaround time for those services, clarifying that you're not ready to book yet."
    - discomfort_area:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - lower back
          - knees
          - wrists
          - neck
          - shoulders
    - bike_type:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - road bike
          - mountain bike
          - gravel bike
          - commuter bike
    - recent_change:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data:
          - installed a new saddle
          - raised the handlebar height
          - started longer training rides
          - swapped to a stiffer wheelset
          - haven't changed anything recently

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, I didn't catch that—could you say that again?"
  output:
    - fit_adjustments:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Bike fit adjustments or positioning tips suggested to relieve discomfort."
    - component_checks:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Inspection steps or signs of wear the chatbot recommends checking."
    - service_recommendations:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Shop services or assessments proposed to diagnose fit or component problems."
    - service_pricing:
        type: string
        description: "Any pricing details or cost ranges provided for those services."
    - service_timeframe:
        type: string
        description: "Expected timeframe or turnaround mentioned for the recommended services."
    - follow_up_guidance:
        type: string
        description: "Advice on next steps the rider can take without booking yet."

conversation:
  number: combinations(0.6, pairwise)
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 25
  interaction_style:
    - long phrases
```

Feel free to save it as something like `bike_fit_discomfort_information.yaml`. It’s ready for downstream rules that check the `fit_adjustments`, `component_checks`, or `service_recommendations` the bot returns. Let me know if you’d like tweaks (e.g., different interaction style or extra outputs)! 😊
