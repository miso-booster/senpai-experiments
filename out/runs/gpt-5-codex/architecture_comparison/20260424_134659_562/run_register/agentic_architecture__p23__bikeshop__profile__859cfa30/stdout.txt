**What was going wrong**

- In profiles, `llm.temperature` must be a number (see *sensei-chat-guide.md*, §3). The value `low` causes Sensei to reject the profile, so I replaced it with a sensible float and added the optional `model_prov` for clarity.
- Each item under `chatbot.output` needs a `type` (same doc, §6). `seat_price` and `care_bike` were missing theirs, and using the built-in `currency`/`string` types keeps everything consistent.

Here’s the fixed profile:

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.2
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  model_prov: openai

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop.
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: currency
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: currency
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

Give it another run and it should load cleanly now. Let me know if you’d like to tweak interaction styles or add rule validations next!
