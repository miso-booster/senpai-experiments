Sure thing! Here’s a ready-to-use Sensei-check rule that enforces a clear statement about lactose-free availability. Since the profile doesn’t exist yet, I picked a future `chatbot.output` field named `lactose_free_availability`. Feel free to rename it later if your profile uses a different slot 😊.

```yaml
name: lactose_free_availability_reported
description: >
  Ensures the chatbot explicitly reports lactose-free product availability.
  Expects the target profile to expose a `lactose_free_availability` field in chatbot.output.
conversations: 1
oracle: lactose_free_availability is not None and lactose_free_availability.strip() != '' and ('available' in lactose_free_availability.lower() or 'stock' in lactose_free_availability.lower())
yields: f"Lactose-free availability not reported clearly: {lactose_free_availability}"
```
