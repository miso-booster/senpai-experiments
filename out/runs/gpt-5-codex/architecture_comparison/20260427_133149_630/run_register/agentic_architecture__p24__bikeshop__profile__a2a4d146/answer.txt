Here’s a ready-to-use Sensei profile that captures the bike shop scenario exactly as you described:

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_general_info_smoke"

llm:
  temperature: 0.2
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a polite customer who is planning to visit the bike shop soon.
    You want to gather general information about the shop's standard services.
  context:
    - "Keep the conversation focused on shop hours, basic pricing, and everyday bike care."
    - "Do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery, or any other unrelated services."
    - "Phrase every question clearly and directly like a normal customer."
  goals:
    - "Greet the assistant and explain that you want general information about the bike shop's basic services."
    - "Ask what the bike shop's opening and closing times are."
    - "Ask how much a new bike tire costs."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask how much a new bike seat costs."
    - "Request general advice on routine bike care, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you say it again?"
  output:
    - shop_hours:
        type: string
        description: "Opening and closing times provided by the assistant."
    - tire_price:
        type: currency
        description: "Quoted price for a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: currency
        description: "Quoted price for a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: "Quoted price for a new bike seat."
    - maintenance_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General bike care guidance offered by the assistant."
    - off_topic_offers:
        type: string
        description: "Any unrelated services or unsolicited suggestions mentioned."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      limit: 12
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### Why this works
- The `user` section keeps the simulated customer firmly on the allowed topics and spells out each required question in the `goals`.
- `chatbot.output` captures every answer you’ll want to validate later: operating hours, pricing for the three services, maintenance advice quality, and a field to note if the bot strays into unrelated territory.
- `goal_style: all_answered` ensures the conversation continues until every question has been addressed (or the safety limit is reached), while a single conversation keeps things simple for smoke testing.

This structure follows the guidance from **`sensei-chat-guide.md`**, especially around organizing user goals and defining `chatbot.output` as the contract for future rules. Let me know if you’d like variants (e.g., different interaction styles or additional output fields) 😊
