Hey there! Thanks for sharing the profile — I spotted a few little bumps that were preventing it from running smoothly. Here’s what needed love:

1. `llm.temperature` must be a number, not `"low"`.  
2. Booleans in YAML need to be lowercase (`false` instead of `False`).  
3. Every field under `chatbot.output` needs an explicit `type`.  
4. It’s a good idea to include a `goal_style` so the conversation knows when to stop.

All fixed up, your profile now looks like this:

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.0
  model: gpt-4o-mini

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: str
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: money
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: money
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: str
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: str
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 10
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

Give this version a spin, and you should be back on track. If anything else misbehaves, just let me know — I’m happy to help! 😊
