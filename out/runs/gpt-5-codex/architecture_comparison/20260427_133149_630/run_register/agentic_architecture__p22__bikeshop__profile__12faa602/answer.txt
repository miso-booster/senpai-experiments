```yaml
test_name: "bike_fit_discomfort_guidance"

  temperature: 0.4

  format:

    You are a recreational cyclist chatting with a bike shop assistant because recent rides have become uncomfortable.

    - "You commute during the week and take longer rides on weekends."
    - "You are unsure whether the discomfort comes from an improper bike fit or worn components."

    - "Explain that you ride a {{bike_type}} and feel discomfort in your {{discomfort_area}}, asking whether the cause might be poor fit or worn parts."
    - "Mention that you ride about {{weekly_mileage}} miles per week and ask for adjustments you could try yourself."
    - "Ask what services the shop offers to diagnose or fix the issue, while clearly stating you are not ready to book an appointment."
    - bike_type:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - road bike
          - hybrid
          - gravel bike
    - discomfort_area:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - hands and wrists
          - lower back
          - knees
          - saddle area
    - weekly_mileage:
        function: another()
        type: int
        data:
          min: 40
          max: 120
          step: 10

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, I didn't follow that. Could you rephrase your question about bike fit or maintenance?"
  output:
    - recommended_adjustments:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Specific bike fit tweaks or component checks the bot suggests."
    - service_options:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Shop services recommended for assessing fit or component wear."
    - appointment_prompt:
        type: string
        description: "How the bot responds regarding booking or scheduling service."

conversation:
  number: combinations(0.6, pairwise)
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - long phrases
    - single questions
```

### Why it works
- **Goal coverage:** The three goal prompts capture symptom description, home adjustments, and service exploration while explicitly avoiding an appointment request.
- **Variable design:** `bike_type` and `discomfort_area` use `pairwise()` so the `conversation.number` setting can sample their combinations efficiently, and `weekly_mileage` varies with an `another()` numeric range (per the guidance in `sensei-chat-guide.md`).
- **Outputs for later rules:** `recommended_adjustments`, `service_options`, and `appointment_prompt` give you structured hooks to verify the advice quality and ensure the chatbot doesn’t push unwanted bookings.
