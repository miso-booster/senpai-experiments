Here’s a ready-to-use profile that gets the chatbot to propose items for a shopping cart 🛒. It follows the structure and best practices from `sensei-chat-guide.md`.

```yaml
test_name: "shopping_cart_item_recommendations"

llm:
  temperature: 0.4
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a proactive online shopper building a cart on a large marketplace.
    Ask the assistant to recommend incremental items, clarify product details,
    and ensure the cart aligns with your scenario.
  context:
    - "You already have a few essentials in the cart and want complementary ideas."
    - "You prefer balanced suggestions across groceries, home, and office needs."
    - "You want to maximise value bundles while avoiding out-of-stock picks."
  goals:
    - "Ask for suggestions to add {{primary_category}} items to your cart for a {{use_case}} scenario."
    - "Clarify recommended quantities or variations for {{secondary_category}} products while staying within a {{budget}} budget."
    - "Request any bundle or promotional offer that would improve the cart."
    - primary_category:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - fresh produce
          - snacks
          - office supplies
          - pet care
    - secondary_category:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - pantry staples
          - beverages
          - cleaning supplies
          - personal care
    - use_case:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - weekly meal prep
          - remote work setup
          - family game night
          - weekend getaway
    - budget:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - "$50"
          - "$75"
          - "$120"
          - "$200"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you rephrase?"
  output:
    - items_recommended:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Specific items the assistant suggests adding to the cart"
    - category_coverage:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Categories addressed by the assistant while recommending items"
    - estimated_total:
        type: float
        description: "Estimated total spend for the recommended additions"
    - promotions_applied:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Promotions or bundles the assistant proposes"

conversation:
  number: combinations(0.6, pairwise)
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 6
    max_cost: 0.1
  interaction_style:
    - random:
        - long phrases
        - make spelling mistakes
        - change your mind
        - single questions
```

### Why these choices?
- **User goals & variables**: Three `pairwise()` variables (`primary_category`, `secondary_category`, `use_case`) guarantee diverse combinations of shopper scenarios without exploding the total cases, as advised in the guide.
- **Budget variability**: A simple `random()` list keeps spending expectations fresh.
- **Chatbot outputs**: The schema (`items_recommended`, `category_coverage`, `estimated_total`, `promotions_applied`) captures exactly what future rules are likely to check when evaluating the cart suggestions.
- **Conversation controls**: `combinations(0.6, pairwise)` plus the interaction styles from the doc create a mix of behaviours (long phrases, typos, mind changes) to stress-test the bot’s recommendation logic.

Feel free to tweak categories, budgets, or interaction styles if you need a different flavour of shopping session!
