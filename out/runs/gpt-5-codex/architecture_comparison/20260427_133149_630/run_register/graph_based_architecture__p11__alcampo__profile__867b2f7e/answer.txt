¡Perfecto! Aquí tienes un perfil nuevecito enfocado en consultar información sobre los servicios de suscripción y sus beneficios, tal como pediste—todo con metas directas y sin variables 😊

```yaml
test_name: Subscription Services Benefits Inquiry

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: Customer interested in understanding Alcampo’s subscription services and perks before signing up.
  context:
    - You want to compare the value of different subscription services before making a commitment.
    - Saving on delivery fees and accessing exclusive perks are especially important to you.
    - Clear instructions on how to join and manage the subscription will help you decide confidently.
  goals:
    - Ask for an overview of the available subscription services at Alcampo.
    - Request a detailed explanation of the benefits included in the Alcampo Más subscription.
    - Inquire about the cost of the subscription and what payment options are accepted.
    - Ask what requirements or eligibility criteria are needed to join the service.
    - Request step-by-step guidance on how to subscribe and manage the membership.

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: Parece que tu mensaje no es claro o no es una pregunta relacionada con Alcampo. 😊 ¿En qué puedo ayudarte hoy? Estoy aquí para resolver tus dudas sobre nuestros productos y servicios.
  output:
    - subscription_services_overview:
        type: string
        description: Summary of the subscription services offered by Alcampo
    - subscription_benefits:
        type: string
        description: Detailed benefits included with the main subscription
    - subscription_cost:
        type: money
        description: Pricing information and accepted payment methods
    - eligibility_requirements:
        type: string
        description: Criteria or conditions needed to join the subscription
    - subscription_guidance:
        type: string
        description: Steps to subscribe, manage, or cancel the membership

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    steps: 20
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

Si quieres ajustar el tono del usuario, añadir alguna personalidad específica o cambiar los campos de salida, dime y lo refinamos juntxs 💪✨
