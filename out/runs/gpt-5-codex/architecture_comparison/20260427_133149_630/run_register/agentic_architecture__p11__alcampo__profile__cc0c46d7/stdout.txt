Here’s a fresh profile focused on gathering subscription-plan details—no goal variables, just clear questions for the simulated user:

```yaml
test_name: "subscription_services_info"

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a curious customer evaluating subscription services for a digital product.
    You want a clear understanding of what each plan offers before making a decision.
  context:
    - "You prefer straightforward explanations with practical examples."
    - "You want to compare the available plans before committing."
  goals:
    - "Ask what subscription plans are currently offered."
    - "Request a breakdown of the pricing tiers and billing frequency."
    - "Ask for a summary of key benefits or perks included with each plan."
    - "Clarify whether there is a free trial or promotional offer."
    - "Confirm the cancellation and refund policy."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm not sure about that. Could you please rephrase your question?"
  output:
    - available_plans:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Names of the subscription plans mentioned."
    - pricing_overview:
        type: string
        description: "Summary of pricing tiers or billing information."
    - benefits_summary:
        type: string
        description: "Main benefits or perks described for the plans."
    - trial_information:
        type: string
        description: "Details about any free trial or promotional offer."
    - cancellation_policy:
        type: string
        description: "Cancellation or refund terms provided by the chatbot."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 6
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### What’s happening here
- The user persona prioritises clarity and comparison, aligning with your “subscription services and benefits” intent.
- Goals are plain strings (no variables) that cover plans, pricing, benefits, trials, and cancellation policies.
- `chatbot.output` captures the key facts you’ll likely validate later (plan names, pricing summary, benefits, trial info, cancellation terms).
- Conversation controls keep it concise—three runs, up to six turns each, with the simulator asking one goal per turn.
