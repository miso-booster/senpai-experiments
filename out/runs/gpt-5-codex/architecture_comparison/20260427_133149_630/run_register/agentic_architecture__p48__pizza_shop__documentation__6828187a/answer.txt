Hey there! 😊 Here's what the official guide (`sensei-chat-guide.md`) says about non-mandatory pieces in a profile:

### Optional at the top level
- **`llm` block** – The FAQ explicitly notes you can leave it out if you’re fine with the defaults for model/temperature/format. Add it only when you need overrides or speech mode.
  
### Optional inside specific sections
- **`llm.format.config`** – Only needed when `format.type` is `speech`; for normal text runs you can skip it.
- **`user.context` entries** – The guide calls the persona snippets “optional prompt fragments,” so you can omit the whole list or just the `personality` lines if you don’t need extra flavour.
- **Many `conversation` controls** – Fields like `conversation.max_cost`, `goal_style.max_cost`, or even the entire `interaction_style` block are optional; use them only when you need cost caps or special behaviours.

### What probably needs to stay
The docs don’t spell out a strict minimal schema, but every example keeps the core blocks: `test_name`, `user`, `chatbot`, and `conversation`. Dropping those would leave Sensei without enough instructions to run.

So, in short: keep the structural backbone, but feel free to skip the `llm` settings, persona/context extras, and any of the advanced conversation controls unless your scenario calls for them. If you’re ever unsure, start from the template in the guide and trim the pieces you don’t need.
