Hey there! 😊 I put together a fresh Sensei profile tailored to that rider who’s feeling discomfort and isn’t sure whether it’s a fit issue or worn components. This user focuses on gathering advice—no appointment requests—so the chatbot can respond with guidance, adjustments, and service suggestions. You can drop it under `profiles/bike_fit_discomfort_advice_seeker.yaml` (or your preferred name). Here’s the full YAML:

```yaml
test_name: bike_fit_discomfort_advice_seeker

llm:
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  temperature: 0.4
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    A regular rider who has started to feel discomfort on the bike and wants to understand
    whether the fit is off or certain components are wearing out. You’re looking for
    guidance on adjustments or services the shop might offer, but you’re not trying
    to book anything yet.
  context:
    - You commute several times a week and also ride longer routes on weekends.
    - Lately you’ve felt more fatigue and soreness after rides.
    - You’d prefer to gather concrete advice before deciding to bring the bike in.
  goals:
    - "Explain that you’ve been experiencing {{discomfort_area}} discomfort while riding and ask whether it could be due to bike fit or worn components."
    - "Ask what adjustments or fit checks you can try to relieve the discomfort."
    - "Ask if the shop offers any bike fitting or assessment services and what they include."
    - "Ask how to tell if your {{component_concern}} needs to be replaced."
    - "Ask for any at-home inspection or maintenance steps to try before visiting the shop."
    - discomfort_area:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data:
          - knee
          - lower back
          - wrists and hands
          - neck and shoulders
    - component_concern:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data:
          - chain
          - brake pads
          - saddle
          - wheel bearings

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - discomfort_cause_guidance:
        type: string
        description: Guidance the chatbot gives about whether the discomfort is likely due to bike fit, component wear, or both.
    - adjustment_recommendations:
        type: string
        description: Suggested bike fit tweaks or riding-position changes to ease the discomfort.
    - fitting_service_details:
        type: string
        description: Information about any bike fitting or assessment services the shop offers.
    - component_wear_advice:
        type: string
        description: Tips for checking whether the specified component shows signs of wear or needs replacement.
    - at_home_checks:
        type: string
        description: DIY inspection or maintenance steps the chatbot recommends trying before scheduling service.
    - appointment_offer:
        type: string
        description: Whether the chatbot suggests booking an appointment even though the user didn’t request one.

conversation:
  number: 4
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 20
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

Feel free to tweak the discomfort areas or component list if you want different coverage. Let me know if you’d like companion Sensei rules or a custom type for any extracted fields! 🚴‍♂️✨
