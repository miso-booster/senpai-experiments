Here’s a fresh Sensei profile you can drop under `profiles/` to exercise the bike shop assistant exactly the way you described:

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_basic_info_profile"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a normal customer visiting a neighborhood bike shop.
    You want clear, accurate information about everyday services and pricing.
    Speak politely and phrase each question clearly and directly.
  context:
    - "Keep the conversation focused on opening hours, standard service pricing, and general bike care tips."
    - "Do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, rentals, or any other unrelated topics."
    - "If the assistant introduces unrelated services or advanced repairs, steer the conversation back to shop hours, basic pricing, or routine maintenance advice."
  goals:
    - "Greet the assistant and explain that you are gathering general information about the bike shop and its standard services."
    - "Ask for the shop's opening and closing times."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Request general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices rather than advanced repairs."
    - "Thank the assistant once all questions have been answered."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, could you please rephrase that?"
  output:
    - opening_hours:
        type: string
        description: "Stated opening and closing times for the bike shop."
    - tire_price:
        type: currency
        description: "Quoted price for a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: currency
        description: "Quoted price for a standard bike tune-up service."
    - seat_price:
        type: currency
        description: "Quoted price for a new bike seat."
    - maintenance_advice:
        type: string
        description: "Summary of the basic bike care advice the chatbot provided."
    - unrelated_services_mentioned:
        type: string
        description: "List any unrelated services or topics the chatbot introduced; leave empty if none."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      limit: 12
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

### Why it matches your brief
- The **user role, context, and goals** enforce a friendly, on-topic customer who hits every required question (hours, three prices, maintenance advice) and avoids forbidden topics.
- `conversation.goal_style.all_answered` keeps the simulator on task until each goal is covered, with a safety limit so it won’t loop forever.
- `chatbot.output` captures the exact information you’ll likely want to validate later (hours, each price, maintenance tips, and any off-topic suggestions).

I put this together following the structure and best practices from `sensei-chat-guide.md`, ensuring the profile is ready for downstream Sensei-check rules.
