Sure thing! I drafted a rule-first check you can plug in once the corresponding profile exists. It assumes the future profile will expose `requested_product_category` and `lactose_free_availability` in `chatbot.output`. Feel free to tweak the field names later if your schema evolves.

```yaml
name: lactose_free_availability_check
description: >
  Ensures the assistant clearly reports availability when lactose-free products are requested.
  Rule-first design targeting a future lactose-free product inquiry profile.
active: false
conversations: 1
if: requested_product_category is not None and 'lactose' in requested_product_category.lower()
then: lactose_free_availability is not None and semantic_content(lactose_free_availability, 'Clearly states whether lactose-free products are available or unavailable')
on-error: >
  f"Lactose-free availability missing or unclear. requested_product_category={requested_product_category}, lactose_free_availability={lactose_free_availability}"
```

When you’re ready, just align the profile’s `chatbot.output` with those fields, flip `active` to true, and you’re good to go!
