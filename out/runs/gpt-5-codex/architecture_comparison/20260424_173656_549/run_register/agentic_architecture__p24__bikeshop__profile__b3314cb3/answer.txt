Here’s a fresh Sensei Chat profile tailored to exercise a bike shop assistant exactly the way you described 😊:

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_information_check"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a polite customer contacting a neighborhood bike shop to learn about its general services and pricing.
    Ask clear, direct questions and behave like a normal walk-in customer.
  context:
    - "You only want basic information about shop hours, standard pricing, and everyday bike care."
    - "Do not ask about appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, or other specialized topics."
  goals:
    - "Greet the assistant and mention that you are looking for general information about the bike shop."
    - "Ask what the bike shop's opening and closing times are."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Request general advice on how to take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."
    - "Thank the assistant and confirm that you received the information you needed."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, could you rephrase that?"
  output:
    - opening_hours:
        type: string
        description: "The bike shop's opening and closing times provided by the assistant."
    - tire_price:
        type: string
        description: "Quoted price or price range for a new bike tire."
    - tuneup_price:
        type: string
        description: "Quoted price or price range for a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: string
        description: "Quoted price or price range for a new bike seat."
    - maintenance_advice:
        type: string
        description: "Summary of the assistant's basic bicycle care and maintenance guidance."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      limit: 12
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

**Why it fits (based on `sensei-chat-guide.md`):**
- Keeps the simulated user squarely focused on hours, basic pricing, and general care, matching your constraints.
- Uses clear goal prompts so the assistant is tested on each required question.
- Defines `chatbot.output` fields for hours, the three prices, and maintenance advice so rules can validate the responses later.
- Sets a single conversation with `goal_style: all_answered` and default interaction style for a natural, customer-like flow.
