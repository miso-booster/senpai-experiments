In Sensei, a **metamorphic rule** is a special kind of Sensei-check rule that evaluates the relationship between **multiple conversations at once** rather than judging a single dialog in isolation. Instead of saying “this single answer must be X,” it checks how outputs should change (or stay the same) when the inputs vary in a controlled way across different conversations.

Think of it like this:

- You generate a batch of conversations with Sensei Chat profiles.
- A metamorphic rule picks pairs (or larger sets) of those conversations that meet a specific filter.
- It then asserts a relationship between their outputs. For example, “if both orders are the same size but one has more toppings, its price should be higher.”

This approach is super handy when there isn’t one perfect “correct” answer, but there *is* a reliable pattern you expect the chatbot to follow.
