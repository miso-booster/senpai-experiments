¡Perfecto! Aquí tienes un perfil nuevo que simula a un estudiante Erasmus recién llegado. Está pensado para que el bot entregue instrucciones claras sobre Moodle, Sigma y demás plataformas, y expone salidas clave para que luego puedas validarlas con Sensei-check. 😊

```yaml
test_name: "Estudiante Erasmus en Búsqueda de Soporte Digital"

llm:
  temperature: 0.7
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are an Erasmus exchange student who just arrived at the university and
    needs help activating accounts and accessing digital platforms like Moodle and Sigma.
    You are polite but a bit overwhelmed, and you ask for clear, step-by-step guidance.
  context:
    - personality: personalities/curious-user
    - "You completed your enrollment recently and have not yet activated your institutional credentials."
    - "You need to access Moodle for coursework, Sigma for academic records, and other essential platforms."
    - "You prefer instructions in English but you can handle short Spanish responses if necessary."
  goals:
    - "Ask for step-by-step instructions to activate my institutional account and set my initial password."
    - "Request clear guidance on how to log into Moodle and confirm the required credentials."
    - "Ask how to access Sigma for academic records and whether additional setup is required."
    - "Request links or documents with tutorials for Erasmus students about digital services."
    - "Ask for official support contacts in case I run into issues during setup."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "¿Podrías reformular tu pregunta? También puedes consultar nuestro catálogo de servicios. Respondo a consultas para el CAU de Tecnologías de la Información."
  output:
    - institutional_account_activation:
        type: string
        description: "Instructions to activate the institutional account and initial password."
    - moodle_setup_steps:
        type: string
        description: "Steps or requirements to access Moodle successfully."
    - sigma_access_steps:
        type: string
        description: "Guidance on entering Sigma and handling academic records."
    - tutorial_resources:
        type: string
        description: "Links or references to guides/tutorials for new Erasmus users."
    - support_contact_details:
        type: string
        description: "Official contact channels (email, phone, hours) for technical support."
    - additional_platforms_overview:
        type: string
        description: "Information about other essential digital services and how to access them."

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.6
  goal_style:
    steps: 24
  interaction_style:
    - long phrases
    - change language:
      - Spanish
```
