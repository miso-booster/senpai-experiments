Hey there! Thanks for sharing the profile 💛 I spotted a few little hiccups that were likely stopping it from passing validation:

1. `temperature` has to be a number, not the word “low”.  
2. YAML booleans need to be lowercase (`true`/`false`).  
3. Every field in `chatbot.output` needs an explicit `type`.

Here’s a cleaned-up version you can drop in. It keeps everything else the same, just with valid values and a goal style so the run ends once all questions are asked.

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.0
  model: gpt-4o-mini

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: true
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: str
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: money
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: money
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: str
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: str
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 9
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

Give this version a spin in Sensei and it should validate right away. If you want any other tweaks (different conversation count, interaction style, etc.), just let me know—I’m happy to help! 🚴‍♀️✨
