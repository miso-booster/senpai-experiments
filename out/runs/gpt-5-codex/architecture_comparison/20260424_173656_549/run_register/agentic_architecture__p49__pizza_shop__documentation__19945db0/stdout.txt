Hi! I just checked the Sensei-check rule docs we have on hand (`sensei-check-doc.md` and `rules-examples.md`), and they don’t mention any helper or function literally called `pairwise`. Instead, Sensei-check handles pairwise comparisons by letting you write metamorphic rules with `conversations: 2`, and then you refer to the two matched conversations through `conv[0]` and `conv[1]`. You control which conversations get paired using the `when:` filter, and the relation you want to enforce goes in `oracle:` or in an `if`/`then` block.  

So, for example (from `sensei-check-doc.md`), the toppings monotonicity rule is “pairwise” because it sets `conversations: 2`, filters comparable cases in `when:`, and then compares `conv[0]` and `conv[1]`. There’s no separate `pairwise()` helper; the DSL gives you direct access to the two conversations instead.
