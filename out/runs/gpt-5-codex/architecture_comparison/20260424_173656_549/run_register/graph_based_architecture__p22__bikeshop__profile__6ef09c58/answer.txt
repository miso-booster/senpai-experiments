```yaml
test_name: bike_fit_discomfort_advice

llm:
  format:
    type: text
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  temperature: 0.5

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are an avid cyclist who recently began feeling uncomfortable on your bike.
    You're unsure whether the fit is off or if certain components are worn, and you
    want expert guidance before committing to any service.
  context:
    - You've noticed recurring discomfort during longer rides and want to avoid making the problem worse.
    - You're open to professional services but first want a clearer picture of adjustments or inspections that might help.
    - You prefer collecting thorough advice before deciding on any appointments or purchases.
  goals:
    - "Explain that you're experiencing discomfort in your {{discomfort_area}} while riding."
    - "Share that you’re unsure whether the issue is a bike fit problem or worn {{components_concern}} components."
    - "Ask what adjustments or fitting services the shop recommends to address the discomfort."
    - "Ask which inspections or maintenance services the shop offers to check for worn parts."
    - "Ask for any at-home checks or observations you can perform before bringing the bike in."
    - discomfort_area:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - lower back
          - wrists and hands
          - knees
          - neck and shoulders
    - components_concern:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - saddle
          - handlebar and stem
          - drivetrain
          - suspension fork

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - fit_adjustment_advice:
        type: string
        description: "Guidance the chatbot gives on bike-fit adjustments to relieve the reported discomfort."
    - service_recommendations:
        type: string
        description: "Shop services suggested for assessing or correcting fit issues and worn components."
    - component_wear_guidance:
        type: string
        description: "Advice on how to identify whether the mentioned component is worn or needs replacement."
    - at_home_checklist:
        type: string
        description: "Any do-it-yourself checks, measurements, or observations recommended before visiting the shop."

conversation:
  number: combinations(0.75, pairwise)
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 18
  interaction_style:
    - default
```
