Here’s a tidy rule you can drop into your `rules/` folder to validate those outputs 😊:

```yaml
name: qa_expected_prices_and_hours
description: Ensures the QA profile returns the expected hours and prices.
conversations: 1
oracle: |
  opening_and_closing
  and '10' in opening_and_closing
  and '20' in opening_and_closing
  and extract_float(tire) == 20
  and extract_float(tune_up) == 50
  and extract_float(seat_price) == 20
on-error: |
  f"Unexpected values: hours='{opening_and_closing}', tire='{tire}', tune_up='{tune_up}', seat='{seat_price}'"
```

This rule checks that the opening hours mention both “10” and “20,” and the tire, tune-up, and seat prices match the exact amounts you specified. Let me know if you’d like to expand it to cover care instructions or currency validation too!
