Here you go! This rule activates whenever the scenario involves a small pizza and verifies that the extracted price is exactly $10 USD. 🍕💵

```yaml
name: small_pizza_price_exact
description: Small pizzas cost exactly $10 (pizza_order_test_predefined profile)
active: true
conversations: 1
when: 'small' in size
oracle: extract_float(price) == 10 and currency(price) == 'USD'
on-error: f"Expected a $10 small pizza, but got {price}"
```
