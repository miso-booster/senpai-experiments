### Why this fits your brief
- **Date & time coverage:** `appointment_date` and `appointment_time` both use `pairwise()` so Sensei explores many combinations when `conversation.number` is set to `combinations(0.75, pairwise)`. The fallback variables (`backup_date`, `backup_time`) nudge the chatbot into rescheduling paths.
- **Outputs for later rules:** Fields such as `promised_date` and `promised_time` (typed as `date`/`time`) plus the raw `requested_*` strings give you everything you need to validate how the bot handled scheduling later.
- **User behaviour:** Interaction styles (`change your mind`, `random` extras) encourage the simulator to push for alternate slots, exposing how the bot reacts.
