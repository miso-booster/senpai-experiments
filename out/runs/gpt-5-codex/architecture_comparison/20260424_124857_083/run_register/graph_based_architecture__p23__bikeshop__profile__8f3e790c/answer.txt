Hi there! How can I help you? 😊 Sounds like this QA profile just needs a couple of tweaks to line up with Sensei’s expectations. Here’s what was holding it back:

1. `llm.temperature` has to be a number—`low` isn’t recognized.  
2. `chatbot.output` entries must always specify a `type`; two of them were missing.  
3. While `False` works in YAML, Sensei tends to prefer lowercase booleans and quoted strings for safety.

I’ve cleaned everything up below. This version mirrors the working `qa.yml` in your project, so it should validate and run without hiccups:

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.0
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: string
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: money
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: money
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: string
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: string
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 9
  interaction_style:
    - default
```
