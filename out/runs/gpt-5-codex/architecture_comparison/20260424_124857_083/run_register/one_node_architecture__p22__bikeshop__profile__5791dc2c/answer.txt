Absolutely! I’ve put together a new profile that captures a concerned rider seeking fit and maintenance advice—without nudging the bot toward booking an appointment. Have a look 👇

```yaml
test_name: bike_fit_discomfort_advice

llm:
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  temperature: 0.5
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a cyclist experiencing discomfort during rides and want to understand
    whether the issue comes from poor bike fit or worn components. You’re looking
    for guidance and suggestions without committing to scheduling anything yet.
  context:
    - You recently started feeling persistent discomfort while riding and want to fix the issue.
    - You are unsure whether the bike fit is wrong or some components are worn out.
    - You prefer gathering detailed advice before deciding on any service visit.
    - You are not ready to book an appointment and will only ask for information.
  goals:
    - "Describe where you feel discomfort around your {{discomfort_area}} and ask if it could be related to bike fit or worn parts."
    - "Ask for adjustments you can try on your own to improve overall comfort."
    - "Ask how to tell if the {{component}} might need to be repaired or replaced."
    - "Ask what services the shop offers to diagnose or resolve bike fit or comfort issues."
    - "Ask for any quick checks or maintenance tips to try before bringing the bike in."
    - discomfort_area:
        type: string
        function: forward()
        data:
          - lower back
          - shoulders
          - knees
          - wrists
          - neck
    - component:
        type: string
        function: forward(discomfort_area)
        data:
          - saddle
          - handlebars
          - drivetrain
          - brake pads
          - pedals

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - fit_adjustment_advice:
        type: string
        description: >
          Guidance the chatbot gives about bike fit adjustments to improve rider comfort.
    - component_wear_guidance:
        type: string
        description: >
          Information on signs that a specific component (e.g., saddle, handlebars, drivetrain,
          brake pads, pedals) might be worn or failing.
    - diagnostic_services_information:
        type: string
        description: >
          Details about services the shop offers to assess fit or diagnose comfort-related issues.
    - self_help_tips:
        type: string
        description: >
          Quick checks or maintenance steps the user can try before visiting the shop.

conversation:
  number: 5
  max_cost: 0.6
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: true
      limit: 18
  interaction_style:
    - default
```
