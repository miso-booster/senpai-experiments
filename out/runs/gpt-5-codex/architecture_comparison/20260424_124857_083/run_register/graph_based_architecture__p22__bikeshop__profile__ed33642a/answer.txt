Absolutely! I’ve put together a Sensei profile tailored for a rider who’s feeling discomfort and wants advice on bike fitting and component wear, without jumping straight into booking an appointment. Here’s a quick rundown before the full YAML:

- **User persona:** A concerned cyclist looking for expert guidance before taking action.
- **Goals covered:** Asking about fit assessments, adjustment suggestions, spotting worn parts, relevant services, and potential costs.
- **Variables:** Rotating discomfort areas, bike types, and components to keep the scenarios varied.
- **Outputs:** Capture the assistant’s guidance on fit services, adjustment tips, wear indicators, service options, and pricing.

You can drop this file into `profiles/bike_fit_discomfort_advice_seeker.yaml` within your project:

```yaml
test_name: bike_fit_discomfort_advice_seeker

llm:
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  temperature: 0.5
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a rider who feels uncomfortable on your bike and wants expert guidance
    on whether the issue is related to bike fit or worn components.
  context:
    - You've recently noticed aches during rides and want to resolve them before your next long outing.
    - You'd prefer to understand your options before committing to any service appointments.
    - You rely on your bike for regular commutes and weekend rides.
  goals:
    - "Mention that you experience {{discomfort_area}} on your {{bike_type}} and ask if the shop offers bike fitting assessments."
    - "Ask what adjustments might help relieve the discomfort, such as saddle height, handlebar reach, or cleat alignment."
    - "Ask how to tell if the {{component_of_concern}} might be worn and due for replacement."
    - "Ask about services the shop offers to diagnose or adjust bikes for comfort issues."
    - "Ask about the typical cost of a professional bike fitting or adjustment session."
    - discomfort_area:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - knee pain
          - lower back stiffness
          - wrist numbness
          - neck strain
    - component_of_concern:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - saddle padding
          - handlebar tape
          - drivetrain
          - pedal cleats
    - bike_type:
        function: forward(discomfort_area)
        type: string
        data:
          - road bike
          - mountain bike
          - commuter bike
          - gravel bike

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - fit_assessment_availability:
        type: string
        description: Confirmation or denial that the shop offers bike fitting or comfort assessment services.
    - adjustment_recommendations:
        type: string
        description: The specific adjustments suggested by the chatbot to address the rider's discomfort.
    - component_wear_guidance:
        type: string
        description: Guidance on how to identify wear on the mentioned component and whether it needs replacement.
    - service_options:
        type: string
        description: A summary of the services the shop offers for diagnosing fit issues or worn components.
    - fit_service_price:
        type: money
        description: The quoted price (or price range) for a fitting or adjustment service.

conversation:
  number: combinations(0.6, pairwise)
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 18
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

Feel free to tweak the goal wording or variable lists if you want even more variety. Let me know if you’d like matching Sensei-check rules or connector tips next! 😊
