Hey there! Here’s a brand-new Sensei profile you can drop under `profiles/` (for example, `profiles/wheel_change_appointment_scheduler.yaml`). It focuses on an appointment-seeker who wants their bike’s wheels changed, and it cycles through multiple date/time combinations so you can watch how the chatbot handles scheduling details 🗓️🔧

```yaml
test_name: wheel_change_appointment_scheduler

llm:
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  temperature: 0.5
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are an organized cyclist trying to book a wheel change at a neighborhood
    bike shop. You expect the assistant to confirm the date, time, and service,
    and you double-check that everything is set.
  context:
    - You recently replaced your tires and now want the shop to install them properly.
    - You try to be flexible with your schedule but still prefer clear confirmation.
    - You’d like to know if there’s anything to prepare before dropping off the bike.
  goals:
    - "Schedule a wheel change appointment for {{appointment_date}} at {{appointment_time}}."
    - "Confirm the service is specifically a wheel change."
    - "Ask whether you need to prepare anything before the appointment."
    - appointment_date:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - tomorrow
          - next Monday
          - next Wednesday
          - "July 18th, 2024"
    - appointment_time:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - "9:00 AM"
          - "11:15 AM"
          - "2:30 PM"
          - "4:45 PM"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - confirmation_message:
        type: string
        description: >
          The chatbot's explicit confirmation that the appointment has been booked.
          Look for language such as "You're all set" or "Your appointment is confirmed."
    - scheduled_date:
        type: date
        description: "The appointment date confirmed by the chatbot."
    - scheduled_time:
        type: time("hh:mm am/pm")
        description: "The appointment time confirmed by the chatbot."
    - service_confirmed:
        type: string
        description: >
          The service the chatbot has scheduled. Should reference a wheel change
          or similar wording.

conversation:
  number: combinations(1, pairwise)
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 12
  interaction_style:
    - default
  max_cost: 0.6
```

### Why it’s handy
- **Pairwise date/time variables** → Sensei will generate a variety of combinations while keeping the run size reasonable.
- **Clear `chatbot.output` schema** → lets you plug in metamorphic rules later (e.g., checking that the confirmed date matches the requested one).
- **Goal flow** keeps the simulator focused on appointment handling without extra fluff.

If you need any tweaks—like adding weekend requests, pushing times into the evening, or mixing in interaction styles such as `change your mind`—just say the word! 😊
