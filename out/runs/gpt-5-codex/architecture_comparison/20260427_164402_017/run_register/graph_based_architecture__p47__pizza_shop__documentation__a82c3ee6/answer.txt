Great question! 😊 In Sensei, a **metamorphic rule** is part of the Sensei-check system—a DSL that lets you verify how your chatbot behaves across *multiple conversations* instead of just checking single responses.

Here’s the idea in plain terms:

- Instead of saying “Conversation A must have property X,” a metamorphic rule says: **“Whenever Conversation A and Conversation B meet certain conditions, their outputs should relate in a specific way.”**
- These rules compare pairs or tuples of conversations, looking for consistent, logical relationships—like checking that adding more pizza toppings should never make the price go down across similar orders.

So, metamorphic rules are perfect when you want to test *relations* between different scenarios generated by your Sensei profiles. They help catch weird inconsistencies by expressing expectations such as “If input changes this way, the output should change that way,” which is super handy when absolute right-or-wrong answers are hard to define.
