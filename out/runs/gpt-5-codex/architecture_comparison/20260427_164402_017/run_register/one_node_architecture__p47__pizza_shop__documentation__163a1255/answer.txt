Hey there! 😊 In Sensei, a **metamorphic rule** is a check you write in Sensei-check (our testing DSL) that evaluates **how the chatbot’s responses change when the inputs change across multiple conversations**. Instead of judging a single dialog in isolation, metamorphic rules compare two or more conversations that meet specific conditions and assert a relationship that should hold between their outputs.

For example, you might say: “If two pizza orders have the same size and drinks but one has more toppings, the one with more toppings should cost more.” The rule then looks at the structured data extracted from those conversations (via the profile’s `chatbot.output`) and verifies that this relational condition remains true. It’s a powerful way to validate consistency and business logic without needing a “correct” reference answer for every possible interaction.
