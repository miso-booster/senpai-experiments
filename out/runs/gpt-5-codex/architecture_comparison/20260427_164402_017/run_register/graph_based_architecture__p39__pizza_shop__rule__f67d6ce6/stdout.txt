Hey hey! 😊 Here’s a metamorphic rule you can drop into your Sensei-check project to verify that adding a drink bumps the price by exactly $2, while keeping everything else the same.

**What it checks**

- Same pizza size and toppings across the pair of conversations.
- Exactly one of those conversations includes a drink (`conv[i].drink` not in `[None, "", "none"]`).
- The price with the drink must be precisely $2 more than the price without it.

```yaml
name: drink
description: Adding a drink to the same pizza order should add exactly $2 to the total price
conversations: 2
when: conv[0].size == conv[1].size and sorted(conv[0].toppings) == sorted(conv[1].toppings)
if: (conv[0].drink in [None, '', 'none']) != (conv[1].drink in [None, '', 'none'])
then: (conv[0].drink in [None, '', 'none'] and extract_float(conv[1].price) == extract_float(conv[0].price) + 2) or (conv[1].drink in [None, '', 'none'] and extract_float(conv[0].price) == extract_float(conv[1].price) + 2)
```

✨ **Heads-up:** If your “no drink” value is written differently (e.g., `no_drink`, `without_drink`), just swap that list in both the `if` and `then` clauses so it matches your profile’s `chatbot.output`.
