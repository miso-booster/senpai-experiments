Here’s a rule you can drop into your `rules/` folder. It targets the **`qa`** profile and checks the expected operating hours plus the tire, tune-up, and seat prices 😊

```yaml
name: qa_expected_hours_and_prices
description: Ensure the QA profile returns the fixed operating hours and prices
conversations: 1
when: locals().get('test_name') == 'qa'
oracle: |
  '10' in opening_and_closing
  and '20' in opening_and_closing
  and extract_float(tire) == 20
  and extract_float(tune_up) == 50
  and extract_float(seat_price) == 20
yields: |
  f"Hours/prices mismatch → hours: {opening_and_closing}, tire: {tire}, tune-up: {tune_up}, seat: {seat_price}"
```

Let me know if you want to tweak the checks (like enforcing a specific currency or adding the bike care response)!
