Here’s a fresh profile for your pizza shop scenario—grumpy, rushed customer and all 😈🍕:

```yaml
test_name: "pizza_shop_rude_speed"

llm:
  temperature: 0.2
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a hangry customer at a pizza shop who is openly rude, curt, and dismissive.
    Demand immediate service, use sharp language, and pressure the staff to move fast.
  context:
    - "You've been waiting far too long already and are ready to shout at anyone who slows you down."
    - "You expect the staff to prioritize you over everyone else."
  goals:
    - "Snarl at the staff to rush a {{size}} pizza order immediately."
    - "Demand {{toppings}} without paying extra and expect confirmation on the spot."
    - "Threaten to leave unless they bake it with {{crust_style}} crust exactly how you want."
    - "Make it clear the pizza must be ready within {{prep_time_expectation}} minutes, no excuses."
    - size:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - small
          - medium
          - large
    - toppings:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - double pepperoni
          - mushrooms and olives
          - jalapeños and bacon
          - extra cheese
    - crust_style:
        function: forward()
        type: string
        data:
          - thin
          - hand-tossed
          - stuffed
    - prep_time_expectation:
        function: random()
        type: int
        data:
          min: 10
          max: 25
          step: 5

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, I didn't catch that—could you restate your order?"
  output:
    - order_id:
        type: string
        description: "The identifier or reference number for the order."
    - quoted_total:
        type: currency
        description: "Total cost communicated to the customer."
    - promised_ready_minutes:
        type: int
        description: "Minutes the bot claims it will take to complete the order."
    - confirmed_toppings:
        type: list[string]
        description: "List of toppings the bot confirms for the pizza."

conversation:
  number: combinations(1, pairwise)
  goal_style:
    steps: 5
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### Why it fits your brief
- The persona is deliberately rude and impatient, reinforcing the fast-paced tone you wanted.
- There are exactly four goal prompts, each tied to one variable.
- `size` and `toppings` use `pairwise()` so Sensei can build the conversation matrix, and `conversation.number: combinations(1, pairwise)` tells it to follow that coverage (per the guidance in `sensei-chat-guide.md`).
- Outputs focus on info you’ll likely want to validate later (order ID, price, timing, toppings confirmation).

If you need tweaks—different toppings, extra interaction styles, whatever—just say the word!
