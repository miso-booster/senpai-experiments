Hey there! 😊 Thanks for sharing the profile — I see why it was giving you trouble.

### What was going on
1. **`llm.temperature`** was set to the word `low`, but Sensei expects a number (like `0.0` or `0.2`).  
2. In `chatbot.output`, the fields `seat_price` and `care_bike` were missing their `type`, so the extractor didn’t know how to parse them.  
3. I also switched the boolean to lowercase (`false`) and wrapped the fallback in quotes — that’s just to keep the YAML tidy and unambiguous.

### Fixed profile
Here’s a clean version you can drop in:

```yaml
test_name: qa

llm:
  temperature: 0.0
  model: gpt-4o-mini

user:
  language: English
  role: you have to act as a user talking to a virtual assistant of a bike shop
  context:
    - You are interested in asking the items below.
    - Just stick to asking opening times, prices of tire, price of tune-up, price of seat, and actions to the care of bike. Do not ask anything else.
  goals:
    - "opening and closing time of the bike shop"
    - "Which is the price of a new tire?"
    - "Which is the price of a tune-up?"
    - "Which is the price of a new seat?"
    - "What can I do to take care of my bike?"

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - opening_and_closing:
        type: str
        description: the opening and closing time of the shop
    - tire:
        type: money
        description: the price of a new tire
    - tune_up:
        type: money
        description: the price of a tune-up
    - seat_price:
        type: str
        description: the price of a new seat
    - care_bike:
        type: str
        description: actions to take care of the bike

conversation:
  number: 5
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

Give it a spin now — it should validate and run just fine. If you need to adjust the conversation flow or add extraction types later, I’m here for it! 🚴‍♀️💬
