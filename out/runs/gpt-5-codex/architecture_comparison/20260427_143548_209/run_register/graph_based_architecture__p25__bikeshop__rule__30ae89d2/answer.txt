Here’s a single-conversation Sensei-check rule that validates the outputs exposed by your bike-shop profile:

- It makes sure the opening hours mention both **10** and **20**.
- It checks that the prices for **tire**, **tune-up**, and **seat** all line up with the expected values.
- It also confirms the assistant actually provides some text for the bike-care advice.

```yaml
name: bike_shop_expected_values
description: Confirms the bike shop profile reports the expected hours and prices
conversations: 1
oracle: |
  (
    isinstance(opening_and_closing, str)
    and '10' in opening_and_closing
    and '20' in opening_and_closing
    and abs(extract_float(tire) - 20) < 0.01
    and abs(extract_float(tune_up) - 50) < 0.01
    and abs(extract_float(seat_price) - 20) < 0.01
    and len(str(care_bike).strip()) > 0
  )
on-error: f"Opening={opening_and_closing}, tire={tire}, tune_up={tune_up}, seat={seat_price}, care_bike={care_bike}"
```

Drop this into your rules folder (for example, `rules/bike_shop_expected_values.yaml`) and you’re good to go. Let me know if you’d like to tighten or expand any of the checks! 🚲✨
