Hey, thanks for sharing the draft rule! I tweaked it so it follows the Sensei-check syntax and actually enforces the consistency you’re looking for. The idea is:

* Only look at pairs where one user asks for general contact options and the other asks specifically for the customer service number.
* If both conversations captured the relevant outputs, make sure the number provided in the second case is also present inside the broader contact options response.

Here’s the polished version you can drop into your rules folder:

```yaml
name: customer_service_contact_info
description: Ensure the chatbot provides consistent contact options and customer service number.
conversations: 2
when: conv[0].user_request == 'contact options' and conv[1].user_request == 'customer service number'
if: conv[0].contact_options is not None and conv[1].customer_service_number is not None
then: str(conv[1].customer_service_number) in str(conv[0].contact_options)
```

💡 Quick reminder: this assumes your profile exposes `contact_options` and `customer_service_number` exactly with those names. If they differ, just update the field names accordingly. Let me know if you’d like an `on-error` message or further validation rules!
