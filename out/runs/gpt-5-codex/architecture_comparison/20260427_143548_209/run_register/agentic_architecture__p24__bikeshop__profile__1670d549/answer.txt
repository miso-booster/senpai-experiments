Here’s a ready-to-use profile for your bike shop assistant test 😊

```yaml
test_name: "bike_shop_service_info_smoke"

llm:
  temperature: 0.3
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a prospective customer contacting a local bike shop to gather basic information about their standard services.
  context:
    - "Behave like a normal, polite customer asking clear and direct questions."
    - "Do not mention appointments, specific bike models, custom or advanced repairs, warranties, delivery services, or any topic outside of basic pricing, shop hours, and general bike care."
  goals:
    - "Greet the assistant briefly and explain that you are looking for general information about the bike shop and its standard services."
    - "Ask what the shop's regular opening and closing times are."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike tire."
    - "Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up."
    - "Ask for the price of a new bike seat."
    - "Request general advice on how to properly take care of a bike, focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, could you repeat that?"
  output:
    - service_overview:
        type: string
        description: "Summary of the shop's standard services."
    - operating_hours:
        type: string
        description: "Stated opening and closing times."
    - new_tire_price:
        type: string
        description: "Price quoted for a new bike tire, including currency."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: string
        description: "Price quoted for a standard bike tune-up, including currency."
    - new_seat_price:
        type: string
        description: "Price quoted for a new bike seat, including currency."
    - maintenance_guidance:
        type: string
        description: "General maintenance advice or best practices provided."

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      limit: 12
  interaction_style:
    - default
```

**Why it fits the brief**

- The `user.role`, `context`, and fixed `goals` enforce a polite, English-speaking customer who sticks strictly to shop hours, core service pricing, and basic care advice—no off-topic asks.
- `goal_style: all_answered` makes sure the simulator keeps going until every question is covered, while `interaction_style: default` keeps the tone natural.
- `chatbot.output` captures the exact pieces you’ll want to validate later (hours, three key prices, service summary, and maintenance guidance).

This structure follows the patterns and best practices described in `sensei-chat-guide.md`, ensuring your future rules can reference the declared outputs cleanly. Let me know if you’d like variants (e.g., multi-conversation combos or additional outputs)!
