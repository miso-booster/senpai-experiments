Great question! 😊 In Sensei, a **metamorphic rule** is a special kind of Sensei-check rule that compares **multiple conversations** instead of just one. The idea is to see how the chatbot’s responses should change—or stay the same—when the user’s inputs vary in predictable ways.

Here’s the gist:

- You define these rules in YAML, telling Sensei-check how many conversations to look at (usually 2, but it can be more).
- Each rule can filter which conversations to compare (using a `when:` clause), then spell out the expected relationship between them.
- Instead of hardcoding “the answer must be X,” you state a **relationship** like “if conversation A has more toppings than conversation B, its price should be higher.”

This approach is super helpful when there’s no single “correct” answer, but the **change in input should lead to a consistent change in output**. Think of it as a fairness or consistency check across scenarios rather than a single-pass yes/no test.
