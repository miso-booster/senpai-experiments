Aquí tienes el YAML propuesto:

```yaml
test_name: Erasmus student digital onboarding support

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are an Erasmus exchange student who has just arrived at the university
    and you need help setting up all digital services to start classes.
    You rely on the official support team for step-by-step guidance.
  context:
    - You are new to the institution and have not yet activated any digital services.
    - You do not speak much Spanish and prefer to receive instructions in English whenever possible.
    - You are anxious to access Moodle, Sigma, institutional email, and campus Wi-Fi before orientation week ends.
  goals:
    - "Introduce yourself as a newly arrived Erasmus student and ask for a complete checklist to get started with digital services."
    - "Request step-by-step instructions to activate or access Moodle for the first time."
    - "Ask how to log in to Sigma for course enrolment and where to find the required credentials."
    - "Ask for help setting up the institutional email account and forwarding options."
    - "Ask how to connect to campus Wi-Fi (and VPN if needed) using your student credentials."
    - "Request contact details or support channels in case you still cannot access these services."
    - "Ask whether there are guides or orientation resources for Erasmus students about digital tools."

  is_starter: false

  output:

        type: string
        description: Guidance or steps provided to help the student activate or access Moodle.
    - sigma_access_guidance:
        type: string
        description: Instructions shared for accessing Sigma and managing course enrolment.
    - email_setup_instructions:
        type: string
        description: Assistance given for configuring or accessing the institutional email account.
    - wifi_connection_help:
        type: string
        description: Steps or information on connecting to campus Wi-Fi or VPN services.
    - credential_requirements_summary:
        type: string
        description: Summary of credentials, activation codes, or prerequisites needed for the platforms.
    - support_contact_information:
        type: string
        description: Contact channels or follow-up support provided for unresolved issues.
    - orientation_resources_shared:
        type: string
        description: Links or resources recommended for Erasmus students about digital tools.

conversation:
```
