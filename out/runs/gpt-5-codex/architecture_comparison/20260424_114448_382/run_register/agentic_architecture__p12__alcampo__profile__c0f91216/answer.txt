Hey there! 😊 Here’s a fresh profile that gets the simulator to ask your chatbot for items to add to a shopping cart. I leaned on the guidance from `sensei-chat-guide.md` to structure the goals, variables, conversation controls, and `chatbot.output` schema.

```yaml
test_name: "shopping_cart_item_recommendations"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >-
    You are an organized online shopper building a cart for upcoming needs.
    Gather recommendations, compare options, and make sure the items suit the intended recipient.
  context:
    - "You are browsing a large marketplace with groceries, home goods, and electronics."
    - "You prefer recommendations that balance value and quality."
    - "You will push the assistant to verify stock and delivery details before accepting."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to recommend items to add to the cart for {{recipient}} to cover {{shopping_need}}."
    - "Mention you would like to stay under {{budget}} and prefer {{preference}}."
    - "Confirm the items can arrive {{delivery_timeframe}} and request at least {{quantity_request}} units if needed."
    - "Ask whether there are bundle deals or alternative brands worth considering."
    - shopping_need:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - weekly groceries
          - home office refresh
          - fitness starter kit
          - birthday gift basket
    - recipient:
        function: pairwise()
        type: string
        data:
          - yourself
          - your partner
          - a coworker
          - a close friend who loves cooking
    - budget:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - "$50"
          - "$100"
          - "$200"
    - preference:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - eco-friendly products
          - bundle deals
          - top-rated brands
    - delivery_timeframe:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - within three days
          - by the weekend
          - before next Tuesday
    - quantity_request:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - two
          - three
          - half a dozen

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "Sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you rephrase?"
  output:
    - cart_items:
        type: list[string]
        description: "List of item names recommended for the cart."
    - estimated_total:
        type: currency
        description: "Estimated total price for the recommended items."
    - delivery_window:
        type: string
        description: "Promised delivery timeframe for the items."
    - stock_status:
        type: string
        description: "Stock or availability confirmation for the items."

conversation:
  number: combinations
  goal_style:
    steps: 6
    max_cost: 0.2
  interaction_style:
    - random:
        - long phrases
        - make spelling mistakes
        - change your mind
```

### Why this setup?
- **User goals & variables**: Pairwise coverage on `shopping_need` and `recipient` exercises different cart scenarios, while randomized `budget`, `preference`, delivery timing, and quantity keep the conversations varied.
- **Chatbot outputs**: The schema captures the key facts future rules might inspect—recommended items, price, delivery promise, and stock confirmation—exactly as outlined in the profile guide.
- **Conversation controls**: `combinations` ensures Sensei walks through the pairwise matrix, and the interaction style introduces natural variation in how the simulated shopper nudges the bot.

Feel free to tweak the goal strings or the value lists if you have specific product categories or budgets in mind. Let me know if you’d like companion rules or a matching connector too!
