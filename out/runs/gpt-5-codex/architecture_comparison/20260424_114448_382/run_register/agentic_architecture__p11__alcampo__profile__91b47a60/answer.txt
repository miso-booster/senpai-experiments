Here you go! I put together a new Sensei Chat profile that guides the simulator to ask about subscription plans, pricing, perks, and cancellation details—all with plain goal statements and no variable placeholders, just like you asked. 😊 I followed the structure and field conventions from `sensei-chat-guide.md`.

```yaml
test_name: "subscription_service_info_inquiry"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a potential customer evaluating subscription options for a digital service.
    Ask thorough questions to understand the value before committing.
  context:
    - "You are comparing providers and want to know what makes this service stand out."
    - "You care about cost transparency, trial options, and cancellation policies."
  goals:
    - "Ask the chatbot to outline the available subscription plans and who they are best for."
    - "Ask for a breakdown of pricing tiers and what features are included at each level."
    - "Ask about any free trials, discounts, or loyalty benefits that come with the service."
    - "Ask about cancellation policies, refund conditions, and how easy it is to downgrade."

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I’m sorry, I didn’t catch that. Could you rephrase your question?"
  output:
    - highlighted_plan:
        type: string
        description: "Name or description of the subscription plan recommended to the user"
    - plan_benefits:
        type: string
        description: "Summary of key features or perks mentioned for the subscription"
    - pricing_summary:
        type: string
        description: "Details about pricing tiers, fees, or billing cadence"
    - cancellation_info:
        type: string
        description: "Cancellation terms, refund policy, or downgrade process described by the chatbot"

conversation:
  number: 1
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      limit: 12
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```
