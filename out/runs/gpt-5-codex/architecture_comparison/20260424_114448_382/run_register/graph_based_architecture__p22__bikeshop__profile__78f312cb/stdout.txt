```yaml
test_name: bike_fit_discomfort_advice_seeker

llm:
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  temperature: 0.4
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a cyclist feeling uncomfortable on your current bike setup.
    You’re trying to understand whether the discomfort comes from an improper fit
    or worn components, and you want professional advice without committing to an appointment.
  context:
    - You rely on your bike for regular rides and would like to stay comfortable and safe.
    - You are open to trying small adjustments yourself if the shop suggests them.
    - You want to know what services the shop offers that could diagnose or resolve the issue.
  goals:
    - "Mention that your {{discomfort_area}} has been hurting during rides and ask whether it might be due to bike fit or worn components."
    - "Ask what adjustments or quick checks you can try right now to improve comfort."
    - "Ask what services the shop offers to assess fit, inspect components, or tune the bike without booking anything yet."
    - "Ask how to tell if your {{component_concern}} might need to be repaired or replaced."
    - "Request preventive maintenance tips that could help keep the bike comfortable going forward."
    - discomfort_area:
        type: string
        function: another()
        data:
          - lower back
          - knees
          - wrists
          - neck
    - component_concern:
        type: string
        function: random()
        data:
          - saddle
          - handlebars
          - drivetrain
          - pedals

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry it's a little loud in my shop, can you say that again?"
  output:
    - fit_cause_analysis:
        type: string
        description: >
          The chatbot's explanation about whether the discomfort is likely due to bike fit,
          component wear, or another cause.
    - adjustment_recommendations:
        type: string
        description: >
          Specific adjustments or at-home checks the chatbot suggests to improve comfort.
    - service_options:
        type: string
        description: >
          The services the shop offers for bike fit assessments, component inspections, or related support.
    - component_guidance:
        type: string
        description: >
          Advice on evaluating the mentioned component for wear and determining if replacement is needed.
    - preventive_tips:
        type: string
        description: >
          Preventive maintenance or care tips that can help keep the ride comfortable over time.

conversation:
  number: 3
  max_cost: 0.5
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      export: false
      limit: 18
  interaction_style:
    - single questions
```

### Why this setup works
- **Clear goals:** The user stays focused on discomfort, fit vs. component issues, services, and preventive tips—no accidental appointment requests.
- **Dynamic realism:** `discomfort_area` and `component_concern` rotate, so conversations won’t feel repetitive.
- **Structured outputs:** Five fields capture the exact info you’ll want to validate or write Sensei-check rules against.
