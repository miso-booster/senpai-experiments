Hey there! 😊 In Sensei, a **metamorphic rule** is a Sensei-check rule that evaluates relationships across **multiple conversations** rather than judging a single dialog in isolation. Instead of saying “this one conversation must produce output X,” a metamorphic rule compares two or more conversations that share certain conditions (defined with `when:`) and checks how their outputs should relate.

For example, you might say: if two pizza orders have the same size and drink, but one has more toppings, then the total price in that conversation should be higher. You’d express that with `conv[0]`, `conv[1]`, etc., pointing to the conversations being compared, and use `if:`/`then:` or an `oracle:` condition to capture the expected relation.

These rules are super handy because they let you verify consistency or monotonic behaviors over a batch of generated conversations—perfect for catching tricky issues that wouldn’t show up in single-run tests.
