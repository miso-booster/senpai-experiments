A metamorphic rule in Sensei-check is a validation that looks at **relationships between multiple conversations** rather than judging just one dialogue in isolation. Instead of saying “conversation X must mention Y,” it says something like “if two conversations have the same pizza size and drink but one has more toppings, the price in that conversation should be higher.” In practice you:

- Request at least 2 conversations (`conversations: 2` or `all`).
- Optionally narrow down comparable cases with `when`.
- Express the expected relation using either an `oracle` boolean or an `if`/`then` implication over `conv[0]`, `conv[1]`, etc.

This style helps you capture monotonicity, invariants, or consistency requirements across variations generated by a profile. The details come from the **`sensei-check-doc.md`** guide, which lays out how metamorphic rules compare tuples of conversations and rely on `chatbot.output` fields defined in the associated SENSEI Chat profile.
