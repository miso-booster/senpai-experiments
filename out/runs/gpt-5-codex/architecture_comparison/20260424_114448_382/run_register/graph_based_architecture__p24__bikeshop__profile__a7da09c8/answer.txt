Here’s a ready-to-use Sensei profile that matches your requirements perfectly 😊. Just drop it into your project’s `profiles/` folder and you’re set!

```yaml
test_name: bike_shop_basic_info_check

llm:
  model: gpt-4o-mini
  temperature: 0.4
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are a straightforward customer chatting with a bike shop’s virtual assistant.
    You want clear, accurate answers and stay focused on basic shop information.
  context:
    - "You’re planning a visit soon and want essential details only."
    - "You’re not interested in appointments, specific bike models, custom repairs, warranties, delivery, or other advanced services."
    - "You prefer clear, concise questions and expect the assistant to stick to relevant information."
  goals:
    - Ask for the bike shop's opening and closing times.
    - Ask for the price of a new bike tire.
    - Ask for the price of a standard bike tune-up.
    - Ask for the price of a new bike seat.
    - Ask for general advice on basic bike care and good maintenance practices.

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, it's a little loud in my shop—could you say that again?"
  output:
    - shop_hours:
        type: string
        description: "Opening and closing times provided by the chatbot."
    - tire_price:
        type: money
        description: "Price quoted for a new bike tire."
    - tune_up_price:
        type: money
        description: "Price quoted for a standard bike tune-up."
    - seat_price:
        type: money
        description: "Price quoted for a new bike seat."
    - bike_care_advice:
        type: string
        description: "General bike care advice focusing on basic maintenance and good practices."

conversation:
  number: 2
  goal_style:
    all_answered:
      limit: 10
      export: false
  interaction_style:
    - default
  max_cost: 0.4
```

✨ Highlights:
- The user sticks to friendly, direct questions about hours, key prices, and basic care.
- The context reminds Sensei to avoid drifting into advanced topics.
- The `chatbot.output` fields capture exactly what you want to validate later—hours, three prices, and maintenance advice.
