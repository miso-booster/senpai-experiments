Here’s a profile draft that captures the “discomfort while riding” scenario and keeps the user focused on advice rather than booking:

```yaml
test_name: "bike_fit_discomfort_advice"

llm:
  temperature: 0.6
  model: gpt-4o
  model_prov: openai
  format:
    type: text

user:
  language: English
  role: >
    You are an avid cyclist feeling discomfort during rides and you want guidance on
    whether the issue comes from bike fit or worn components.
  context:
    - "You commute and train several times a week and recently noticed discomfort even on shorter rides."
    - "You usually maintain your bike at home but rely on a trusted local shop for deeper inspections."
    - "You are budget-conscious and prefer to understand options before committing to a service."
  goals:
    - "Explain that you feel discomfort primarily in your {{pain_area}} after rides."
    - "Describe your bike as a {{bike_type}} with components that are {{component_age}} and ask if wear could be causing the issue."
    - "Ask what fit adjustments or assessments the shop recommends to diagnose the discomfort."
    - "Ask what maintenance or component inspection services the shop offers that address your concerns."
    - "Ask about expected turnaround time and approximate cost range for those services."
    - "Clarify that you're seeking advice only right now and not ready to book an appointment."
    - pain_area:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - lower back
          - right knee
          - neck and shoulders
          - wrists and hands
    - bike_type:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - road bike
          - gravel bike
          - commuter bike
    - component_age:
        function: random()
        type: string
        data:
          - about 1 year old
          - about 2 years old
          - recently replaced
          - more than 3 years old

chatbot:
  is_starter: false
  fallback: "I'm sorry, could you rephrase that so I can help you better?"
  output:
    - suggested_fit_adjustments:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Recommended bike fit changes or rider adjustments to reduce discomfort."
    - suggested_service_offers:
        type: list[string]
        description: "Services the shop proposes to evaluate bike fit or component wear."
    - service_time_estimate:
        type: string
        description: "Any timing guidance or turnaround mentioned for the services."
    - estimated_cost_range:
        type: string
        description: "Cost guidance provided for adjustments or services, if any."
    - invitation_to_book:
        type: string
        description: "Whether the chatbot pushed for booking despite the user wanting advice only."

conversation:
  number: 3
  goal_style:
    steps: 6
  interaction_style:
    - long phrases
    - default
```

### Why these choices?
- The goals mirror the rider’s uncertainty—one asks about fit, another about worn components, and there’s a clear note that the user isn’t ready to book.
- Variables (`pain_area`, `bike_type`, `component_age`) keep conversations varied while staying true to the scenario.
- `chatbot.output` tracks the critical advice we expect: adjustments, services, timing, cost, plus whether the bot pushes for an appointment (useful for future rules).

Reference: I followed the structure and best practices from `sensei-chat-guide.md` to make sure the profile aligns with Sensei Chat expectations.
